## BEC

- **descriptive / finding or result**: BEC remains one of the most financially detrimental threats in Africa and has shifted from simple spoofing to sophisticated multi-stage attacks driven by AI and stealer malware, with operations expanding from West Africa into Eastern and Southern Africa. (p. 22)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "BEC has shifted from simple spoofing to sophisticated, multi-stage attacks driven by AI and “stealer” malware."</sub>

## Investment Fraud

- **descriptive / typology or red flag**: Investment fraud in Africa is an industrialised hybrid model often originating from scam centres in Southern and West Africa, using AI-driven advertising and polished apps to target high-value victims mainly in North America and Europe. (p. 22)[^original]

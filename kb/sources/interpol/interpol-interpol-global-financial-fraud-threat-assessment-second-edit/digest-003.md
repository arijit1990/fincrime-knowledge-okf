## Impersonation Fraud: From Remote Calls to Physical Theft

- **descriptive / typology or red flag**: Impersonation fraud, often starting with a phone call, is evolving into a hybrid threat combining virtual deception with physical crime; criminals posing as police, bank staff or officials convince victims they face investigation or penalties and may have them leave cash at their doorstep for accomplices to collect. ## BEC Fraud: Leveraging Technology for Sophisticated Deception

- **descriptive / typology or red flag**: BEC fraudsters in Asia and Pacific use email spoofing, phishing kits and AI-generated personalised messages to impersonate executives or trusted partners, often manipulating employees into authorising fraudulent wire transfers. (p. 28)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Fraudsters use email spoofing, phishing kits, and AI-generated, hyper-personalized messages to impersonate executives"</sub>

- **descriptive / context**: 'Fraud-as-a-Service' platforms powered by generative AI and large language models enable low-skill actors to run professional-grade BEC campaigns, providing tools such as automated phishing websites, fake payment gateways and bot-generated fake testimonials.

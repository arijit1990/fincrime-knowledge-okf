Figure C.2: The AUC-ROC (solid bars) and AUC-PR (hatched bars) results on the LI-Large IBM dataset.

We include a sensitivity analysis where only transactions involving the bank’s own clients are visible. This obfuscates the smurfing pattern if money launderers transfer their funds through multiple banks (see Section 6).

The IBM datasets include the sender’s and receiver’s bank information. The experiments presented in this appendix are based on the HI-Small dataset, in which the 515,080 accounts and 5,078,345 transactions are spread over 30,528 banks. The main limitation of this dataset is that there are 6,193 banks with a single client, and the median number of clients per bank is 4, which is not a realistic distribution of clients.

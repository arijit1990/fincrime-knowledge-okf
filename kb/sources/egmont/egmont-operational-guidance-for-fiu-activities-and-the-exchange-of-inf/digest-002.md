## Annex A: Sample query form

- **descriptive / requirement**: In section I (Requestor representations) of the sample request form, the requesting agency agrees, on receipt of information from the disclosing FIU, to maintain the confidentiality of all information provided, not to disclose it outside its agency without the prior written permission of the disclosing FIU, and to limit its use to the purposes stated on the form. (p. 28)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "not disclose the information outside of its agency without the prior written permission of the disclosing FIU; and"</sub>
- **descriptive / data or feature**: Section II of the request form (all fields mandatory) captures: requesting agency, country/territory, case number, date submitted, routine or urgent (if urgent, desired response date and reason for urgency), contact name and title, telephone and fax, email, signature, authorizing official (position, telephone and fax, date, signature) and the agency receiving the request. (p. 28)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "REQUESTOR INFORMATION (all fields are mandatory)"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Annex A is the Egmont Group sample form titled 'Request for FIU information'. (p. 28)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The Egmont Group of Financial Intelligence Units _Request for FIU information_"</sub>
- **descriptive / data or feature**: Section III of the request form (subject information requested) has fields for an individual: name (surname, given, middle), aliases, sex, date of birth, two addresses, phone numbers, passport number and issuing jurisdiction, other ID number and issuing jurisdiction, professional activity, relationship to the investigation, place of birth, nationality, bank account numbers, bank name and bank address. (p. 29)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "SUBJECT INFORMATION REQUESTED (attach additional sheets as necessary)"</sub>
- **descriptive / data or feature**: Section III also has business fields for two related or associated businesses (1) and (2): business address, relationship to the investigation, bank account numbers, bank name and bank address. (p. 29)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "SUBJECT INFORMATION REQUESTED (attach additional sheets as necessary)"</sub>
- **descriptive / data or feature**: Section IV of the request form (nature of the investigation) asks the requester to describe the case under investigation and state the principal violation(s) (criminal or civil), describe the link with the country of the disclosing FIU, and say what information is needed from the disclosing FIU. (p. 29)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Describe the case under investigation and state the principal violation(s): Criminal Civil"</sub>
- **descriptive / data or feature**: Section IV further asks: how and for what purposes the information will be used; whether there are ongoing formal investigations or judicial proceedings; whether asset forfeiture or securement is anticipated; the amount and type or nature of assets involved; and what other agencies or countries are involved. The form also records the number of additional sheets attached. (p. 30)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Do you anticipate asset forfeiture or securement in this case?"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The sample Spontaneous Disclosure cover letter, which starts at the end of this excerpt, carries an instruction to attach it to the front of the spontaneous disclosure. (p. 30)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Please attach this cover letter to the front of the spontaneous disclosure"</sub>

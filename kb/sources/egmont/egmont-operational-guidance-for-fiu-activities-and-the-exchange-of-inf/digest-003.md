## Annex C: Promoting the role of FIUs in national counter - terrorism apparatus

- **descriptive / finding or result**: The FIUs involved in the ISIL Project found that information exchange with intelligence services was crucial to being effective in performing FIU functions in combating terrorism. (p. 32)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "found that information exchange with intelligence services was crucial to be effective"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The ISIL Project approach on information exchange with intelligence services is described as consistent with the Interpretative Note to FATF Recommendation 29 when it addresses 'access to information'. (p. 32)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "This approach is consistent with the Financial Action Task Force (FATF) Recommendation 29 Interpretative Note"</sub>
- **descriptive / expectation**: One element of FIUs being part of a national counter-terrorism apparatus is a national plan or strategy to combat terrorism and/or terrorism financing that includes the FIU. (p. 32)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "A national plan or strategy to combat terrorism and/or terrorism financing that includes the FIU."</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: FIUs should help drive their national counter-terrorism financing strategy by having a central role in the national risk assessment. (p. 32)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "FIUs should help drive their national counter-terrorism financing strategy by having a central role in the national risk assessment"</sub>
- **descriptive / expectation**: A legislative mandate for FIUs provides the basis to counter the financing of terrorism, including the collection of reporting, analysis and dissemination of intelligence. (p. 32)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "A legislative mandate for FIUs also provides the basis to counter the financing of terrorism"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: In the context of countering the financing of terrorism, FIU relationships with, and access to information from, intelligence services are complemented by the same engagement with law enforcement and other relevant partners. (p. 32)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "FIU relationships with, and access to information from, intelligence services is complemented by the same engagement"</sub>
- **descriptive / expectation**: Another element is a multi-agency body that sets operational priorities for counter-terrorism efforts, where the FIU seconds an officer and/or is a permanent member of a committee structure. (p. 32)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "A multi-agency body which sets operational priorities for CT efforts, where the FIU seconds an officer"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: For FIUs to be an effective part of the national counter-terrorism (CT) effort, domestic relationships are enhanced by the FIU being part of a national CT apparatus, which includes the national strategy, legislative mandate and multi-agency body elements described. (p. 32)[^original]

- **descriptive / context**: FIUs can take a number of actions to ensure they have the right information to trigger actionable intelligence on terrorism financing; Diagram 2 outlines relevant aspects, which (as legible) include context of reporting having relevant national TF risks, a well-timed flow of information to FIUs, and an increased amount of information available to FIUs. ## OVERCOMING BARRIERS TO SHARING FOR INTELLIGENCE PURPOSES

- **descriptive / finding or result**: The FIUs in the ISIL Project found that several participating FIUs were unable to share information, bilaterally or multilaterally, when there were ongoing investigations or a mutual legal assistance request in progress. (p. 36)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "found that several participating FIUs were unable to share information - either bilaterally or multilaterally"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: Such national laws placed undue restrictions on FIUs' ability to share information for intelligence purposes, restricting exchange when it could be most useful: when there is an ongoing law enforcement or prosecutorial focus on a particular subject. (p. 36)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "These limitations restrict information exchange when it could be most useful"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: The guidance says the FATF Standards are clear on international cooperation and unduly restrictive measures, citing paragraph 2 of the Interpretive Note to Recommendation 40, and that FIUs should continue to work with national partners to ensure domestic and multilateral measures do not impede information sharing for intelligence purposes. (p. 36)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "FIUs should continue to work with national partners to ensure domestic and multilateral measures do not impede information sharing"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Ground (d) in the quoted Interpretive Note concerns the nature or status of the requesting counterpart authority being different from that of its foreign counterpart. (p. 36)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the nature or status (civil, administrative, law enforcement, etc.) of the requesting counterpart authority"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: The quoted Interpretive Note to FATF Recommendation 40, paragraph 2, says countries should not prohibit international exchange of information or assistance, nor make it subject to unreasonable or unduly restrictive conditions. (p. 36)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Countries should not prohibit or place unreasonable or unduly restrictive conditions on the provision of exchange of information or assistance"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Under the quoted Interpretive Note to Recommendation 40, paragraph 2, competent authorities should not refuse a request for assistance on the ground (a) that the request is also considered to involve fiscal matters, and/or (b) that laws oblige financial institutions or DNFBPs to maintain secrecy or confidentiality, except where the sought information is held in circumstances where legal privilege or legal professional secrecy applies. (p. 36)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the request is also considered to involve fiscal matters; and/or"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Under the same quoted note, competent authorities should not refuse a request on the ground (c) that an inquiry, investigation or proceeding is under way in the requested country, unless providing the assistance would impede it, and/or (d) that the requesting counterpart authority has a different nature or status (civil, administrative, law enforcement, etc.) from its foreign counterpart. (p. 36)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "there is an inquiry, investigation or proceeding underway in the requested country,"</sub>

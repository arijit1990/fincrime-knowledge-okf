## Mandate

- **descriptive / definition**: The Regional Groups (RGs) support member FIUs in their regions and represent their members in the Egmont Committee (EC) on compliance matters and other important issues. (p. 8)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The RGs support member FIUs in their respective regions"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: The Egmont Group Secretariat (EGS), led by the Executive Secretary, provides strategic, administrative and technical support to the HoFIU, EC, WGs and RGs, and assists with content management in open areas of the ESW. (p. 8)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The EGS provides strategic, administrative, and technical support to the HoFIU, EC, WGs, and RGs"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: The Egmont Committee is an advisory body made up of the Chair, Vice-Chairs, Working Group Chairs, Regional Representatives, the ESW Representative and the Executive Secretary; it consults and coordinates for the HoFIU, Regional Groups and Working Groups, is the decision-maker in areas designated by the HoFIU, and ensures the work is horizontally and vertically cohesive.   <sub>anchor: "Comprised of the Egmont Group Chair, Vice-Chairs, Working Group Chairs, Regional Representatives, ESW Representative, and Executive Secretary"</sub>

  <sub>anchor: "Created by the HoFIU upon the EC"</sub>

- **descriptive / definition**: The Egmont Group Secure Web (ESW) is an electronic collaboration system owned by all members and critical to the Group's effective functioning; the HoFIU provides its mandate and strategy and approves and monitors its major policies. (p. 8)[^original]

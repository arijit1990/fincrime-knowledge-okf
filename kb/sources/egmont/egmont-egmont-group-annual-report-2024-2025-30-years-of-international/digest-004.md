## Membership Support and Compliance Working Group (MSCWG)

  <sub>anchor: "the MSCWG must maintain these high standards among Egmont Group members"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: During the reporting period the MSCWG was led by Chair Fuad Aliyev (FIU Azerbaijan), supported by Vice-Chairs Tarek Zahran (FIU Lebanon) and Vince O'Brien (FIU UK). (p. 38)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the MSCWG was led by Chair FUAD ALIYEV (FIU Azerbaijan)"</sub>

## Policy and Procedures Working Group (PPWG)

- **descriptive / context**: The PPWG was led by Chair Eva ten Brink (FIU the Netherlands), supported by Vice-Chairs Yaqoob Almuftah (FIU Bahrain), Mariana Currais (FIU Argentina) and Marine Siva (FIU France). (p. 40)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the PPWG was led by Chair EVA TEN BRINK (FIU the Netherlands)"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: Completed project on sustainability of the Egmont Group against geopolitical situations: after analysing recent events and governance, it was recommended to keep the current flexible approach using existing core documents and emergency powers rather than creating new regulations, so Egmont stays focused on its technical mission and keeps its apolitical stance. (p. 40)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "it was recommended to keep the current flexible approach"</sub>
- **descriptive / method**: Completed 'Indie' Project on FIU operational independence and autonomy, described as a cornerstone for effective AML/CFT systems: it seeks to make independence a measurable KPI by analysing governance frameworks, funding structures and HR processes, builds on Chapter 6 of the 2018 ECOFEL paper, and proposed making its characteristics mandatory with a phased implementation plan. (p. 40)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "It seeks to make independence a measurable KPI by analysing governance frameworks, funding structures, and HR processes"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: The PPWG's role is to identify important operational policy and strategic issues that affect the Egmont Group and to coordinate a response both across the organization and with partner organizations. (p. 40)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "responsible for identifying important operational policy and strategic issues affecting the Egmont Group"</sub>
- **descriptive / method**: PPWG project listed as in progress, Revision of the Support and Compliance Process (SCP): the work updates how the Egmont Group monitors and supports member compliance with its binding documents and standards. The SCP is described as making sure FIUs follow Egmont principles and giving structured help where gaps are found. The revision aims to simplify procedures, make clear what triggers a compliance review, and improve transparency and accountability when cases are referred to the MSCWG. (p. 40)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The SCP ensures that FIUs adhere to Egmont principles and provides structured assistance when gaps are identified"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: PPWG project "FIU Funding Models": it examines the range of funding models FIUs use around the world, aiming to identify best practices, challenges to financial sustainability and innovative self-funding approaches. It offers a comparative analysis of government-funded, hybrid and self-funded models, pointing to advantages such as guaranteed budgets and risks such as conflicts of interest, and is informed by extensive surveys and consultations with PPWG members. The PPWG's looking-ahead list includes finalizing the FIU Funding Model. (p. 40)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "comparative analysis of government-funded, hybrid, and self-funded models"</sub>
- **descriptive / method**: Project 4, revision of the Egmont Group Principles of Information Exchange between FIUs (joint PPWG and IEWG): updates the 2013 Principles to reflect FATF recommendation changes, technological advances and operational needs; key areas are data protection, confidentiality, secure platforms and timeliness of exchanges; aims to enhance cooperation, address barriers and ensure alignment with global standards. (p. 41)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Key areas include data protection, confidentiality, secure platforms, and timeliness of exchanges."</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Planned PPWG initiatives: finalize the revision of the Support and Compliance Process; finalize the revision of the Principles of Information Exchange; finalize the FIU Funding Model; and support the EC Governance Reference Group in a strategy to make Chapter 6 of the 2018 ECOFEL paper on FIU Operational Independence and Autonomy mandatory, to be presented at the HoFIU meeting in Luxembourg. (p. 41)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "making Chapter 6 of the 2018 ECOFEL paper on FIU Operational Independence and Autonomy mandatory"</sub>

(p. 42)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "provide a forum for Egmont Group members, candidate FIUs, Observers, and international partner organizations"</sub>

## Egmont Centre of FIU Excellence and Leadership (ECOFEL)

- **descriptive / context**: Since its establishment in 2018, ECOFEL has supported over 190 jurisdictions with technical assistance and developed the first unique e-learning platform for FIUs; it is developing a first-of-its-kind Certification Program for FIUs and their personnel, and its vision is to position and promote FIUs within national systems and internationally. (p. 45)[^original]

## Eurasia Regional Group

  <sub>anchor: "The Eurasia Regional Group comprises 7 FIUS from members jurisdictions"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: EAG training workshops on the updated FATF standards and assessor preparation were held August 5-9, 2024 in Tashkent, September 23-27, 2024 in Dushanbe and March 3-7, 2025 in Shenzhen; regional members also periodically trained at the FATF Training Institute in Busan, Republic of Korea. (p. 55)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "EAG held training workshops focused on the updated FATF standards and the preparation of assessors"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: ECOFEL workshops: September 2-4, 2024 in Istanbul, training for Americas, Europe and Eurasia representatives on transforming intelligence into evidence in the fight against ML/TF; and November 18-20, 2024 in Astana, a three-day strategic analysis seminar for two Eurasia members on identifying new trends, patterns and developments in ML and TF. (p. 55)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Transforming Intelligence into Evidence in the Fight Against ML/TF"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: The first Forum of FIUs of the Member States of the Organization of Turkic States (OTS) was held October 8-9, 2024 in Shusha, Azerbaijan, to exchange knowledge on financial monitoring and regulation, and the Shusha Initiative was adopted; the first meeting of OTS heads of FIUs and the 2nd Forum followed on June 26, 2025 in Cholpon-Ata, Kyrgyzstan. (p. 55)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "As a result of the Forum, the Shusha Initiative was adopted."</sub>

(p. 56)[^original]

- **descriptive / finding or result**: On December 10-11, 2024 the Council of Heads of FIUs of CIS Member States held a workshop in Dushanbe, with the EAG and CIS bodies, on terrorist group activities, methods to finance them and modern payment instruments; attendees included CIS member state authorities, CIS bodies and the CSTO Secretariat. ## Europe I Regional Group

- **descriptive / context**: The Europe I Regional Group comprises 30 FIUs from jurisdictions in FATF and MONEYVAL, all EU or EEA states; Regional Representatives were Toms Platacis (FIU Latvia) and Alfred Zammit (FIU Malta), with Efstathios Tsirmpas (FIU Greece) selected after Platacis's term ended in July 2025. (p. 57)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "All Europe I Region FIUs are either member states of the EU or the EEA counties."</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: The Europe I Intersessional Meeting in The Hague (September 30-October 1, 2024) focused on strategic alignment and operational collaboration, including positioning FIUs within the AMLA framework, enhancing joint analysis capabilities and supporting a proposal for a dedicated strategic analyst meeting. (p. 57)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "positioning FIUs within the AMLA framework, enhancing joint analysis capabilities"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: A virtual Europe I Strategic Analysts Meeting on 31 October 2024, organized by FIU the Netherlands, shared national approaches to prioritizing STRs/SARs, discussing tools, challenges and workflows to improve strategic analysis and cross-FIU collaboration. (p. 57)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "to share national approaches to prioritizing STRs/SARs"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: A Europe I meeting in Malta on January 23-24, 2025, hosted by FIU Malta, discussed AMLA integration, FIU delegate roles and joint analysis practices, with updates from EU FIU Platform Working Groups and feedback from earlier analyst sessions. An ECOFEL online workshop on July 21-22, 2025, supported by the U.S. INL Bureau, focused on information sharing between FIUs, law enforcement and supervisory agencies. (p. 57)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Discussions focused on AMLA integration, FIU delegate roles, and joint analysis practices"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: Europe I countries have engaged with AMLA development, aligning strategic priorities and operational discussions with the evolving EU AML/CFT framework and supporting AMLA's start-up phase and joint analysis and coordination. Daniel Thelesklaf of FIU Germany was elected Egmont Vice-Chair at the July 2025 Plenary in Luxembourg. (p. 57)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The region has focused on supporting AMLA"</sub>

## Europe II Regional Group

- **descriptive / context**: The Europe II Regional Group comprises 23 FIUs from MONEYVAL and FATF member jurisdictions; Regional Representatives were Oxana Gisca (FIU Moldova) and Michaela Schutz (FIU Switzerland), with Ghizlane Dadyou (FIU Monaco) selected after Gisca's term ended in July 2025. (p. 57)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "comprises 23 FIUs from member jurisdictions within the Council of Europe Committee of Experts"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: Europe II held an Intersessional in Strasbourg on the margins of the Moneyval Plenary. Its regional plan covers topics including FIU.net accessibility for all of the region, online gambling and links to organized groups, Immediate Outcomes 2 and 6, the metaverse, GIZ, United for Wildlife, Eversheds Sutherland and the Council of Europe; the extracted list is fragmentary. (p. 58)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Online gambling and its likes to organized groups"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Europe II also lists coordination on the Support and Compliance Process, partnerships (Europe I, FATF, Moneyval and non-traditional partners), and future items such as the new round of evaluations (Moneyval and FATF), PPPs (FIUs from Europe I and Europe II), typologies and effective use of FinInt. (p. 58)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "New round of evaluations (Moneyval and FATF)"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Europe II was organizing an intersessional meeting in Kosovo in November 2025, at which the region was expected to endorse a paper on a horizontal analysis of Immediate Outcomes 2 and 6 and of Recommendations 29 and 40. (p. 58)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "endorse a paper on a horizontal analysis of Immediate Outcome 2 and 6, as well as Recommendations 29 and 40"</sub>

## East and Southern Africa (ESA) Regional Group

- **descriptive / context**: The ESA Regional Group comprises 15 FIUs from jurisdictions in the Eastern and Southern Africa Anti-Money Laundering Group (ESAAMLG); Bryan Eiseb (FIU Namibia) served as Regional Representative. (p. 59)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "comprises 15 FIUs from member jurisdictions of the Eastern and Southern Africa Anti-Money Laundering Group"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: In a January 2025 ESA virtual meeting the Secretariat presented the Egmont membership procedure: sponsors must be Egmont Group Members, active participants in the MSCWG, possess sufficient resources, and ensure all parties agree to the voluntary sponsorship relationship. (p. 59)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Egmont Sponsors must be Egmont Group Members, active participants in the MSCWG, possess sufficient resources"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: At the FATF Plenary in July 2025, a session on AI and Virtual Assets discussed digitalization and AI within FATF, identifying emerging risks and typologies, AI and deepfake technology, challenges in implementing FATF standards on virtual assets, and a comparison of risks between traditional financial institutions and VASPs. (p. 59)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the uses and implications of AI and deepfake technology"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: A United for Wildlife presentation (July 2025 Plenary) covered combating illegal wildlife trade and related financial crimes, including the Financial and Transport Taskforces, the importance of public-private partnerships, the need for better legislation and coordination, and the benefits of following financial trails. (p. 59)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "establishment of the Financial and Transport Taskforces"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: A UNODC update (July 2025 Plenary) on international money laundering networks and schemes, focused on the ESA region, covered new risks and trends, methodologies for FIUs using machine learning and advanced data analytics, a catalogue of technical assistance products, and mechanisms and operational modes of money laundering. (p. 59)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "methodologies for Financial Intelligence Units (FIUs) using machine learning and advanced data analytics"</sub>
- **permissive / requirement**: As presented by the EGS Secretariat at the ESA virtual working group meeting in January 2025, any FIU that meets the Egmont Group's definition of an FIU may apply for membership by sending an expression of interest to the Executive Secretary of the Egmont Group Secretariat (EGS), using the Membership Application Package (MAP) published on the Egmont Group's public website. (p. 59)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "may apply for membership by submitting an expression of interest to the Executive Secretary"</sub>
- **descriptive / typology or red flag**: The UNODC presentation highlighted roles of terrorist and transnational criminal networks, financial institutions and professional gatekeepers, and the use of international banking networks, money mules, laundromat-type schemes and virtual assets in facilitating money laundering. (p. 60)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "money mules, laundromat-type schemes, and virtual assets in facilitating money laundering"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The Regional Representative attended the WCO Hot Zone Prioritization Forum, November 4-8, 2024 in Sofia, Bulgaria, and presented on trends and typologies of pre-eminent predicate offences and money laundering. (p. 60)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "made a presentation on Trends and Typologies of pre-eminent predicate offences and money laundering"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: ESA regional developments: FIU Mozambique was formally admitted to the Egmont Group on July 10, 2025; the group continued engaging non-member African FIUs to promote membership and capacity building and contributed to global AML/CFT efforts; ECOFEL initiatives listed are the Advanced Strategic Analysis Course (A-SAC), FIU Suspension Powers Workshop, Virtual Assets Analysis Course and Secondment program. (p. 60)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "FIU Mozambique was formally admitted to the Egmont Group on July 10, 2025"</sub>

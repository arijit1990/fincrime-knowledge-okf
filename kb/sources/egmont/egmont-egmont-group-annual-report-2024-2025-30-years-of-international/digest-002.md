## Looking Back Through the Years

- **descriptive / context**: The Egmont Group was established in 1995 at the Egmont Palace in Brussels as an informal network of 13 national FIUs to facilitate cooperation and intelligence sharing against money laundering and terrorist financing, and now has over 182 member FIUs. (p. 23)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Initially, it was an informal network of 13 national FIUs"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The Egmont Secure Web (ESW) was launched in 1997 for secure information exchange among FIUs; working groups were created from the late 1990s to mid-2010s. The Egmont Committee was established in 2002, the Egmont Group Charter was published in 2007, and the Secretariat (EGS) was established in 2007 in Canada. (p. 23)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The Egmont Group Secretariat (EGS) was established in 2007 in Canada"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The first annual report was published in 2008 and the first strategic plan (2009-2012) launched in 2009; the Best Egmont Case Award competition began in 2010-2011 to inspire member FIUs to contribute to the Group's database on ML/TF cases and typologies. (p. 23)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "which aimed to inspire member FIUs to contribute to the group"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The ECOFEL program provides training and promotes best practices to improve the expertise of FIU personnel. The Group has recently kept updating its charter, principles for information exchange, operational guidance and support and compliance processes, and collaborates regularly with FATF, INTERPOL, WCO, IMF, the World Bank and UNODC. (p. 23)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The group also collaborates regularly with international organizations such as FATF, INTERPOL, WCO, IMF"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: IMF General Counsel Yan Liu is quoted describing FIUs as a trusted bridge between reporting institutions and law enforcement that protect the financial integrity of jurisdictions, and expressing the IMF's wish to continue strengthening these agencies. (p. 23)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "You are a trusted bridge between reporting institutions and law enforcement"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Milestones for 2016: new Working Groups (IEWG, MSCWG, PPWG and TATWG) were launched; the Operational Guidance and the Support & Compliance process were updated; the first Communications Strategy was adopted; and the ISIL Financing project began. A separate 2016 entry records the first multilateral information-sharing project (ISIL Phase 2). (p. 23)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Operational Guidance and Support & Compliance updated; First Communications Strategy; ISIL Financing project begins"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Milestones for 2018: ECOFEL started operations, the third Strategic Plan (covering 2018-2021) was adopted, the Charter was updated, and an ECOFEL paper on FIU operational independence and autonomy was published. (p. 23)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "ECOFEL begins operations; Third Strategic Plan (2018–2021); Charter updated"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Milestones 1995-2009: 1995 Egmont Group founded in Brussels and first Plenary held; 1997 Egmont Secure Web launched; late 1990s-2010s Working Groups established; 2002 Egmont Committee created; 2007 Charter published and Secretariat established; 2008 first Annual Report; 2009 first Strategic Plan (2009-2012). (p. 24)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Egmont Group founded in Brussels; first Plenary held"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Milestones 2010-2017: 2010 first BECA competition; 2013 Charter updated and Principles for Information Exchange and Operational Guidance published; 2014 second Strategic Plan (2014-2017), expansion to eight regions and Support & Compliance Process introduced; 2015 20th anniversary and Working Groups restructured; 2016 new Working Groups launched (IEWG, MSCWG, PPWG, TATWG), first Communications Strategy, ISIL Financing project begins and first multilateral info-sharing project (ISIL Phase 2); 2017 Operational Guidance updated. (p. 24)[^original]

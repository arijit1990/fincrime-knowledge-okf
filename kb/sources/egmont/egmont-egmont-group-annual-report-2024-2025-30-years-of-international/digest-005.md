## Regional Group Developments

- **descriptive / definition**: Regional Groups help achieve the Egmont Group's objectives at regional level; each has a dedicated Regional Representative on the Egmont Committee, and each member FIU is a Regional Group member. (p. 51)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Each member FIU is a Regional Group member."</sub>

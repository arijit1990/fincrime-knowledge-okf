## Concealment of Beneficial Ownership

- **descriptive / context**: The July 2018 joint FATF-Egmont Group study Concealment of Beneficial Ownership examines mechanisms and techniques for obscuring ownership and control of illicit assets, drawing on over 100 case studies, law enforcement experience, FATF Mutual Evaluation Report outcomes and academic reports. Legal persons, legal arrangements and professional intermediaries support business growth but can be misused to conceal proceeds of crime. (p. 189)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "drawing on over 100 case studies, the experiences of law enforcement experts"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The report aims to raise awareness of the risks among national authorities, financial institutions and other professional service providers. (p. 189)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "raise awareness with national authorities, financial institutions and other professional service providers"</sub>

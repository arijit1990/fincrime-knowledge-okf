## C. International Cooperation

- **expected / expectation**: Principle 11: FIUs should exchange information with foreign FIUs regardless of their status, whether administrative, law enforcement, judicial or other. (p. 5)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "FIUs should exchange information with foreign FIUs, regardless of their status; be it administrative, law enforcement, judicial or other."</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Principle 12: FIUs should have an adequate legal basis for providing cooperation on money laundering, associated predicate offences and the financing of terrorism. (p. 5)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "FIUs should have an adequate legal basis for providing cooperation on money laundering, associated predicate offences"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Principle 13: FIUs should exchange information freely, spontaneously and upon request, on the basis of reciprocity and mutual assistance. (p. 5)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "FIUs should exchange information freely, spontaneously and upon request on the basis of reciprocity and mutual assistance."</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Principle 13 also says FIUs should ensure they can rapidly, constructively and effectively provide the widest range of international cooperation against money laundering, associated predicate offences and terrorist financing, both spontaneously and upon request, and that there should be a lawful basis for providing cooperation. (p. 5)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "FIUs should ensure that they can rapidly, constructively, and effectively provide the widest range of international cooperation"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Principle 15: to conduct a proper analysis, FIUs should have access to the widest possible range of financial, administrative and law enforcement information, including open or public sources, relevant information collected or maintained by or on behalf of other authorities and, where appropriate, commercially held data. (p. 5)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "FIUs should have access to the widest possible range of financial, administrative and law enforcement information."</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Principle 16: FIUs should be able to disseminate, spontaneously and upon request, information and the results of their analysis to relevant competent authorities. (p. 5)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "FIUs should be able to disseminate, spontaneously and upon request, information and the results of their analysis"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Principle 17: FIUs should use the most efficient means to cooperate; if bilateral or multilateral agreements or arrangements such as an MOU are needed, they should be negotiated and signed in a timely way with the widest range of foreign FIUs. (p. 5)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "FIUs should use the most efficient means to cooperate."</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Principle 18: FIUs should be able to conduct domestic queries on behalf of foreign FIUs as needed and exchange with them all the information they could obtain if such queries were carried out domestically; such queries are subject to any limitations or restrictions imposed by either FIU. (p. 5)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "FIUs should be able to conduct domestic queries on behalf of foreign FIUs as needed"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Principle 19: FIUs should have mechanisms in place to allow exchange of information necessary to assist provisional measures and, where possible, confiscations pursuant to FATF Recommendation 41. (p. 5)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "FIUs should have mechanisms in place to allow for the exchange of information necessary to assist the provisional measures"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Principle 19 also says FIUs should ensure they can expeditiously exchange information necessary to suspend or withhold consent to a transaction; this may be done directly with counterparts or indirectly if legally permissible. (p. 5)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "FIUs should ensure that they can expeditiously exchange information necessary to suspend or withhold consent to a transaction."</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Principle 19: in the context of requests from non-counterpart authorities, FIUs should notify the counterpart FIU of the indirect information exchange. (p. 5)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "FIUs should notify the counterpart FIU of the indirect information exchange."</sub>
- **recommended / recommendation**: Footnote 1 says that before asking a counterpart FIU to assist in measures to suspend a transaction or provisionally freeze assets, member FIUs are encouraged to research the counterpart's capacity to carry out these measures; the Egmont Biennial Census may help. (p. 5)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "member FIUs are encouraged to research the capacity of the counterpart to carry out these measures"</sub>

## Obligations for the FIU Making the Request or Submitting Spontaneous Disclosures

- **expected / expectation**: Principle 20: when requesting cooperation, FIUs should make their best efforts to provide complete, factual and, as appropriate, legal information, including a description of the case being analyzed and the potential link with the country receiving the request, and should indicate any need for urgency. (p. 6)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "FIUs should make their best efforts to provide complete, factual and as appropriate, legal information"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Principle 20: FIU requests should be well-defined and specific, with clear and direct relevance to the matter at hand. (p. 6)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "FIUs requests should be well-defined and specific, with clear and direct relevance to the matter at hand."</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Principle 20: the scope of a request should be limited to what is necessary and proportionate, avoiding overly broad queries that may place an undue burden on the responding FIU. (p. 6)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The scope of the request should be limited to what is necessary and proportionate"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Principle 20: when requesting information for a particularly long period, FIUs should provide a clear justification for why that timeframe is relevant. (p. 6)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "FIUs should provide a clear justification for why that timeframe is relevant."</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Principle 21: an FIU making an urgent request should mark its urgency clearly and justify it sufficiently. (p. 6)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "An FIU making an urgent request should mark its urgency clearly and justify it"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Principle 21: if the FIUs participating in the exchange do not agree on the urgency of the request, they should communicate to find an acceptable resolution. (p. 6)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "If the FIUs participating in the exchange are not in agreement as to the urgency of the request"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Footnote 2 says urgent requests should be limited to instances where a timely response is critical. (p. 6)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Urgent requests should be limited to instances where timely response is critical"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: Footnote 2 says urgent requests must include appropriate prior consent for dissemination. (p. 6)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "timely response is critical and must include appropriate prior consent for dissemination"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Footnote 2 says that, to avoid duplication of efforts, urgent requests should be avoided where the necessary information has already been requested through other channels by other competent authorities, such as law enforcement authorities (LEAs). (p. 6)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "urgent requests should be avoided in cases where the necessary information has already been requested through other channels"</sub>
- **permissive / expectation**: Principle 22: FIUs may decide to exchange information indirectly with non-counterparts in response to requests from competent authorities. (p. 6)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "FIUs may decide to exchange information indirectly with non-counterparts in response to requests from competent authorities."</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Principle 22: FIUs should ensure that a competent authority requesting information indirectly always makes clear whom it is requesting the information for and for what purpose. (p. 6)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the competent authority requesting the information indirectly always makes it clear whom they are requesting the information for"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Principle 23: upon request and whenever possible, FIUs should provide feedback to their foreign counterparts on the use of the information provided and on the outcome of the analysis conducted based on it. (p. 6)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "FIUs should provide feedback to their foreign counterparts on the use of the information provided"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Principle 25: requests sent simultaneously to several FIUs should be justified with a clear explanation of the link involving the specific set of countries. (p. 6)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Requests for information that are sent simultaneously to several FIUs should be justified with a clear explanation"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Principle 25: requests for information should not be sent to large distribution lists if the link is not justifiable to all recipients. (p. 6)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "should not be sent to large distribution lists if the link is not justifiable to all recipients"</sub>
- **recommended / recommendation**: Principle 26: FIUs are encouraged to share spontaneous disclosures of information with counterpart FIUs; these do not require a response from the receiving FIU. (p. 6)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "FIUs are encouraged to share spontaneous disclosures of information with counterpart FIUs."</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Principle 26: when a spontaneous disclosure results in a successful ML/TF investigation or prosecution, the receiving FIU should strive to provide relevant feedback about the usefulness of the information received. (p. 6)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the receiving FIU should strive to provide relevant feedback about the usefulness of the received information"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Principle 24 says an FIU that makes a request should tell the FIU that will process it why the request is made and, to the extent possible, what the information will be used for, and should supply enough information to let the receiving FIU respond lawfully. (p. 6)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "An FIU requesting information should disclose, to the FIU that will process the request, the reason for the request"</sub>

## Obligations for the FIU Receiving the Request

- **expected / expectation**: Principle 27: FIUs should have the power to exchange (b) any other information they have the power to obtain or access, directly or indirectly, at the domestic level, subject to the principle of reciprocity. (p. 7)[^original]

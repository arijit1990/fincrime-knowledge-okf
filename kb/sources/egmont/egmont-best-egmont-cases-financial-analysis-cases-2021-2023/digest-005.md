## Drug Smuggling & Gambling

- **descriptive / context**: Drug trafficking is described as a highly lucrative illegal business practiced by criminal organizations including drug cartels, generating billions of dollars in revenue annually and being one of the most profitable criminal activities. (p. 41)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "It generates billions of dollars in revenue annually"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: UNODC observed a significant increase in drug use worldwide: in 2018 about 269 million drug users were 5.3 percent of the global population aged 15 to 64, rising by 2020 to an estimated 284 million, or 5.9 percent. (p. 41)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "approximately 269 million drug users accounted for 5.3 percent of the global population"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: According to the FATF, drug trafficking yields substantial proceeds that must be handled discreetly; criminals control and conceal funds by disguising their origins, altering their form, or moving them to locations where they are less likely to attract attention. (p. 42)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "drug trafficking yields substantial proceeds that must be handled discreetly"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Payment for drugs often involves large amounts of cash that must be transported and eventually integrated into the legitimate financial system, allowing criminals to fund personal expenses or other illicit activities while creating an appearance of legality. (p. 42)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Payment for drugs often involves large amounts of cash"</sub>
- **descriptive / typology or red flag**: Gambling, particularly at casinos, is another money laundering avenue: although casinos are not classified as financial institutions, the large number and frequency of transactions make the sector highly susceptible. Casinos deal largely in cash and carry out financial activities similar to traditional financial institutions, making them attractive to launderers; understanding gambling-sector laundering methods is described as crucial for policymakers and experts. (p. 42)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Although casinos are not classified as financial institutions"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Criminal groups continuously adapt their distribution and payment methods; a notable trend is the growing industry of selling synthetic drugs online, and the drug market has become more diversified. (p. 42)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "One notable trend is the growing industry of selling synthetic drugs online."</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: The UNODC World Drug Report 2022 highlights increasing complexity of drug markets, including numerous synthetic drugs that are not internationally regulated, and a rapid increase in the non-medical use of pharmaceutical drugs. (p. 42)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The UNODC’s World Drug Report 2022 highlights the increasing complexity of drug markets"</sub>
- **descriptive / typology or red flag**: Indicators of tobacco smuggling include the level of expertise displayed by individuals or groups involved and their ability to mitigate the risk of detection at various stages of smuggling, which directly affects criminals' profit margins. (p. 42)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Indicators of tobacco smuggling include the level of expertise displayed by individuals or groups"</sub>
- **descriptive / typology or red flag**: Casino-sector red flags for potential money laundering include clients providing false or counterfeit identification, using multiple names or aliases, or using counterfeit bank-payment cards. (p. 43)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "clients providing false or counterfeit identification, using multiple names or aliases"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: By analyzing financial transaction data, FIUs have traced the origin and destination of funds and established connections between criminal groups previously unknown to law enforcement agencies. (p. 43)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "FIUs) have been able to trace the origin and destination of funds"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Smuggling involves moving significant sums needing laundering. Tobacco and cigarette smuggling gives a financial incentive to source products from cheaper markets and sell them where prices are higher, domestically or internationally, exploiting price differences; illicit tobacco trade is driven by the desire to avoid excise taxes and circumvent regulations prohibiting sale of such goods. (p. 43)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Smuggling involves moving significant sums of money that need to be laundered."</sub>

## CASE 9

- **descriptive / case example**: Case 9 (Israel IMPA): in-depth strategic analysis by Israel's FIU triggered a joint multi-agency task force investigation into a global illegal online gambling operation, assessed as medium-high risk in Israel, that used seemingly legitimate businesses, front men and virtual assets. (p. 44)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "which is a medium-high risk in Israel"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: Factors credited for success were strong domestic cooperation within a dedicated task force, sophisticated IT tools and data mining, and international collaboration between IMPA and counterpart FIUs, which resulted in immediate freezing of assets abroad. (p. 44)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "resulting in immediate freezing of assets abroad"</sub>
- **descriptive / method**: Within the task force, IMPA conducted strategic analysis of financial intelligence to identify suspicious entities in online gambling, including keyword searches in Unusual Activity Reports (UARs), statistical analysis of Currency Transaction Reports (CTRs) and open-source intelligence review, considering relevant typologies, indicators and activity patterns of online gambling. (p. 44)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "keyword searches in Unusual Activity Reports (UARs), statistical analysis of Currency Transaction Reports (CTRs)"</sub>
- **descriptive / method**: IMPA produced detailed network analysis and fund-flow charts showing the money trail, including transactions in Israel and abroad, amounts, dates and bank account details. Advanced IT tools helped detect links to new suspects, assets and businesses abroad, further established through counterpart FIU information requests. (p. 44)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "detailed network analysis and fund-flow charts showing the money trail"</sub>
- **descriptive / case example**: IMPA sent a spontaneous disclosure to the Israeli National Police (INP) about a previously uninvestigated entity, leading to an investigation by a thematic Task Force of the INP, the Israeli Tax Authority (ITA), the State Attorney's Office (STO) and IMPA, where IMPA's intelligence was shared with information from other agencies to build the fullest picture. (p. 44)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "IMPA disseminated a spontaneous disclosure based on the results of the strategic analysis to the Israeli National Police (INP)"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: The investigation revealed a worldwide online gambling network that organized illegal gambling, collected and distributed funds and laundered proceeds using seemingly legitimate businesses, front men and complex methods including virtual assets, involving money laundering, illegal online gambling, fraud and tax evasion. (p. 44)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The criminal activity revealed a wide scope of offences, including money laundering, illegal online gambling, fraud and tax evasion."</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: IMPA contributed to the case by mapping natural entities and businesses, providing intelligence reports, confirming suspicions about offenses, and giving a comprehensive financial intelligence picture of fund movements; after the covert phase it pursued further intelligence on new and existing subjects and affiliated entities. (p. 45)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "mapping the natural entities and businesses involved, providing intelligence reports"</sub>
- **descriptive / method**: IMPA sent information requests to several foreign FIUs; information from those FIUs and local reporting entities enabled it to detect cash transfers abroad and identify how the parties collected gamblers' funds, moved them to related accounts and integrated proceeds into the legitimate financial system. (p. 45)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "IMPA sent information requests to several foreign FIUs."</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: Foreign FIU replies provided new information, revealed newly involved entities, and included transactions, business activity information, bank account details and related assets. After an urgent request via the Egmont Secure Web (ESW), Ukraine's FIU and IMPA coordinated by correspondence and telephone, and Ukraine's FIU immediately froze the bank accounts of the main suspect and his spouse, giving time to proceed with a formal mutual legal assistance request. (p. 45)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Following an urgent request via the Egmont Secure Web (ESW), the FIU of Ukraine and IMPA took coordinated, coherent actions"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: All gathered information was integrated for the STO to continue legal actions including asset seizure and indictments. The case is presented as showing that results are achieved when the FIU is an integral part of the investigation team through covert, overt and pre-trial stages, with financial intelligence playing a crucial role at every stage. (p. 45)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "when the FIU forms an integral part of the investigation team throughout the covert, overt and pre-trial stages"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: IMPA's cooperation with counterpart FIUs revealed information on new entities and led to investigation advancement and immediate freezing of funds abroad by the counterpart FIU. (p. 45)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "led to investigation advancement and the immediate freezing of funds abroad by the counterpart FIU"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: IMPA initiated the investigation by strategic analysis using sophisticated methods and IT tools to assess known red flags and typologies for laundering online gambling proceeds, which identified an individual whose financial activity raised suspicion of involvement in illegal online gambling and laundering of proceeds. (p. 45)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "to assess known red flags and typologies relevant to money-laundering of proceeds from online gambling"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: The total value of the offenses was estimated at ILS 29.2 million and assets seized in Israel at ILS 3.4 million. When the investigation became public, seven suspects were arrested and dozens detained for questioning and evidence collection, and the State Attorney's Office submitted six Mutual Legal Assistance (MLA) requests to various jurisdictions. (p. 46)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The total value of the offenses was estimated at (ILS) 29.2 million"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: The prosecution indicted four key persons operating the network, with four charges of managing and organizing illegal gambling worth approximately ILS 3.0 million, money laundering, receiving property by deception and severe tax evasion. (p. 46)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The prosecution filed an indictment against four key persons operating the illegal network."</sub>
- **descriptive / limitation**: The case states that indicators alone may not always lead to suspicion of illegal online gambling, but can be a very useful resource in triggering enhanced inspection and further monitoring. (p. 46)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Indicators alone may not always lead to suspicion of illegal online gambling"</sub>
- **descriptive / typology or red flag**: Indicator: frequent, low-value money transfers sent to a bank account without reasonable business explanation, often using different payment methods; micro transactions are a financial behaviour associated with gambling activity. (p. 46)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Frequent, low-value money transfers sent to a bank account without reasonable business explanation"</sub>

  <sub>anchor: "An account characterized by numerous sources of deposits juxtaposed with"</sub>

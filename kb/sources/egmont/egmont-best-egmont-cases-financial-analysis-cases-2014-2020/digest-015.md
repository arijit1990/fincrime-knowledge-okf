## What Makes a Good Case

- **descriptive / context**: The Best Egmont Case Awards (BECA) competition is one of the highlights of the Egmont Group's annual activities, letting members share examples of exceptional work; the many submitted cases are judged against established criteria on what makes a case interesting and educative. (p. 126)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The Best Egmont Case Awards (BECA) competition is one of the highlights"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Cases were judged on complexity and results achieved; because judicial procedures are lengthy, some cases were unfinished at publication and others began before 2016, and those still in court were sanitized so as not to jeopardize them. (p. 126)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Those that were still under court proceedings were sanitized so as to not jeopardize cases."</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Complexity was judged by intelligence gathering procedures, challenges, use of open source criteria, and gathering intelligence within jurisdictions and from foreign jurisdictions through the Egmont Secure Web; selected cases showed superior FIU analyst skill in gathering information from private and public sources and piecing together complex financial, legal and corporate obfuscation. (p. 126)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The complexity of cases was judged according to intelligence gathering procedures"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: In each case the FIU played a crucial role in uncovering the scheme, bringing perpetrators to justice and confiscating illicit assets through judicial procedures; FIU analysts proved their importance in analyzing complex data and assembling puzzles that would often confound criminal investigators, using their unique position to cooperate with financial institutions, domestic law enforcement and foreign FIUs to uncover novel schemes. (p. 127)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the FIU played a crucial role in uncovering the criminal scheme and bringing the perpetrators to justice"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: Success in the cases is measured in effective sanctioning of criminals and retrieval of hidden stolen assets; FIUs worked with law enforcement to find which financial and non-financial instruments were used, often coordinating police, prosecutors, judges, tax and customs officials, and often advised and supported prosecutors and police to build cases that stand up to judicial review. (p. 127)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Success in these cases is measured in effective sanctioning of criminals"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: Because financial crime is often international, the FIU plays the most important role in gathering information and intelligence from other Egmont members and using it for mutual legal assistance requests and eventually freezing criminal assets hidden abroad, and plays a central role in domestic and international coordination leading to arrest, conviction, sentencing and return of stolen assets. (p. 127)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "it is the FIU that plays the most important role in gathering information and intelligence from other Egmont members"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: In many cases FIUs worked successfully with the private sector through public-private partnerships: banks and other institutions gathered information on suspected clients with FIU guidance and proactively shared information indicating possible criminal activity, showing how sectors can clean up the financial system and ensure a level playing field. (p. 127)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Of exceptional note in many of these cases is the ability of FIUs to successfully work with the private sector"</sub>

## Criteria for BECA Case

- **expected / expectation**: BECA criterion (effective case example): cases should have been concluded within the last five years. (p. 127)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Cases should have been concluded within the last five years."</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: BECA criterion: effective case examples should be submitted providing the most value to Egmont member FIUs as an informative case example. (p. 127)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Effective case examples should be submitted providing the most value to Egmont member FIUs"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: BECA criterion: cases should highlight new or sophisticated ML schemes, innovative techniques and methods, the number of agencies and jurisdictions involved, and the amount of funds. (p. 127)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Cases should highlight new or sophisticated ML schemes, innovative techniques and methods"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: BECA criterion (focus on FIU work and analysis): cases should elaborate on the FIU's work and paramount role in case development, for instance where the FIU initiated the case and/or developed intelligence and drew on international connections not otherwise easily accessible. (p. 127)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Cases should elaborate on the work of the FIU and its paramount role in the case development"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: BECA criterion: cases should demonstrate value added by FIU analysis. (p. 128)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Cases should demonstrate value added by FIU analysis."</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: BECA criterion (domestic and international cooperation): cases should give examples where effective international and/or domestic collaboration contributed to a more successful outcome. (p. 129)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Cases should provide examples where effective international and/or domestic collaboration contributed"</sub>
- **permissive / expectation**: BECA criterion: cases could indicate where they included other jurisdictions and how they demonstrated effective international cooperation. (p. 129)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Cases could indicate where they have included other jurisdictions"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: BECA criterion (evolution of the case): cases should illustrate how feedback enhanced case development and contributed to a positive outcome. (p. 129)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Cases should illustrate how feedback enhanced the development of the case"</sub>
- **permissive / expectation**: BECA other results: a case can show how it influenced changes to domestic legislation or to policies and procedures of the FIU and/or domestic and international partners. (p. 129)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "A case can show how it influenced changes to domestic legislation"</sub>
- **permissive / expectation**: BECA other results: a case can support conclusions of a national risk assessment. (p. 129)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "A case can support conclusions of a national risk assessment."</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: A BECA case describes the challenges faced in identifying the money laundering scheme and/or identifies new ML techniques or trends. (p. 129)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Cases describe challenges faced in identifying the ML scheme; and/or identify new ML techniques or trends."</sub>

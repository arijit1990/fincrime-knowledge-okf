## CASE

- **descriptive / case example**: Israel (IMPA) case 'Revealing the Many Facets of Cross-Border Money Laundering Scheme Through Diamond Trading': IMPA research exposed diamond industry vulnerabilities: extensive use of cash, very large sums per transaction, ease of moving funds internationally undetected, and ease of manipulating the expertise needed to value diamonds. - **descriptive / typology or red flag**: IMPA's database analysis found diamond dealers laundering through their companies via transfers to/from foreign entities unrelated to diamonds, fictitious invoices made out to entities different from those sending funds, MSB activity by diamond companies under guise of import/export of diamonds, and dealer accounts used solely to funnel funds; funds also passed through accounts of dealers suspected abroad of tax fraud and illegal diamond trade, and possible links to criminal organizations were identified.

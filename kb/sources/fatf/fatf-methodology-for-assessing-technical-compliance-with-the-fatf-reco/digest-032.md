## RECOMMENDATION 16 PAYMENT TRANSPARENCY

- **descriptive / limitation**: Criteria 16.4 to 16.24 of revised R.16 do not apply to transfers flowing from a transaction carried out with a credit, debit or prepaid card for the purchase of goods or services, nor to cash withdrawals. (p. 264)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Assessors should note that criteria 16.4 to 16.24 do not apply to:"</sub>
- **descriptive / limitation**: Settlement of payments or value transfers may occur under a net settlement arrangement. Where net settlement results from payments carried out on behalf of customers, information about the underlying transactions need not accompany the net settlement, but R.16 requirements still apply to the underlying transactions themselves. (p. 264)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "information about the underlying transactions is not required to accompany the net settlement"</sub>
- **descriptive / limitation**: Note to Assessors on R.16 (paragraph 3(a)): for the requirements on ordering, intermediary and beneficiary financial institutions, no information needs to accompany transfers and settlements between financial institutions when the originator and the beneficiary are both financial institutions acting on their own behalf. (p. 264)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "no information is required to accompany financial institution-to-financial institution transfers and settlements where both the originator"</sub>

## General principles

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 16.1: countries should ensure the payment chain starts at the financial institution that receives the originator's instructions to transfer funds to the beneficiary, and ends with the financial institution that services the beneficiary's account or provides cash to the beneficiary. (p. 265)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "starts at the financial institution that receives the instructions from the originator for transfer of funds to the beneficiary"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 16.2: countries should ensure information accompanying cross-border and domestic payments or value transfers is structured, to the extent possible, in line with the established standards of the system used, such as ISO 20022, and is sufficiently detailed to enable identification of the originator and beneficiary. (p. 265)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "structured, to the extent possible, in accordance with the established standards of the system used such as ISO 20022"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 16.3: financial institutions should be required to ensure that the account number accompanying cross-border and domestic payments or value transfers is not used to disguise the identification of the country where the financial institution servicing the account resides. (p. 265)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "should not be used to disguise the identification of the country where the financial institution that services the account resides"</sub>

## Batch transfers

- **permissive / requirement**: Criterion 16.8: several individual cross-border payments from a single originator bundled in a batch file may be exempted from the originator information requirements of criteria 16.4 and 16.6, provided the ordering financial institution is required to include the originator's account number or unique transaction reference number, and the batch file contains required and accurate originator information and full beneficiary information that is fully traceable within the beneficiary country. (p. 266)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "they may be exempted from the requirements of criteria 16.4 and 16.6 in respect of the originator information"</sub>

## Requirements for ordering (debtor) financial institutions – Domestic payments and value transfers

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 16.9: where countries apply a de minimis threshold (no higher than USD/EUR 1 000) for domestic payments or value transfers, the ordering financial institution should be required to ensure that those below the threshold are always accompanied by the originator's name and the originator's account number or a unique transaction reference number allowing the transaction to be traced back to the originator or beneficiary. This criterion does not apply if the information can be made available to the beneficiary financial institution and appropriate authorities by other means, in which case criterion 16.12 applies. (p. 266)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "If countries apply a de minimis threshold (no higher than USD/EUR 1 000) for domestic payments and value transfers"</sub>

## Requirements for Ordering (debtor) financial institutions - Cross-border payments and value transfers

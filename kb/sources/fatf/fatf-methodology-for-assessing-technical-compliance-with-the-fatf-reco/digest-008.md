## Sustained outreach concerning terrorist financing issues

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 8.2(a): countries should have clear policies to promote accountability, integrity and public confidence in the administration and management of NPOs. (p. 52)[^original]

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 8.2(b): countries should undertake outreach and educational programmes, as appropriate, to raise and deepen awareness among NPOs and the donor community of NPOs' potential vulnerabilities to terrorist financing abuse and TF risks, and of the measures NPOs can take to protect themselves.   <sub>anchor: "undertake outreach and educational programmes as appropriate to raise and deepen awareness among NPOs as well as the donor community"</sub>

  <sub>anchor: "work with NPOs to develop and refine best practices to address terrorist financing risk"</sub>

## Focused, proportionate and risk-based oversight or monitoring of NPOs

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 8.3: countries should take steps to promote focused, proportionate and risk-based oversight or monitoring of NPOs, and should demonstrate that such measures are in place for NPOs. (p. 52)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "demonstrate that they have in place focused, proportionate and risk-based measures applying to NPOs"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 8.4: appropriate authorities should monitor, where needed, NPOs' compliance with the focused, proportionate and risk-based measures applied to them, and should be able to apply effective, proportionate and dissuasive sanctions for violations by NPOs or persons acting on their behalf. Footnote 68 notes that risk-based measures may include NPOs' self-regulatory measures and internal controls. (p. 52)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "be able to apply effective, proportionate and dissuasive sanctions for violations by NPOs or persons"</sub>

## Sustained outreach concerning terrorist financing issues

- **expected / requirement**: Under criterion 8.2(d), countries should encourage NPOs to carry out their transactions through regulated financial and payment channels where this is feasible, taking account of how far the capacity of financial sectors differs between countries and areas, and of the risks that come with using cash. (p. 52)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "encourage NPOs to conduct transactions via regulated financial and payment channels, wherever feasible"</sub>

## Focused, proportionate and risk-based oversight or monitoring of NPOs

- **descriptive / context**: Footnote 67 notes that regulatory and self-regulatory measures already in place, internal controls within NPOs, or other measures may already be enough to address the present terrorist financing risk to NPOs in a jurisdiction. (p. 52)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "already sufficiently address the current terrorist financing risk to the NPOs in a jurisdiction"</sub>

  <sub>anchor: "terrorist financing risks to the sector should be periodically re- assessed"</sub>

  <sub>anchor: "freezing of accounts, removal of trustees, fines, de-certification, delicensing and de-registration"</sub>

## Effective information gathering and investigation

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 8.5(a) to (c): countries should ensure effective co-operation, co-ordination and information-sharing, to the extent possible, among all levels of appropriate authorities or organisations holding relevant information on NPOs; have investigative expertise and capability to examine NPOs suspected of being exploited by, or actively supporting, terrorist activity or organisations; and ensure that information on the administration and management of particular NPOs, including financial and programmatic information, can be obtained during an investigation. (p. 53)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "have investigative expertise and capability to examine those NPOs suspected of either being exploited by, or actively supporting, terrorist activity"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 8.5(d): countries should establish appropriate mechanisms to ensure that, where there is suspicion or reasonable grounds to suspect an NPO is involved in TF abuse or is a front for terrorist fundraising; is being exploited as a conduit for TF, including to escape asset freezing measures; or is concealing or obscuring clandestine diversion of funds intended for legitimate purposes to terrorists or terrorist organisations, this information is promptly shared with competent authorities for preventive or investigative action. (p. 53)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "that this information is promptly shared with competent authorities, in order to take preventive or investigative action"</sub>

## Effective capacity to respond to international requests for information about an NPO of concern

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 8.6: countries should identify appropriate points of contact and procedures to respond to international requests for information about particular NPOs suspected of terrorist financing or other forms of terrorist support. (p. 53)[^original]

## RECOMMENDATION 9 FINANCIAL INSTITUTION SECRECY LAWS

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 9.1: financial institution secrecy laws should not inhibit implementation of the FATF Recommendations. (p. 54)[^original]

(p. 54)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the sharing of information between financial institutions where this is required by R.13, R.16 or R.17"</sub>

## RECOMMENDATION 10 CUSTOMER DUE DILIGENCE (CDD)

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 10.1: financial institutions should be prohibited from keeping anonymous accounts or accounts in obviously fictitious names. (p. 55)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "prohibited from keeping anonymous accounts or accounts in obviously fictitious names"</sub>

## When CDD is required

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 10.2: financial institutions should be required to undertake CDD when establishing business relations; when carrying out occasional transactions above the applicable designated threshold of USD/EUR 15 000, including linked transactions in a single or several operations; when carrying out occasional wire transfers in the circumstances covered by Recommendation 16 and its Interpretive Note; when there is a suspicion of ML/TF regardless of any exemptions or thresholds; or when they doubt the veracity or adequacy of previously obtained customer identification data. (p. 55)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "carrying out occasional transactions above the applicable designated threshold (USD/EUR 15 000)"</sub>

## Required CDD measures for all customers

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 10.3: financial institutions should be required to identify the customer (permanent or occasional, natural or legal person or legal arrangement) and verify the customer's identity using reliable, independent source documents, data or information (identification data). (p. 55)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "verify that customer’s identity using reliable, independent source documents, data or information (identification data)"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 10.4: financial institutions should be required to verify that any person purporting to act on behalf of the customer is so authorised, and to identify and verify that person's identity. (p. 55)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "verify that any person purporting to act on behalf of the customer is so authorised"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 10.5: financial institutions should be required to identify the beneficial owner and take reasonable measures to verify the beneficial owner's identity, using relevant information or data from a reliable source, so that the institution is satisfied it knows who the beneficial owner is. (p. 55)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "take reasonable measures to verify the identity of the beneficial owner, using the relevant information or data"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Footnote 71 says the principle that financial institutions carry out CDD should be set out in law, while the specific CDD requirements may be set out in enforceable means. (p. 55)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "should be set out in law, though specific requirements may be set out in enforceable means"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 10.6: financial institutions should be required to understand and, as appropriate, obtain information on the purpose and intended nature of the business relationship. (p. 56)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "understand and, as appropriate, obtain information on, the purpose and intended nature of the business relationship"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 10.7: financial institutions should be required to conduct ongoing due diligence on the business relationship, including scrutinising transactions throughout the relationship for consistency with the institution's knowledge of the customer, their business and risk profile and, where necessary, source of funds; and keeping CDD documents, data and information up-to-date and relevant through reviews of existing records, particularly for higher risk customer categories. (p. 56)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "particularly for higher risk categories of customers"</sub>

## Specific CDD measures required for legal persons and legal arrangements

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 10.9: for legal person or arrangement customers, the financial institution should identify and verify the customer through name, legal form and proof of existence; the powers that regulate and bind the legal person or arrangement and the names of relevant persons in senior management positions; and the address of the registered office and, if different, a principal place of business. (p. 56)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the address of the registered office and, if different, a principal place of business"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 10.10(a): for legal person customers, financial institutions should be required to identify, and take reasonable measures to verify, the beneficial owners through the identity of the natural person(s) (if any) who ultimately has a controlling ownership interest in the legal person. (p. 56)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the identity of the natural person(s) (if any73) who ultimately has a controlling ownership interest74 in a legal person"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Footnote 74: a controlling ownership interest depends on the company's ownership structure and may be based on a threshold, for example any person owning more than a certain percentage of the company (e.g. 25%). Footnote 73 notes that ownership interests can be so diversified that no natural person, acting alone or together, controls through ownership. (p. 56)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "any person owning more than a certain percentage of the company (e.g. 25%)"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Footnote 72: where the customer or the owner of the controlling interest is a company listed on a stock exchange and subject to disclosure requirements ensuring adequate transparency of beneficial ownership, or is a majority-owned subsidiary of such a company, it is not necessary to identify and verify any shareholder or beneficial owner; the data may come from a public register, the customer or other reliable sources. (p. 56)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "it is not necessary to identify and verify the identity of any shareholder or beneficial owner of such companies"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Under criterion 10.8, where the customer is a legal person or legal arrangement, the financial institution should be required to understand what the customer's business is and how the customer is owned and controlled. (p. 56)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the financial institution should be required to understand the nature of the customer"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 10.10(b) and (c): where there is doubt under (a) whether the person(s) with controlling ownership interest is the beneficial owner, or where no natural person exerts control through ownership, financial institutions should identify the natural person(s) (if any) exercising control through other means; where no natural person is identified under (a) or (b), they should identify the relevant natural person who holds the position of senior managing official. (p. 57)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the identity of the relevant natural person who holds the position of senior managing official"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 10.11: for legal arrangement customers, financial institutions should be required to identify, and take reasonable measures to verify, beneficial owners: for trusts, the settlor, trustee(s), protector (if any), beneficiaries or class of beneficiaries, and any other natural person exercising ultimate effective control over the trust (including through a chain of control or ownership); for other types of legal arrangements, persons in equivalent or similar positions. (p. 57)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the identity of the settlor, the trustee(s), the protector (if any), the beneficiaries or class of beneficiaries"</sub>

## CDD for Beneficiaries of Life Insurance Policies

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 10.12: in addition to CDD on the customer and beneficial owner, financial institutions should be required to carry out CDD on the beneficiary of life insurance and other investment-related insurance policies as soon as the beneficiary is identified or designated: take the name of a specifically named beneficiary; for a beneficiary designated by characteristics, class or other means, obtain sufficient information to be satisfied that they can establish the beneficiary's identity at pay-out; and in both cases verify the beneficiary's identity at the time of pay-out. (p. 57)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the verification of the identity of the beneficiary should occur at the time of the pay-out"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 10.13: financial institutions should be required to include the life insurance beneficiary as a relevant risk factor in deciding whether enhanced CDD applies; if a beneficiary that is a legal person or arrangement presents higher risk, enhanced measures should include reasonable measures to identify and verify the beneficiary's beneficial owner at the time of pay-out. (p. 57)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "include the beneficiary of a life insurance policy as a relevant risk factor in determining whether enhanced CDD"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Footnote 75 says that, for trust beneficiaries designated by characteristics or by class, financial institutions should obtain enough information about the beneficiary to be satisfied they can establish the beneficiary's identity at pay-out or when the beneficiary intends to exercise vested rights. (p. 57)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "financial institutions should obtain sufficient information concerning the beneficiary"</sub>

## Timing of verification

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 10.14: financial institutions should be required to verify the identity of the customer and beneficial owner before or during establishing a business relationship or conducting transactions for occasional customers; or, if permitted, may complete verification after the relationship is established provided it occurs as soon as reasonably practicable, is essential not to interrupt the normal conduct of business, and ML/TF risks are effectively managed. (p. 58)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "this is essential not to interrupt the normal conduct of business"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 10.15: financial institutions should be required to adopt risk management procedures on the conditions under which a customer may use the business relationship before verification. (p. 58)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "conditions under which a customer may utilise the business relationship prior to verification"</sub>

## Existing customers

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 10.16: financial institutions should be required to apply CDD requirements to existing customers on the basis of materiality and risk, and to conduct due diligence on existing relationships at appropriate times, taking into account whether and when CDD was previously undertaken and the adequacy of data obtained. Footnote 76 defines existing customers as those as at the date the new national requirements come into force. (p. 58)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "apply CDD requirements to existing customers76 on the basis of materiality and risk"</sub>

## Risk-Based Approach

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 10.17: financial institutions should be required to perform enhanced due diligence where ML/TF risks are higher. (p. 58)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "required to perform enhanced due diligence where the ML/TF risks are higher"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Footnote 77: encouragement of simplified measures can take the form of guidance from government, supervisors or other competent authorities on when simplified measures may be appropriate and their form, or outreach or other engagement with financial institutions and DNFBPs. (p. 58)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "encouragement can take the form of guidance issued by the government, supervisor or other competent authority"</sub>

## Failure to satisfactorily complete CDD

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 10.19: where a financial institution cannot comply with relevant CDD measures, it should be required not to open the account, commence business relations or perform the transaction, or to terminate the business relationship, and should be required to consider making a suspicious transaction report (STR) on the customer. (p. 58)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "it should be required not to open the account, commence business relations or perform the transaction"</sub>

## Risk-Based Approach

- **expected / requirement**: Under criterion 10.18, financial institutions should be allowed and encouraged to apply simplified CDD where lower risks have been identified through an adequate risk analysis by the country or by the institution. (p. 58)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "should be allowed and encouraged77 to apply simplified CDD measures where lower risks have been identified"</sub>

## CDD and tipping-off

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 10.20: where a financial institution forms a suspicion of ML or TF and reasonably believes that performing CDD will tip off the customer, it should be permitted not to pursue CDD and instead should be required to file an STR. (p. 59)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "they should be permitted not to pursue the CDD process and instead should be required to file an STR"</sub>

## RECOMMENDATION 11 RECORD KEEPING

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 11.1: financial institutions should be required to maintain all necessary records on domestic and international transactions for at least five years following completion of the transaction. (p. 59)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "maintain all necessary records on transactions, both domestic and international, for at least five years following completion of the transaction"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 11.2: financial institutions should be required to keep all records obtained through CDD, account files, business correspondence and results of any analysis for at least five years after the termination of the business relationship or after the date of the occasional transaction. (p. 60)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "account files and business correspondence and results of any analysis undertaken, for at least five years"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 11.3: transaction records should be sufficient to permit reconstruction of individual transactions so as to provide, if necessary, evidence for prosecution of criminal activity. (p. 60)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "sufficient to permit reconstruction of individual transactions so as to provide, if necessary, evidence for prosecution of criminal activity"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Under criterion 11.4, financial institutions should be required to make sure that all CDD information and transaction records can be obtained quickly by domestic competent authorities when they have appropriate authority. (p. 60)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "available swiftly to domestic competent authorities upon appropriate authority"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Footnote 78 states that the principle that financial institutions keep records of transactions and of information obtained through CDD should be set out in law. (p. 60)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "should maintain records on transactions and information obtained through CDD measures should be set out in law"</sub>

## Key Recommended Actions (KRA)

- **expected / expectation**: For IO.5, KRAs are kept separate from Other Recommended Actions and only relate to Immediate Outcomes rated ME or LE, or Recommendations rated PC or NC where these relate to an IO rated ME or LE. If IO.5 is rated HE or SE, the KRA section is deleted and all recommended actions for the chapter go under Other Recommended Actions. There should not normally be more than 2-3 KRAs per Immediate Outcome, including KRAs for technical compliance for related Recommendations. (p. 227)[^original]

## Chapter 5 (IO.5)

- **descriptive / context**: Footnote 236 notes that the OECD Global Forum on Transparency and Exchange of Information for Tax Purposes also assesses the availability of accurate, up-to-date basic and beneficial ownership information, and that its findings may sometimes differ from FATF's because the two bodies' methodologies, objectives and standard scope differ. (p. 227)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The availability of accurate and up-to-date basic and beneficial ownership information is also assessed by the OECD Global Forum"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: For IO.5, weighting and conclusion follow the IO.1 instructions. The IO.5 template analysis covers: 5.1 identifying, assessing and understanding ML/TF risks of legal persons and arrangements; 5.2 mitigating measures preventing misuse of legal persons and arrangements; 5.3 legal persons: timely access to adequate, accurate and current basic and beneficial ownership information; 5.4 legal arrangements: timely access to the same information; and 5.5 effectiveness, proportionality and dissuasiveness of sanctions. (p. 228)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "5.3. Legal persons: Timely access to adequate, accurate and current basic and beneficial ownership information"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: For beneficial ownership information on legal arrangements under IO.5 core issue 5.4, the Methodology refers assessors to the Methodology for Recommendation 25. (p. 228)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "See the Methodology for Recommendation 25 regarding beneficial ownership information for legal arrangements."</sub>

## Chapter 6. Financial Intelligence

- **descriptive / context**: Chapter 6 of the MER template assesses Immediate Outcome 6 (IO.6). The Recommendations relevant to the effectiveness assessment are R.29-32 and elements of R.1, 2, 4, 8, 9, 15, 34 and 40. (p. 229)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The relevant Immediate Outcomes considered and assessed in this chapter is IO.6."</sub>

## Key Recommended Actions (KRA)

- **expected / expectation**: For IO.6, assessors should list all the main corrective actions the country needs to improve effectiveness and technical compliance in a targeted and prioritised way, and indicate which IO or Recommendation each action relates to. (p. 230)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Assessors should list all the main corrective actions required for the country to"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: For IO.6, KRAs are separate from Other Recommended Actions and relate only to IOs rated ME or LE or Recommendations rated PC or NC tied to such an IO. If IO.6 is rated HE or SE the KRA section is deleted. There should not normally be more than 2-3 KRAs per Immediate Outcome. (p. 230)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "If IO.6 is rated HE or SE, delete this section, and reflect all recommended actions"</sub>

## Other Recommended Actions

- **expected / expectation**: If IO.6 is rated HE or SE, all recommended actions for the chapter should appear under Other Recommended Actions. ## Chapter 6. Financial Intelligence

- **expected / expectation**: IO.6 relates to both money laundering and the financing of terrorism, so assessors should note any issues that relate specifically to either ML or TF. (p. 231)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Assessors should note any issues which relate specifically to either ML or TF."</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Suggested IO.6 sub-headings are: 6.1 timely access to relevant, accurate and up-to-date information (by the FIU; by other competent authorities); 6.2 production and dissemination of financial intelligence (production; dissemination; FIU financial intelligence supporting needs of competent authorities; other competent authorities producing financial intelligence where relevant); 6.3 cooperation and exchange of information/financial intelligence (co-operation and exchange; security and confidentiality); 6.4 using information/financial intelligence (for investigations and developing evidence; for identifying and tracing criminal proceeds or instrumentalities). (p. 231)[^original]

- **descriptive / context**: The IO.8 template analysis covers: 8.1 prioritisation of asset recovery as a policy objective and effective agency structures and cooperation frameworks (including periodic review of the asset recovery regime); 8.2 identifying and tracing criminal property and property of corresponding value; 8.3 freezing and/or seizing such property (active pursuit of provisional measures from financial investigations; expeditious measures); 8.4 managing frozen or seized property to preserve its value; 8.5 confiscating and enforcing confiscation orders (domestic and abroad); 8.6 returning confiscated property to victims; and 8.7 identifying and confiscating falsely or undeclared currency/BNIs or those related to ML/TF or predicate offences (identifying and seizing; confiscation; sanctions).

## Casinos

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 28.1: countries should ensure casinos are subject to AML/CFT regulation and supervision; at a minimum casinos should be required to be licensed, competent authorities should take legal or regulatory measures to prevent criminals or their associates from holding (or being the beneficial owner of) a significant or controlling interest, holding a management function, or being an operator of a casino, and casinos should be supervised for AML/CFT compliance. (p. 100)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Countries should ensure that casinos are subject to AML/CFT regulation and supervision."</sub>

## DNFBPs other than casinos

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 28.2: there should be a designated competent authority or SRB responsible for monitoring and ensuring DNFBPs' compliance with AML/CFT requirements. (p. 100)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "designated competent authority or SRB responsible for monitoring and ensuring compliance of DNFBPs"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 28.3: countries should ensure the other categories of DNFBPs are subject to systems for monitoring compliance with AML/CFT requirements. (p. 100)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "other categories of DNFBPs are subject to systems for monitoring compliance with AML/CFT requirements"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 28.4: the designated competent authority or SRB should have adequate powers to perform its functions including monitoring compliance; take necessary measures to prevent criminals or their associates from being professionally accredited, or holding (or being beneficial owner of) a significant or controlling interest or a management function in a DNFBP; and have sanctions available in line with Recommendation 35. (p. 100)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "prevent criminals or their associates from being professionally accredited"</sub>

## All DNFBPs

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 28.5: supervision of DNFBPs should be risk-sensitive, including determining the frequency and intensity of AML/CFT supervision or monitoring on the basis of understanding of ML/TF risks and DNFBP characteristics (in particular diversity and number), and taking into account each DNFBP's ML/TF risk profile and the discretion allowed under the risk-based approach when assessing the adequacy of its AML/CFT internal controls, policies and procedures. (p. 100)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Supervision of DNFBPs should be performed on a risk-sensitive basis"</sub>

## RECOMMENDATION 29 FINANCIAL INTELLIGENCE UNITS (FIU)

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 29.1: countries should establish an FIU as a national centre for receiving and analysing suspicious transaction reports and other information relevant to money laundering, associated predicate offences and terrorist financing, and for disseminating the results of that analysis. Footnote 165: R.29 does not prejudge a country's choice of FIU model and applies equally to all. (p. 101)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Countries should establish an FIU with responsibility for acting as a national centre for receipt and analysis"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 29.2: the FIU should serve as the central agency for receipt of disclosures filed by reporting entities, including suspicious transaction reports required by Recommendations 20 and 23 and any other information required by national legislation (such as cash transaction reports, wire transfer reports and other threshold-based declarations or disclosures). (p. 102)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "should serve as the central agency for the receipt of disclosures filed by reporting entities"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 29.3: the FIU should be able to obtain and use additional information from reporting entities as needed for proper analysis, and have access to the widest possible range of financial, administrative and law enforcement information it requires. (p. 102)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "be able to obtain and use additional information from reporting entities, as needed to perform its analysis properly"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Footnote 166: in its analysis the FIU should be able to obtain from any reporting entity additional information relating to a suspicion of ML/TF, but this does not include indiscriminate requests for information ("fishing expeditions"). Footnote 167: the widest possible range of information should include open or public sources, information collected or maintained by or for other authorities and, where appropriate, commercially held data. (p. 102)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "This does not include indiscriminate requests for information to reporting entities"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 29.4: the FIU should conduct operational analysis, which uses available and obtainable information to identify specific targets, follow the trail of particular activities or transactions, and determine links between targets and possible proceeds of crime, ML, predicate offences and TF; and strategic analysis, which uses available and obtainable information, including data from other competent authorities, to identify ML and TF trends and patterns. (p. 102)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "operational analysis, which uses available and obtainable information to identify specific targets"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 29.5: the FIU should be able to disseminate information and the results of its analysis spontaneously and upon request to relevant competent authorities, using dedicated, secure and protected channels. (p. 103)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "should use dedicated, secure and protected channels for the dissemination"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 29.6: the FIU should protect information by having rules governing security and confidentiality (including handling, storage, dissemination, protection of and access to information); ensuring staff have the necessary security clearance and understand their responsibilities in handling sensitive and confidential information; and limiting access to its facilities and information, including IT systems. (p. 103)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "ensuring that FIU staff members have the necessary security clearance levels"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 29.7: the FIU should be operationally independent and autonomous, by having authority and capacity to carry out its functions freely (including autonomous decisions to analyse, request and/or forward or disseminate specific information); being able to make arrangements with other domestic authorities or foreign counterparts independently; having distinct core functions when located within another authority's structure; and being able to obtain and deploy needed resources free from undue political, government or industry influence. (p. 103)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "free from any undue political, government or industry influence or interference"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 29.8: where a country has created an FIU that is not an Egmont Group member, the FIU should apply for Egmont Group membership, submitting an unconditional application and fully engaging in the application process. (p. 103)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The FIU should submit an unconditional application for membership to the Egmont Group"</sub>

## AUTHORITIES

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 30.1: there should be designated law enforcement authorities responsible for ensuring that money laundering, predicate offences and terrorist financing offences are properly investigated within the framework of national AML/CFT policies. (p. 104)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "designated law enforcement authorities that have responsibility for ensuring that money laundering, predicate offences"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 30.2: law enforcement investigators of predicate offences should either be authorised to pursue investigation of any related ML/TF offences during a parallel financial investigation, or be able to refer the case to another agency to follow up, regardless of where the predicate offence occurred. (p. 104)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "regardless of where the predicate offence occurred"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: Footnote 168: a parallel financial investigation is a financial investigation conducted alongside, or in the context of, a traditional criminal investigation into ML, TF and/or predicate offences; a financial investigation is an enquiry into the financial affairs related to criminal activity aimed at identifying the extent of criminal networks and/or scale of criminality, identifying and tracing criminal property and property of corresponding value, and developing evidence usable in criminal and/or confiscation proceedings. (p. 104)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "A parallel financial investigation refers to conducting a financial investigation alongside, or in the context of"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criteria 30.4 and 30.5: Recommendation 30 should also apply to non-law-enforcement competent authorities responsible for pursuing financial investigations of predicate offences, to the extent they exercise functions covered by R.30; and if anti-corruption enforcement authorities are designated to investigate ML/TF offences arising from or related to corruption, they should have sufficient powers to identify, trace and initiate freezing and seizing of criminal property and property of corresponding value. (p. 104)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "If anti-corruption enforcement authorities are designated to investigate ML/TF offences arising from, or related to, corruption offences"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Note to Assessors for Recommendations 30 and 31: assessors should also consult paragraph 19 of the Introduction to the Methodology. For every criterion with requirements about criminal property and property of corresponding value, those requirements cover the property whether a criminal defendant or a third party owns or holds it, without prejudicing the rights of bona fide third parties. (p. 104)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "these apply whether the property is owned or held by a criminal defendant or by a third party"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 30.3: one or more competent authorities should be designated to act quickly in identifying and tracing criminal property and property of corresponding value, and in starting the freezing and seizing of it. (p. 104)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "There should be one or more designated competent authorities to expeditiously identify, trace and initiate freezing"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 31.1: competent authorities investigating ML, associated predicate offences and TF should be able to obtain access to all necessary documents and information for investigations, prosecutions and related actions, including powers to use compulsory measures for production of records held by financial institutions, DNFBPs and other persons; search of persons and premises; taking witness statements; and seizing and obtaining evidence. (p. 105)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "obtain access to all necessary documents and information for use in those investigations, and in prosecutions and related actions"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 31.2: competent authorities conducting investigations should be able to use a wide range of investigative techniques for ML, associated predicate offences and TF, including undercover operations, intercepting communications, accessing computer systems and controlled delivery. (p. 105)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "use a wide range of investigative techniques for the investigation of money laundering"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 31.3: countries should ensure competent authorities have timely access to a wide range of information, particularly to support identification and tracing of criminal property and property of corresponding value, including mechanisms to identify in a timely manner whether natural or legal persons hold or control accounts, and a process to identify assets without prior notification to the owner. Footnote 169 says the examples of information types in R.31 should not be considered exhaustive. (p. 105)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "a process to identify assets without prior notification to the owner"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 31.4: competent authorities investigating ML, associated predicate offences and TF should be able to ask for all relevant information held by the FIU. (p. 106)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "should be able to ask for all relevant information held by the FIU"</sub>

## RECOMMENDATION 32 CASH COURIERS

- **permissive / context**: Recommendation 32 may be implemented on a supra-national basis by a supra-national jurisdiction, so that only movements crossing the external borders of that jurisdiction are considered cross-border; such arrangements are assessed on a supra-national basis per paragraphs 29 to 33 of the Introduction to the Methodology. (p. 107)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "may be implemented on a supra-national basis by a supra-national jurisdiction"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 32.2: in a declaration system, all persons making a physical cross-border transportation of currency or BNIs exceeding a pre-set maximum threshold of USD/EUR 15 000 should be required to submit a truthful declaration to designated competent authorities; countries may choose a written declaration system for all travellers, a written declaration system for travellers carrying amounts above a threshold, and/or an oral declaration system for all travellers. (p. 107)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "a value exceeding a pre-set, maximum threshold of USD/EUR 15 000"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 32.3: in a disclosure system, travellers should be required to give a truthful answer and provide the authorities with appropriate information on request, but are not required to make an upfront written or oral declaration. (p. 107)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "but are not required to make an upfront written or oral declaration"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 32.4: on discovery of a false declaration or disclosure, or a failure to declare or disclose, designated competent authorities should have authority to request and obtain further information from the carrier on the origin of the currency or BNIs and their intended use. (p. 107)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "request and obtain further information from the carrier with regard to the origin of the currency or BNIs"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 32.5: persons who make a false declaration or disclosure should be subject to proportionate and dissuasive criminal, civil or administrative sanctions. (p. 107)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Persons who make a false declaration or disclosure should be subject to proportionate and dissuasive sanctions"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 32.1: countries should operate either a declaration system or a disclosure system covering currency and bearer negotiable instruments (BNIs) transported across borders in either direction. They should require a declaration or disclosure for all physical cross-border transportation, by travellers or through mail and cargo, but may use different systems for different modes of transportation. (p. 107)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Countries should implement a declaration system or a disclosure system for incoming and outgoing cross-border transportation of currency"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 32.6: information obtained through the declaration/disclosure process should be available to the FIU, either through a system whereby the FIU is notified about suspicious cross-border transportation incidents or by making the declaration/disclosure information directly available to the FIU in some other way. (p. 108)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "a system whereby the FIU is notified about suspicious cross-border transportation incidents"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 32.7: at the domestic level, countries should ensure adequate co-ordination among customs, immigration and other related authorities on issues related to implementing Recommendation 32. (p. 108)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "adequate co-ordination among customs, immigration and other related authorities"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 32.8: competent authorities should be able to stop or restrain currency or BNIs for a reasonable time to ascertain whether evidence of ML/TF may be found, where there is a suspicion of ML/TF or predicate offences or where there is a false declaration or false disclosure. (p. 108)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "stop or restrain currency or BNIs for a reasonable time in order to ascertain whether evidence"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: Criterion 32.9: countries should ensure the declaration/disclosure system allows international co-operation and assistance under Recommendations 36 to 40; to facilitate this, information shall be retained when a declaration or disclosure exceeding the prescribed threshold is made, when there is a false declaration or disclosure, or when there is a suspicion of ML/TF. Footnote 170: at a minimum the information should set out the amount of currency or BNIs declared, disclosed or detected and the identification data of the bearer(s). (p. 108)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "To facilitate such co-operation, information170 shall be retained when"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 32.10: countries should ensure strict safeguards for proper use of information collected through declaration/disclosure systems, without restricting trade payments between countries for goods and services or the freedom of capital movements in any way. (p. 108)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "strict safeguards exist to ensure proper use of information collected through the declaration/disclosure systems"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 32.11: persons carrying out a physical cross-border transportation of currency or BNIs related to ML/TF or predicate offences should be subject to proportionate and dissuasive criminal, civil or administrative sanctions, and to measures consistent with Recommendation 4 enabling confiscation of such currency or BNIs. (p. 108)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "measures consistent with Recommendation 4 which would enable the confiscation of such currency or BNIs"</sub>

## Annex C. TECHNICAL COMPLIANCE ANNEX

- **descriptive / context**: The technical compliance annex template continues, in numerical order, with a criterion rating and a Weighting and Conclusion for Recommendations 17 (reliance on third parties) through 40 (other forms of international cooperation), covering internal controls, higher-risk countries, suspicious transaction reporting, tipping-off, DNFBP measures, beneficial ownership transparency, supervision, FIUs, law enforcement, cash couriers, statistics, guidance, sanctions, international instruments, mutual legal assistance and extradition. (p. 252)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Recommendation 20 – Reporting of suspicious transaction"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Regarding Recommendation 36, the UNCAC Implementation Review Mechanism (IRM), with UNODC as secretariat, assesses implementation of the UNCAC. The FATF assesses compliance with R.36, which in relation to the UNCAC has a narrower scope and focus, so findings may differ because of differences in methodologies, objectives and scope. (p. 253)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The FATF assesses compliance with FATF Recommendation 36 which, in relation to the UNCAC, has a narrower scope and focus."</sub>

## Technical Compliance Deficiencies

- **expected / expectation**: Annex Table 1 (Compliance with FATF Recommendations) should set out the rating and a list of all deficiencies identified for each Recommendation, from R.1 (assessing risks and applying a risk-based approach) to R.40 (other forms of international cooperation). ## Glossary of Acronyms

- **descriptive / definition**: The MER template glossary defines AML/CFT/CPF as Anti-Money Laundering, Combating the Financing of Terrorism and Combatting the Financing of Proliferation of Weapons of Mass Destruction; acronyms already defined in the FATF 40 Recommendations are not included. (p. 257)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Acronyms already defined in the FATF 40 Recommendations are not included into this Glossary"</sub>

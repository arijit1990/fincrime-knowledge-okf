## Characteristics of an effective system

- **descriptive / context**: In an effective IO.10 system, terrorists, terrorist organisations and financiers are identified and deprived of resources, including through proper implementation of targeted financial sanctions against persons designated by the UN Security Council and under applicable national or supra-national regimes, and the country understands TF risks and takes appropriate, proportionate action. (p. 163)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "proper implementation of targeted financial sanctions against persons and entities designated by the United Nations Security Council"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: In an effective IO.10 system, focused, proportionate and risk-based measures prevent the raising and moving of funds through NPOs or methods at risk of terrorist misuse without unduly disrupting or discouraging legitimate NPO activities, ultimately reducing TF flows. (p. 163)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "without unduly disrupting or discouraging legitimate NPO activities"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: IO.10 relates primarily to Recommendations 1, 4, 6 and 8 and also to elements of Recommendations 14, 15, 16, 26, 30 to 32, 35, 37, 38 and 40. (p. 163)[^original]

## Characteristics of an effective system

- **descriptive / context**: In an effective IO.11 system, persons and entities designated under UNSCRs on WMD proliferation are identified, deprived of resources and prevented from raising, moving and using funds for proliferation financing, and targeted financial sanctions are fully and properly implemented without delay and monitored for compliance.

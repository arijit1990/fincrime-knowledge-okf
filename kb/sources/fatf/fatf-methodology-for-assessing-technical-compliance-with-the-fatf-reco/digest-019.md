## Immediate Outcome 5

- **descriptive / context**: Immediate Outcome 5 states the intended outcome that legal persons and arrangements are prevented from misuse for ML or TF, and that information on their beneficial ownership is available to competent authorities without impediments. (p. 143)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Legal persons and arrangements are prevented from misuse for money laundering or terrorist financing"</sub>

## Characteristics of an effective system:

- **descriptive / context**: In an effective IO.5 system, the country identifies, assesses and understands ML/TF risks of legal persons and arrangements created in the country and of foreign ones with sufficient links, and has measures to prevent criminal use, make them sufficiently transparent, and ensure adequate, accurate and up-to-date basic and beneficial ownership information is available on a timely basis. (p. 143)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "ensure that adequate, accurate and up-to-date basic and beneficial ownership information"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: In an effective IO.5 system, basic information is publicly available, beneficial ownership information is available to competent authorities, and persons who breach the measures face effective, proportionate and dissuasive sanctions, making legal persons and arrangements unattractive for criminal misuse. (p. 143)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Basic information is available publicly, and beneficial ownership information is available to competent authorities."</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: IO.5 relates primarily to Recommendations 24 and 25 and also to elements of Recommendations 1, 10, 22, 37 and 40. (p. 143)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "This outcome relates primarily to Recommendations 24 and 25"</sub>

## Core Issues to be considered in determining if the Outcome is being achieved

- **descriptive / method**: Core issue 5.1 asks how well the country identifies, assesses and understands ML/TF risks of legal persons created in the country and foreign-created ones with sufficient links, and of legal arrangements governed under its law, administered in or with a trustee resident in the country, and foreign arrangement types with sufficient links. (p. 144)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "How well does the country identify, assess and understand its ML/TF risks associated with legal persons created in the country"</sub>
- **descriptive / method**: Core issue 5.2 asks how well the country has implemented measures to prevent, manage and mitigate misuse of legal persons and arrangements for ML/TF, including the risk from bearer shares, bearer share warrants, nominee directors and nominee shareholders. (p. 144)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "including measures to address the risk of misuse of bearer shares, bearer share warrants, nominee directors and nominee shareholders"</sub>
- **descriptive / method**: Core issue 5.3 asks to what extent competent authorities can obtain adequate, accurate and up-to-date basic and beneficial ownership information, in a timely manner, on all types of legal persons created in the country and foreign-created ones that present ML/TF risks and have sufficient links. (p. 144)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "obtain adequate, accurate and up-to-date basic and beneficial ownership information on all types of legal persons created in the country"</sub>
- **descriptive / method**: Core issue 5.4 asks to what extent authorities can obtain timely, adequate, accurate and up-to-date information on legal arrangements' basic and beneficial ownership, trustees' residence, and assets held or managed by a financial institution or DNFBP for trustees it has a relationship with, and basic information on other regulated agents and service providers such as investment advisors, accountants and tax advisors. (p. 144)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "(b) the residence of the trustees and their equivalents"</sub>
- **descriptive / method**: Core issue 5.5 asks to what extent effective, proportionate and dissuasive sanctions are applied against persons who do not comply with the information requirements. (p. 144)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "effective, proportionate and dissuasive sanctions applied against persons who do not comply with the information requirements"</sub>

## Note to Assessors:

- **recommended / method**: When assessing the IO.5 core issues, assessors should consider the ML/TF risks associated with legal persons and arrangements created in the country and with foreign-created ones that have sufficient links with the country, and whether the activities and measures taken to mitigate those risks are aligned with the identified risk. (p. 144)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "whether the activities and measures it is taking to mitigate those risks are aligned with the identified risk"</sub>

## Ordering financial institutions

- **expected / data or feature**: Criterion 16.1(a): required and accurate originator information is the originator's name; the originator account number where an account is used to process the transaction or, absent an account, a unique transaction reference number permitting traceability; and the originator's address, or national identity number, or customer identification number, or date and place of birth. (p. 68)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the originator’s address, or national identity number, or customer"</sub>
- **expected / data or feature**: Criterion 16.1(b): required beneficiary information is the beneficiary's name and the beneficiary account number where an account is used to process the transaction or, absent an account, a unique transaction reference number permitting traceability. (p. 68)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the beneficiary account number where such an account is used to process the transaction"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: Footnote 96: "accurate" describes information that has been verified for accuracy, i.e. financial institutions should be required to verify the accuracy of the required originator information. (p. 68)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "financial institutions should be required to verify the accuracy of the required originator information"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Footnote 95: the revisions in Annex IV to Recommendation 16 were adopted by the FATF but are not yet in effect; the date they take effect will be decided by the FATF later. (p. 68)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "adopted by the FATF but are not yet in effect"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Under criterion 16.1, financial institutions should be required to make sure that every cross-border wire transfer of USD/EUR 1 000 or more always carries the required and accurate originator information and the required beneficiary information. (p. 68)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "ensure that all cross-border wire transfers of USD/EUR 1 000 or more are always accompanied by the following"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Under criterion 16.1(a), the required originator information consists of the originator's name; the originator's account number where an account is used to process the transaction (or, with no account, a unique transaction reference number that permits traceability); and one of the originator's address, national identity number, customer identification number, or date and place of birth. (p. 68)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "originator account number where such an account is used to process the transaction or, in the absence"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Under criterion 16.1(b), the required beneficiary information consists of the beneficiary's name and the beneficiary's account number where an account is used to process the transaction (or, with no account, a unique transaction reference number that permits traceability). (p. 68)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "beneficiary account number where such an account is used to process the transaction or, in the absence"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Under criterion 16.2, when several individual cross-border wire transfers from one originator are grouped into a batch file for transmission to beneficiaries, the batch file should carry the required and accurate originator information plus full beneficiary information that can be fully traced within the beneficiary country. (p. 68)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the batch file should contain required and accurate originator information and full beneficiary information"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Under criterion 16.2, for batch files of bundled cross-border wire transfers, the financial institution should be required to include the originator's account number or a unique transaction reference number. (p. 68)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the financial institution should be required to include the originator"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: Footnote 96 explains that "accurate" means information that has been verified for accuracy, so financial institutions should be required to verify the accuracy of the required originator information. (p. 68)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Accurate is used to describe information that has been verified for accuracy"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 16.3: if countries apply a de minimis threshold to criterion 16.1 (no higher than USD/EUR 1 000), financial institutions should be required to ensure that cross-border wire transfers below the threshold are always accompanied by the originator's name and account number (or unique transaction reference number) and the beneficiary's name and account number (or unique transaction reference number). (p. 69)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "below any applicable de minimis threshold (no higher than USD/EUR 1 000)"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 16.4: the information in criterion 16.3 need not be verified for accuracy, but the financial institution should be required to verify the information on its customer where there is a suspicion of ML/TF. (p. 69)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "need not be verified for accuracy. However, the financial institution should be required to verify the information"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 16.5: for domestic wire transfers, the ordering financial institution should be required to ensure the accompanying information includes originator information as for cross-border wire transfers, unless it can be made available to the beneficiary financial institution and appropriate authorities by other means. (p. 69)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "includes originator information as indicated for cross-border wire transfers, unless this information can be made available"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 16.6: where information can be made available by other means, the ordering financial institution need only include the account number or a unique transaction reference number, provided it permits tracing the transaction back to the originator or beneficiary; the institution should be required to make the information available within three business days of a request from the beneficiary financial institution or competent authorities, and law enforcement authorities should be able to compel immediate production. (p. 69)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "within three business days of receiving the request either from the beneficiary financial institution or from appropriate competent authorities"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Footnote 97: the term domestic wire transfers also refers to any chain of wire transfers that takes place entirely within the borders of the European Union, and the European internal market and legal framework extends to members of the European Economic Area. (p. 69)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "any chain of wire transfers that takes place entirely within the borders of the European Union"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 16.7: the ordering financial institution should be required to maintain all originator and beneficiary information collected, in accordance with Recommendation 11. (p. 69)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "maintain all originator and beneficiary information collected, in accordance with Recommendation 11"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 16.8: the ordering financial institution should not be allowed to execute the wire transfer if it does not comply with criteria 16.1 to 16.7. (p. 69)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "should not be allowed to execute the wire transfer if it does not comply"</sub>

## Intermediary financial institutions

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 16.9: for cross-border wire transfers, an intermediary financial institution should be required to ensure that all originator and beneficiary information accompanying a wire transfer is retained with it. (p. 70)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "all originator and beneficiary information that accompanies a wire transfer is retained with it"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 16.11: intermediary financial institutions should be required to take reasonable measures, consistent with straight-through processing, to identify cross-border wire transfers lacking required originator or beneficiary information. (p. 70)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "take reasonable measures, which are consistent with straight-through processing, to identify cross-border wire transfers"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 16.12: intermediary financial institutions should be required to have risk-based policies and procedures for determining when to execute, reject or suspend a wire transfer lacking required originator or beneficiary information, and the appropriate follow-up action. (p. 70)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "when to execute, reject, or suspend a wire transfer lacking required originator or required beneficiary information"</sub>

## Beneficiary financial institutions

Footnote 98 notes that countries may adopt a de minimis threshold (no higher than USD/EUR 1 000) and may nevertheless require incoming transfers below it to contain required and accurate originator information. (p. 70)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "required to verify the identity of the beneficiary, if the identity has not been previously verified"</sub>

(p. 70)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Beneficiary financial institutions should be required to have risk-based policies and procedures for determining"</sub>

## Intermediary financial institutions

- **expected / requirement**: Under criterion 16.10, if technical limitations stop the required originator or beneficiary information from staying with a related domestic wire transfer, the intermediary financial institution should be required to retain a record for at least five years of everything it received from the ordering institution or another intermediary. (p. 70)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "keep a record, for at least five years, of all the information received from the ordering financial institution"</sub>

## Beneficiary financial institutions

- **expected / requirement**: Under criterion 16.13, beneficiary financial institutions should be required to take reasonable measures to spot cross-border wire transfers missing required originator or beneficiary information; those measures may include post-event monitoring or, where feasible, real-time monitoring. (p. 70)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "which may include post-event monitoring or real-time monitoring where feasible"</sub>

## Money or value transfer service operators

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 16.16: MVTS providers should be required to comply with all relevant requirements of Recommendation 16 in the countries where they operate, directly or through their agents. (p. 71)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "MVTS providers should be required to comply with all of the relevant requirements of Recommendation 16"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 16.17: where an MVTS provider controls both the ordering and beneficiary side of a wire transfer, it should be required to take into account all information from both sides to determine whether an STR must be filed, and to file an STR in any country affected by the suspicious wire transfer and make relevant transaction information available to the FIU. (p. 71)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "file an STR in any country affected by the suspicious wire transfer"</sub>

## Implementation of Targeted Financial Sanctions

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 16.18: countries should ensure that, when processing wire transfers, financial institutions take freezing action and comply with prohibitions on transactions with designated persons and entities, per obligations in the relevant UNSCRs on preventing and suppressing terrorism and terrorist financing, such as UNSCRs 1267 and 1373 and successors. (p. 71)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "financial institutions take freezing action and comply with prohibitions from conducting transactions with designated persons and entities"</sub>

## RECOMMENDATION 17 RELIANCE ON THIRD PARTIES

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 17.1: if financial institutions are permitted to rely on third-party financial institutions and DNFBPs to perform elements (a)-(c) of Recommendation 10 CDD (customer identification, beneficial owner identification, understanding the nature of the business) or to introduce business, ultimate responsibility for CDD remains with the relying financial institution. (p. 72)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the ultimate responsibility for CDD measures should remain with the financial institution relying on the third party"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 17.2: in determining in which countries a qualifying third party can be based, countries should have regard to information available on the level of country risk. (p. 72)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "should have regard to information available on the level of country risk"</sub>
- **permissive / requirement**: Criterion 17.3: for financial institutions relying on a third party in the same financial group, relevant competent authorities may also consider the criteria met where the group applies CDD and record-keeping requirements in line with Recommendations 10 to 12 and AML/CFT programmes under Recommendation 18; implementation is supervised at group level by a competent authority; and any higher country risk is adequately mitigated by the group's AML/CFT policies. (p. 72)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "relevant competent authorities100 may also consider that the requirements of the criteria above are met"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Footnotes 99 and 100: Recommendation 17 does not apply to outsourcing or agency relationships (paragraph 1 of INR.17); "relevant competent authorities" means the home authority (for group-wide policies and controls) and the host authorities (for branches and subsidiaries). (p. 72)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "This Recommendation does not apply to outsourcing or agency relationships"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Under criterion 17.1(b), the relying financial institution should be required to take steps to be satisfied that copies of identification data and other relevant CDD documentation will be available from the third party on request, without delay. (p. 72)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "take steps to satisfy itself that copies of identification data and other relevant documentation"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Under criterion 17.1(c), the relying financial institution should be required to be satisfied that the third party is regulated and is supervised or monitored for CDD and record-keeping requirements in line with Recommendations 10 and 11, and has measures in place to comply with them. (p. 72)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "satisfy itself that the third party is regulated, and supervised or monitored for"</sub>

## RECOMMENDATION 18 INTERNAL CONTROLS AND FOREIGN BRANCHES AND SUBSIDIARIES

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 18.1: financial institutions should be required to implement ML/TF programmes having regard to ML/TF risks and the size of the business, including compliance management arrangements (including appointing a compliance officer at management level), screening procedures for high hiring standards, an ongoing employee training programme, and an independent audit function to test the system. (p. 73)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "an independent audit function to test the system"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Footnotes 101 and 102: information shared under criterion 18.2(b) could include an STR, its underlying information, or the fact that an STR has been submitted; countries may determine the scope and extent of information shared based on its sensitivity and relevance to AML/CFT risk management. (p. 73)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "This could include an STR, its underlying information, or the fact than an STR has been submitted."</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Under criterion 18.2, financial groups should be required to implement group-wide ML/TF programmes that are applicable and appropriate to all branches and majority-owned subsidiaries, including the criterion 18.1 measures and policies and procedures for sharing information needed for CDD and ML/TF risk management. (p. 73)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "policies and procedures for sharing information required for the purposes of CDD and ML/TF risk management"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Under criterion 18.2(b), group-level compliance, audit and/or AML/CFT functions should be provided with customer, account and transaction information from branches and subsidiaries when needed for AML/CFT purposes, including information and analysis of unusual transactions or activities if such analysis was done; branches and subsidiaries should in turn receive such information from the group-level functions when it is relevant and appropriate to risk management. (p. 73)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "branches and subsidiaries should receive such information from these group-level functions when relevant and appropriate to risk management"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Under criterion 18.2(c), group-wide programmes should include adequate safeguards on the confidentiality and use of information exchanged, including safeguards to prevent tipping-off. (p. 73)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "adequate safeguards on the confidentiality and use of information exchanged, including safeguards to prevent tipping-off"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 18.3: financial institutions should be required to ensure that foreign branches and majority-owned subsidiaries apply AML/CFT measures consistent with home country requirements where host country minimum requirements are less strict, to the extent host country laws and regulations permit; if the host country does not permit proper implementation, financial groups should be required to apply appropriate additional measures to manage ML/TF risks and inform their home supervisors. (p. 74)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "financial groups should be required to apply appropriate additional measures to manage the ML/TF risks and inform their home supervisors"</sub>

## RECOMMENDATION 19 HIGHER RISK COUNTRIES

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 19.2: countries should be able to apply countermeasures proportionate to the risks, both when called upon to do so by the FATF and independently of any FATF call. (p. 75)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Countries should be able to apply countermeasures proportionate to the risks"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 19.3: countries should have measures in place to ensure financial institutions are advised of concerns about weaknesses in the AML/CFT systems of other countries. (p. 75)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "financial institutions are advised of concerns about weaknesses in the AML/CFT systems of other countries"</sub>

## RECOMMENDATION 20 REPORTING OF SUSPICIOUS TRANSACTIONS

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 20.1: if a financial institution suspects or has reasonable grounds to suspect that funds are the proceeds of criminal activity or are related to TF, it should be required to report its suspicions promptly to the Financial Intelligence Unit. (p. 76)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "it should be required to report promptly its suspicions to the Financial Intelligence Unit"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 20.2: financial institutions should be required to report all suspicious transactions, including attempted transactions, regardless of the amount of the transaction. (p. 76)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "report all suspicious transactions, including attempted transactions, regardless of the amount of the transaction"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Footnotes 103 and 104: the requirement that financial institutions report suspicious transactions should be set out in law; criminal activity means all criminal acts that would constitute a predicate offence for ML in the country, or at a minimum those offences that would be predicate offences as required by R.3. (p. 76)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "should be set out in law"</sub>

## RECOMMENDATION 21 TIPPING-OFF AND CONFIDENTIALITY

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 21.1: financial institutions and their directors, officers and employees should be protected by law from criminal and civil liability for breach of any disclosure restriction imposed by contract or legislative, regulatory or administrative provision if they report suspicions in good faith to the FIU; this protection should apply even if they did not know precisely what the underlying criminal activity was and regardless of whether illegal activity actually occurred. (p. 77)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "regardless of whether illegal activity actually occurred"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 21.2: financial institutions and their directors, officers and employees should be prohibited by law from disclosing that an STR or related information is being filed with the FIU; these provisions are not intended to inhibit information sharing under Recommendation 18. (p. 77)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "prohibited by law from disclosing the fact that an STR or related information is being filed"</sub>

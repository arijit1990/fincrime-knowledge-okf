## Chapter 2. International Co-operation

- **descriptive / context**: Chapter 2 of the MER template assesses Immediate Outcome 2 (IO.2). The Recommendations relevant to the effectiveness assessment are R.36-40 and elements of R.9, 15, 24, 25 and 32. (p. 215)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The relevant Immediate Outcome considered and assessed in this chapter is IO.2."</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: In the IO.2 chapter, Key Findings should briefly summarise assessors' conclusions and highlight the most significant findings, and should be consistent in substance with the key recommended actions without strictly mirroring them. (p. 216)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "highlighting the most significant findings. Key findings and key recommended actions should be consistent on the substance"</sub>

## Key Recommended Actions (KRA)

- **expected / expectation**: Assessors should briefly list the main corrective actions the country needs to improve effectiveness and technical compliance, and clearly indicate which IO or Recommendation each action relates to. (p. 216)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Assessors should briefly list the main corrective actions required for the country"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: KRAs are noted separately from Other Recommended Actions and only relate to Immediate Outcomes rated ME or LE, or to Recommendations rated PC or NC where these relate to an IO rated ME or LE. If IO.2 is rated HE or SE, the KRA section is deleted and all recommended actions for the chapter go under Other Recommended Actions. (p. 216)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "If IO.2 is rated HE or SE, delete this section and reflect all recommended actions for this chapter"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: There should not normally be more than 2-3 KRAs per Immediate Outcome, including KRAs for technical compliance for the Recommendations related to that IO. KRA numbering should correspond to the numbering used in the KRA Roadmap. (p. 216)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "There should not normally be more than 2-3 KRAs per Immediate Outcome,"</sub>

## Chapter 2. International Co-operation

- **expected / expectation**: For the Overall Conclusions on IO.2, weighting and conclusion follow the instructions given for IO.1. (p. 217)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "[Weighting and conclusion: See IO.1 for instructions]"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The IO.2 template analysis is organised as: 2.1 providing constructive, timely and quality mutual legal assistance and extradition (providing evidence and locating criminals, extradition, facilitating asset recovery); 2.2 seeking appropriate and timely mutual legal assistance and extradition (seeking evidence and locating criminals, extradition, seeking to facilitate asset recovery); 2.3 seeking other forms of international cooperation for AML/CFT purposes, including asset recovery; and 2.4 providing other forms of international cooperation for AML/CFT purposes, including asset recovery. (p. 217)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "2.1. Providing constructive, timely and quality mutual legal assistance and extradition"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Under core issues 2.3 and 2.4, the template sub-divides other forms of international cooperation by counterpart: the FIU, law enforcement agencies (LEAs), supervisors of FIs, VASPs and DNFBPs, and customs and tax authorities. (p. 218)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "2.3.3. Supervisors of FIs, VASPs and DNFBPs"</sub>

## Chapter 3 (IO.3)

- **descriptive / context**: Chapter 3 of the MER template assesses Immediate Outcome 3 (IO.3). The Recommendations relevant to the effectiveness assessment are R.9-21, 26, 27, 34 and 35 and elements of R.1, 29 and 40. (p. 218)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The Recommendations relevant for the assessment of effectiveness under this chapter are R.9- 21, 26, 27, 34 and 35"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: When assessing effectiveness under IO.3, assessors should take into consideration the risk, context and materiality of the country, and should explain these factors in Chapter One of the MER under the heading Financial Institutions and VASPs, as required by the Methodology instructions under that heading. (p. 219)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "assessors should take into consideration the risk, context and materiality of the country being assessed"</sub>

## Key Recommended Actions (KRA)

- **expected / expectation**: For IO.3, the KRA section is deleted and all recommended actions go under Other Recommended Actions only if IO.3 is rated HE or SE and R.10, 11 and 20 are all rated LC or C. (p. 219)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "If IO.3 is rated HE or SE, and R.10, 11 and 20 are all rated LC or C,"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: For IO.3 in the MER template, there should not normally be more than 2-3 KRAs per Immediate Outcome, counting KRAs for technical compliance on the Recommendations related to that IO. (p. 219)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "There should not normally be more than 2-3 KRAs per Immediate Outcome,"</sub>
- **permissive / expectation**: In addition to the 2-3 KRAs, there may be a separate KRA for each of R.10, R.11 and R.20 rated NC or PC, whatever the IO.3 rating is. (p. 219)[^original]

## Overall Conclusions on IO.3

- **expected / expectation**: For IO.3, the weighting and conclusion follow the IO.1 instructions. The first paragraph of the conclusion should briefly summarise the relative importance assessors gave to the different types of financial institutions and VASPs, taking into account risk, context and materiality, with a cross-reference to the more detailed sector weighting in Chapter One.   <sub>anchor: "what relative importance assessors have given to the different types of financial institutions and VASPs"</sub>

## Chapter 3 (IO.3)

- **descriptive / context**: The IO.3 template analysis is organised as: 3.1 licensing, registration and controls for FIs and VASPs preventing criminals and associates from entering the market (market entry controls; detecting and addressing breaches); 3.2 supervisors identifying, understanding and promoting FI and VASP understanding of ML/TF risks and AML/CFT obligations; 3.3 FI and VASP understanding of existing and evolving ML/TF risks; 3.4 FI and VASP understanding and compliance with AML/CFT obligations and mitigating measures; 3.5 supervisors' risk-based monitoring or supervision of FI and VASP compliance; and 3.6 the impact of monitoring, supervision, outreach, remedial actions and effective, proportionate and dissuasive sanctions on compliance. (p. 220)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "3.1. Licensing, registration and controls for FIs and VASPs preventing criminals and associates from entering the market"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Core issue 3.4 in the IO.3 template is sub-divided into: CDD, record-keeping, beneficial ownership information and ongoing monitoring; enhanced or specific measures; AML/CFT reporting obligations and tipping off; internal controls, procedures and audit to ensure compliance; and legal or regulatory impediments to implementing AML/CFT obligations and mitigating measures. (p. 221)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "3.4.5. Legal or regulatory impediments to implementing AML/CFT obligations and mitigating measures"</sub>

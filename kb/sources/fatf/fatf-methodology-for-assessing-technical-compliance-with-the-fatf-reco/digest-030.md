## Annex C. TECHNICAL COMPLIANCE ANNEX

- **expected / expectation**: The Technical Compliance Annex of the MER gives detailed analysis of compliance with the FATF 40 Recommendations in numerical order. It does not include descriptive text on the country's situation or risks, is limited to analysis of the technical criteria for each Recommendation, and should be read together with the MER. (p. 248)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "does not include descriptive text on the country situation or risks and is limited to the analysis of technical criteria"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The technical compliance annex covers areas where the country has made legal, regulatory or operational framework changes since its last mutual evaluation (or follow-up reports with technical compliance re-ratings), and areas where the FATF Standards have changed and the country has not previously been assessed. Reassessed areas are identified in green. (p. 249)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "areas where there has been a change in the FATF Standards for which the country has not previously been assessed"</sub>

## Recommendation 1 – Assessing risks and applying a risk-based approach

- **expected / expectation**: For each Recommendation, an opening paragraph should set out the rating in the previous MER and main deficiencies, any follow-up conclusions on whether deficiencies have been addressed, an indication of new FATF requirements (relative to the 2013 Methodology) against which the country has not yet been assessed, and the main changes to relevant laws, regulations and other elements since the last assessment. (p. 249)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "For each Recommendation, an opening paragraph should set out:"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: For criteria where the most recent assessment is accurate and up to date, assessors should retain the previous analysis (from publicly available MERs, or follow-up reports with technical compliance re-ratings, that were analysed and adopted by an assessment body). Where they consider the previous analysis inaccurate or out of date, assessors may update or clarify the text. (p. 249)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "assessors should retain the previous analysis"</sub>

## Recommendation 1 - Assessing risks and applying a risk-based approach

- **expected / expectation**: In the MER template, the opening paragraph for each Recommendation should set out four things: the rating in the previous MER, where applicable, and the main deficiencies identified; any follow-up conclusions on whether the country has addressed its deficiencies; an indication that there are new FATF requirements, relative to the 2013 Methodology, against which the country has not yet been assessed; and the main changes to relevant laws, regulations and other elements in the country since its last assessment. (p. 249)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "For each Recommendation, an opening paragraph should set out:"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: All countries should be evaluated on the basis of the FATF Standards and Methodology as they stand on the date the country's technical compliance submission is due. (p. 249)[^original]

## Recommendation 2 - National Co-operation and Co-ordination

- **expected / expectation**: For other criteria, assessors select the applicable criterion rating and insert their analysis of whether the criterion is met only. General descriptions of the country's situation, context or legal and institutional framework belong in the main report, though they may be cross-referenced. (p. 250)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Assessors should include only their analysis of whether the criterion is met."</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Assessors may devote more space to complex criteria or those applying to several sectors, possibly in table form, but the overall length of the technical annex should normally be limited to a maximum of 60 pages. (p. 250)[^original]

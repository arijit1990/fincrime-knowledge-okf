## RECOMMENDATION 12 POLITICALLY EXPOSED PERSONS (PEPS)

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 12.1: for foreign PEPs, in addition to Recommendation 10 CDD, financial institutions should be required to put in place risk management systems to determine whether a customer or beneficial owner is a PEP; obtain senior management approval before establishing (or continuing, for existing customers) such relationships; take reasonable measures to establish the source of wealth and source of funds of customers and beneficial owners identified as PEPs; and conduct enhanced ongoing monitoring of the relationship. (p. 61)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "obtain senior management approval before establishing (or continuing, for existing customers) such business relationships"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 12.2: for domestic PEPs or persons entrusted with a prominent function by an international organisation, in addition to Recommendation 10 CDD, financial institutions should be required to take reasonable measures to determine whether a customer or beneficial owner is such a person and, where the business relationship is higher risk, to adopt the measures in criterion 12.1 (b) to (d). (p. 61)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "higher risk business relationship with such a person, adopt the measures in criterion 12.1 (b) to (d)"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Under criterion 12.3, financial institutions should be required to apply the relevant requirements of criteria 12.1 and 12.2 also to family members and close associates of every type of PEP. (p. 61)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "apply the relevant requirements of criteria 12.1 and 12.2 to family members or close associates of all types of PEP"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Under criterion 12.4, for life insurance policies, financial institutions should be required to take reasonable measures to determine whether the beneficiaries, and where required the beneficial owner of the beneficiary, are PEPs, and this should happen at the latest at the time of the payout. (p. 61)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "take reasonable measures to determine whether the beneficiaries and/or, where required, the beneficial owner"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Under criterion 12.4, where higher risks are identified for a life insurance policy, financial institutions should be required to tell senior management before the policy proceeds are paid out, to carry out enhanced scrutiny of the entire business relationship with the policyholder, and to consider making a suspicious transaction report. (p. 61)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "inform senior management before the payout of the policy proceeds, to conduct enhanced scrutiny on the whole business relationship"</sub>

## RECOMMENDATION 13 CORRESPONDENT BANKING

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 13.1(a) and (b): for cross-border correspondent banking and similar relationships, financial institutions should be required to gather sufficient information about a respondent institution to understand fully its business and to determine from public information its reputation and quality of supervision, including whether it has been subject to an ML/TF investigation or regulatory action; and to assess the respondent's AML/CFT controls. (p. 62)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "including whether it has been subject to a ML/TF investigation or regulatory action"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 13.1(c) and (d): financial institutions should be required to obtain senior management approval before establishing new correspondent relationships, and to clearly understand each institution's respective AML/CFT responsibilities. (p. 62)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "obtain approval from senior management before establishing new correspondent relationships"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 13.2: for payable-through accounts, financial institutions should be required to satisfy themselves that the respondent bank has performed CDD on customers with direct access to the correspondent bank's accounts and can provide relevant CDD information on request. (p. 62)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "has performed CDD obligations on its customers that have direct access to the accounts of the correspondent bank"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 13.3: financial institutions should be prohibited from entering into or continuing correspondent banking relationships with shell banks, and should be required to satisfy themselves that respondent financial institutions do not permit their accounts to be used by shell banks. (p. 62)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "prohibited from entering into, or continuing, correspondent banking relationships with shell banks"</sub>

## RECOMMENDATION 14 MONEY OR VALUE TRANSFER SERVICES (MVTS)

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 14.1: natural or legal persons that provide MVTS should be required to be licensed or registered. Footnote 79 notes that countries need not impose a separate licensing or registration system on licensed or registered financial institutions that are authorised to perform MVTS. (p. 63)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Natural or legal persons that provide MVTS (MVTS providers) should be required to be licensed or registered."</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 14.2: countries should take action to identify natural or legal persons carrying out MVTS without a licence or registration and apply proportionate and dissuasive sanctions to them. (p. 63)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "identifying natural or legal persons that carry out MVTS without a licence or registration"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 14.3: MVTS providers should be subject to monitoring for AML/CFT compliance. (p. 63)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "MVTS providers should be subject to monitoring for AML/CFT compliance."</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 14.5: MVTS providers that use agents should be required to include them in their AML/CFT programmes and monitor them for compliance with those programmes. (p. 63)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "include them in their AML/CFT programmes and monitor them for compliance with these programmes"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Under criterion 14.4, MVTS providers' agents should be required to be licensed or registered by a competent authority; the alternative is that the MVTS provider is required to keep an up-to-date list of its agents that competent authorities can access in the countries where the provider and its agents operate. (p. 63)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "maintain a current list of its agents accessible by competent authorities"</sub>

## RECOMMENDATION 15 NEW TECHNOLOGIES

- **expected / expectation**: The Note to Assessors for Recommendation 15 says countries should consider virtual assets as property, proceeds, funds, funds or other assets, or other corresponding value; the words "virtual assets" need not appear in legislation using those terms, but assessors should satisfy themselves that nothing in legislation or case law precludes virtual assets falling within those definitions, and should note a deficiency in the relevant Recommendation where the terms do not cover virtual assets. (p. 64)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "countries should consider virtual assets as property, proceeds, funds, funds or other assets, or other corresponding value"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Assessors should also satisfy themselves that VASPs may be considered existing sources of information on beneficial ownership for criteria 24.6(b)(iii) and 25.10, and are empowered to obtain relevant information from trustees for criteria 25.7(a) and 25.7(c). Footnote 81 says this is meant to ensure availability of beneficial ownership information and the criteria should not be considered to impose obligations on VASPs. (p. 64)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Assessors should not consider these criteria to impose obligations on VASPs."</sub>

## Virtual assets and virtual asset service providers

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 15.3: in accordance with Recommendation 1, countries should identify and assess the ML, TF and proliferation financing risks from virtual asset activities and VASP operations; based on that understanding apply a risk-based approach so that ML/TF preventive measures are proportionate, and implement proportionate risk-based measures to mitigate PF risks; and require VASPs to identify, assess, manage and mitigate their ML, TF and PF risks as required by criteria 1.12, 1.13 and 1.14. (p. 65)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "require VASPs to take appropriate steps to identify, assess, manage and mitigate their money laundering,"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: Footnote 83: for criterion 15.3, proliferation financing risk refers strictly and only to the potential breach, non-implementation or evasion of the targeted financial sanctions obligations in R.7. (p. 65)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "strictly and only to the potential breach, non-implementation or evasion of the targeted financial sanctions obligations"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 15.4(a): countries should ensure VASPs are required to be licensed or registered at a minimum in the jurisdiction(s) where they are created when the VASP is a legal person, and in the jurisdiction where the place of business is located when the VASP is a natural person. (p. 65)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "VASPs are required to be licensed or registered84 at a minimum:85"</sub>
- **permissive / context**: Footnote 84: a country need not impose a separate licensing or registration system on persons already licensed or registered as financial institutions that may perform VASP activities and are subject to the full range of FATF obligations. Footnote 85: jurisdictions may also require VASPs that offer products or services to customers in, or operate from, their jurisdiction to be licensed or registered there. (p. 65)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Jurisdictions may also require VASPs that offer products and/or services to customers in"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Under criterion 15.4(a)(i), countries should ensure that VASPs that are legal persons are required to be licensed or registered, at a minimum, in the jurisdiction(s) where they are created. (p. 65)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "when the VASP is a legal person, in the jurisdiction(s) where it is created"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 15.4(b): competent authorities should take necessary legal or regulatory measures to prevent criminals or their associates from holding, or being the beneficial owner of, a significant or controlling interest in a VASP, or holding a management function in it. (p. 66)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "prevent criminals or their associates from holding, or being the beneficial owner of, a significant or controlling interest"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Footnotes 86 and 87 clarify that criterion 15.4(a) requires a country to ensure that a VASP created in the country (or, for natural persons, located there) is licensed or registered there, not that a VASP licensed or registered in the country is also registered in any third country where it was created or has a place of business. (p. 66)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "not that any VASP licenced or registered in the country is also registered in any third country"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 15.5: countries should take action to identify natural or legal persons carrying out VASP activities without the requisite licence or registration and apply appropriate sanctions to them. Footnote 88 says this criterion applies to all countries, regardless of whether they have chosen to license, register or prohibit virtual assets or VASPs. (p. 66)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "carry out VASP activities without the requisite license or registration and apply appropriate sanctions to them"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 15.6: consistent with Recommendations 26 and 27, countries should ensure VASPs are subject to adequate regulation and risk-based supervision or monitoring by a competent authority (which, per footnote 89, cannot include an SRB), including systems to ensure compliance with national AML/CFT requirements; and that supervisors have adequate powers to supervise, ensure compliance, conduct inspections, compel production of information and impose a range of disciplinary and financial sanctions, including withdrawing, restricting or suspending a VASP's licence or registration where applicable. (p. 66)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "including the power to withdraw, restrict or suspend the VASP’s license or registration, where applicable"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 15.7: in line with Recommendation 34, competent authorities and supervisors should establish guidelines and provide feedback to assist VASPs in applying national AML/CFT measures, in particular in detecting and reporting suspicious transactions. (p. 66)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "establish guidelines and provide feedback, which will assist VASPs in applying national measures to combat money laundering"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 15.8: in line with Recommendation 35, countries should ensure a range of proportionate and dissuasive criminal, civil or administrative sanctions is available for VASPs failing to comply with AML/CFT requirements, and that sanctions apply not only to VASPs but also to their directors and senior management. (p. 66)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "sanctions should be applicable not only to VASPs, but also to their directors and senior management"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Under criterion 15.4(a)(ii), countries should ensure that VASPs that are natural persons are required to be licensed or registered, at a minimum, in the jurisdiction where their place of business is located. (p. 66)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "when the VASP is a natural person, in the jurisdiction where its place of business is located"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 15.9: VASPs should be required to comply with Recommendations 10 to 21, subject to qualifications. Under R.10, the occasional-transaction designated threshold above which VASPs must conduct CDD is USD/EUR 1 000. (p. 67)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The occasional transactions designated threshold above which VASPs are required to conduct CDD is USD/EUR 1 000."</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 15.9(b)(i) and (ii) (R.16 for virtual asset transfers): originating VASPs should obtain and hold required and accurate originator information and required beneficiary information, submit it immediately and securely to the beneficiary VASP or financial institution (if any), and make it available on request to appropriate authorities; beneficiary VASPs should obtain and hold required originator information and required and accurate beneficiary information and make it available on request to appropriate authorities. (p. 67)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "submit92 the above information to the beneficiary VASP or financial institution (if any) immediately and securely"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 15.9(b)(iii) and (iv): the other requirements of R.16, including monitoring availability of information and taking freezing action and prohibiting transactions with designated persons and entities, apply to VASPs on the same basis, and the same obligations apply to financial institutions when sending or receiving virtual asset transfers on behalf of a customer. (p. 67)[^original]

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 15.11: countries should rapidly provide the widest possible range of international co-operation on money laundering, predicate offences and terrorist financing relating to virtual assets on the basis of Recommendations 37 to 40; in particular, VASP supervisors should have a legal basis for exchanging information with foreign counterparts regardless of the supervisors' nature or status and differences in the nomenclature or status of VASPs. (p. 67)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "supervisors of VASPs should have a legal basis for exchanging information with their foreign counterparts"</sub>

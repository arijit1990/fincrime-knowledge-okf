## RECOMMENDATION 8 NON-PROFIT ORGANISATIONS (NPOS)

- **expected / expectation**: The Note to Assessors for Recommendation 8 says assessors should consider, when assessing criteria 8.2 to 8.4, whether the elements apply without unduly disrupting or discouraging legitimate NPO activities. (p. 51)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "without unduly disrupting or discouraging legitimate NPO activities"</sub>

## Taking a risk-based approach

- **expected / requirement**: Criterion 8.1: because not all not-for-profit organisations are inherently high risk, and without prejudice to Recommendation 1, countries should identify which subset of organisations falls within the FATF definition of NPO, conduct a risk assessment of those NPOs to identify the nature of TF risks posed to them, and have in place focused, proportionate and risk-based measures to address the identified TF risks. (p. 51)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "identify which subset of organisations fall within the FATF definition"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Footnote 62 states that NPOs are at varying degrees of risk of TF abuse by virtue of their types, activities or characteristics, and that the majority may represent low risk. (p. 51)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the majority may represent low risk"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Footnote 63: the exercises under 8.1(a) to (c) should use all relevant and reliable sources of information, including engagement with NPOs, and should be reviewed periodically; they could take a variety of forms and may or may not be a written product. Relevant sources may include information from regulators, tax authorities, FIUs, donor organisations, or law enforcement and intelligence authorities. (p. 51)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "should use all relevant and reliable sources of information, including through engagement with NPOs"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: For Recommendation 8, an NPO is a legal person, arrangement or organisation that primarily engages in raising or disbursing funds for purposes such as charitable, religious, cultural, educational, social or fraternal purposes, or for other types of "good works". (p. 51)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "primarily engages in raising or disbursing funds for purposes such as charitable, religious, cultural, educational, social or fraternal purposes"</sub>
- **permissive / recommendation**: Footnote 65: countries may consider, where they exist, self-regulatory measures and related internal control measures within NPOs for criterion 8.1(c); where they do, assessors should take them into account when considering whether 8.1(c) is satisfied. (p. 51)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Countries may consider, where they exist, any self-regulatory measures and related internal control measures in place within NPOs"</sub>

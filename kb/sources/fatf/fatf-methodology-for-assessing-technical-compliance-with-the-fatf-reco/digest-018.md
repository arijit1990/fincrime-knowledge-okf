## Immediate Outcome 4

- **descriptive / context**: Immediate Outcome 4 states the intended outcome that supervisors appropriately supervise, monitor and regulate DNFBPs for AML/CFT compliance, and that DNFBPs adequately apply preventive measures proportionate to risks and report suspicious transactions. (p. 138)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "appropriately supervise, monitor and regulate DNFBPs for compliance with AML/CFT requirements"</sub>

## Characteristics of an effective system

- **descriptive / context**: In an effective IO.4 system, risk based supervision identifies, assesses and mitigates ML/TF risks in DNFBPs by preventing criminals and their associates from holding or being beneficial owners of significant or controlling interests or management functions in DNFBPs, and by guiding, monitoring and enforcing compliance, with risk-based measures where issues are found. (p. 138)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "guiding, monitoring and enforcing compliance by DNFBPs to ensure that they have"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: In an effective IO.4 system, DNFBPs understand their ML/TF risks, apply risk-based policies (including group-wide policies as appropriate), internal controls and programmes, apply CDD including for beneficial owners, conduct ongoing monitoring, and detect and report suspicious transactions, ultimately reducing ML/TF activity within these entities. (p. 138)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "develop and apply risk-based AML/CFT policies (including group-wide policies as appropriate)"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: IO.4 relates primarily to Recommendations 22, 23, 28, 34 and 35 and elements of Recommendations 1, 29 and 40. (p. 138)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "This outcome relates primarily to Recommendations 22, 23, 28, 34 and 35"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: For IO.4, a footnote says the reference to supervisors for DNFBP supervision, monitoring and regulation should be interpreted in accordance with the FATF Glossary. (p. 138)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the reference to supervisors should be interpreted in accordance with the FATF Glossary"</sub>

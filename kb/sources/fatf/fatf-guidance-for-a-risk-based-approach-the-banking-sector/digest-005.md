## ANNEX 2

- **descriptive / context**: Annex 2 lists the Basel Core Principles designated by the FATF as relevant to AML/CFT supervision under R. 26: Principles 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 26 and 29. (p. 47)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "BASEL CORE PRINCIPLES DESIGNATED BY THE FATF AS BEING RELEVANT TO AML/CFT SUPERVISION (R. 26)"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Basel Core Principle 1 (Responsibilities, objectives and powers): each supervisory authority needs clear responsibilities and objectives and a legal framework giving powers to authorise banks, conduct ongoing supervision, address legal compliance and take timely corrective action. (p. 47)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "undertake timely corrective actions to address safety andsoundness concerns."</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Basel Core Principle 2: the supervisor has operational independence, transparent processes, sound governance, autonomy-preserving budgets, adequate resources, accountability, and legal protection. (p. 47)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The legalframework for banking supervision includes legal protection for thesupervisor."</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Basel Core Principle 5 (Licensing criteria): the licensing authority can set criteria and reject applications; licensing at minimum assesses ownership and governance (including fitness and propriety of Board and senior management), strategic and operating plan, internal controls, risk management and projected financial condition; a foreign parent bank's home supervisor must give prior consent. (p. 47)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Where the proposed owner or parent organisation is a foreignbank, the prior consent of its home supervisor is obtained."</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Basel Core Principle 6: the supervisor can review, reject and impose prudential conditions on proposals to transfer significant ownership or controlling interests, held directly or indirectly, in existing banks. (p. 48)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The supervisor has the power toreview, reject and impose prudential conditions"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Basel Core Principle 8 (Supervisory approach): the supervisor maintains a forward-looking risk profile assessment of banks proportionate to systemic importance, addresses system-wide risks, has an early intervention framework and plans for orderly resolution of non-viable banks. (p. 48)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "have a framework in place for early intervention"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Basel Core Principle 9: the supervisor uses an appropriate range of techniques and tools and deploys resources proportionately to banks' risk profile and systemic importance. (p. 48)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "deploys supervisory resources on a proportionate basis"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Basel Core Principle 11: the supervisor acts early on unsafe and unsound practices, has adequate tools for timely corrective action, including revoking or recommending revocation of the banking licence. (p. 48)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "This includes the ability to revoke thebanking licence or to recommend its revocation."</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Basel Core Principle 12: the supervisor supervises the banking group on a consolidated basis, monitoring and applying prudential standards to all its business worldwide. (p. 48)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "supervises the banking group on a consolidatedbasis"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Basel Core Principle 13 (Home-host relationships): home and host supervisors of cross-border groups share information and cooperate, and local operations of foreign banks must meet the same standards as domestic banks. (p. 49)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Supervisors require the local operations of foreignbanks to be conducted to the same standards"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Basel Core Principle 14: banks and groups must have robust corporate governance covering strategic direction, structure, control environment, Board and senior management responsibilities and compensation, commensurate with risk profile and systemic importance. (p. 49)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "have robust corporate governance policies andprocesses"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Basel Core Principle 15: banks need a comprehensive risk management process with Board and senior management oversight to identify, measure, evaluate, monitor, report and control all material risks, assess capital and liquidity adequacy, and maintain contingency arrangements including recovery plans where warranted. (p. 49)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "including robust and credible recovery plans wherewarranted"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Basel Core Principle 26 (Internal control and audit): banks need adequate internal control frameworks including clear delegation of authority, separation of committing, paying and accounting functions, reconciliation, safeguarding assets, and independent internal audit and compliance functions. (p. 49)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "safeguarding the bank’s assets; and appropriateindependent internal audit and compliance functions"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Basel Core Principle 29 (Abuse of financial services): banks must have adequate policies and processes, including strict customer due diligence rules, to promote high ethical and professional standards and prevent the bank being used, intentionally or unintentionally, for criminal activities. (p. 49)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "including strict customer duediligence rules to promote high ethical and professional standards"</sub>

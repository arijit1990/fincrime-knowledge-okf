## Glossary of specific terms used in this Recommendation

- **descriptive / definition**: In the R.16 glossary, 'Accurate' describes information that has been verified for accuracy. (p. 21)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "is used to describe information that has been verified for accuracy."</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: In the R.16 glossary, 'Address' means the physical location of a residence or business. (p. 21)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "refers to the physical location of a residence or business."</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: A 'Batch transfer' under R.16 is a transfer made up of several individual payments or value transfers sent to the same financial institutions, which may or may not ultimately be intended for different persons. (p. 21)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "but may/may not be ultimately intendedfor different persons."</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: The 'Beneficiary' under R.16 is the natural or legal person or legal arrangement identified by the originator as the receiver of the requested payment or value transfer in a chain of payments or value transfers. (p. 21)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "refers to the natural or legal person or legal arrangement who is identified by theoriginator"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: The 'Beneficiary Financial Institution' is the FI that services the beneficiary's account or remits cash to the beneficiary, and is the end point of the payment chain. (p. 21)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The beneficiary financial institution is the end point in a paymentchain."</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: A BIC is a universal business identifier code based on the ISO 9362 standard assigned to financial and non-financial institutions; 'connected BICs' are those used by FIs, for instance to access the SWIFT network. (p. 21)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Connected BICs are those used by financial institutions, for instance to access theSWIFT network."</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: A 'Cover Payment' combines a payment message sent directly by the ordering FI to the beneficiary FI with routing of the funding instruction (the cover) between them through one or more intermediary FIs. (p. 21)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the routing of the funding instruction (the cover) from the ordering financial institutionto the beneficiary"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: A 'cross-border payment or value transfer' is one where the ordering FI and beneficiary FI are in different countries, and also any chain of payments or value transfers in which at least one involved FI is in a different country. (p. 21)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "where the ordering financial institutionand beneficiary financial institution are located in different countries."</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: A 'domestic payment or value transfer' is one where ordering and beneficiary FIs are in the same country, covering chains entirely within one country even if the messaging system is located elsewhere, and chains entirely within the European Economic Area (EEA). (p. 21)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "even though the system usedto transfer the payment message may be located in another country."</sub>
- **permissive / context**: Footnote 61: an entity may petition the FATF to be designated as a supra-national jurisdiction solely for assessing Recommendation 16 compliance. (p. 21)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "An entity may petition the FATF to be designated as a supra-national jurisdiction"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: An 'Intermediary financial institution' is an FI in a payment chain that receives and transmits a payment or value transfer on behalf of the ordering FI and beneficiary FI, or another intermediary FI. (p. 21)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "payment chain that receives andtransmits a"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: A 'Legal Entity Identifier' is a unique alphanumeric reference code based on the ISO 17442 standard assigned to an entity by the Global LEI System. (p. 21)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "refers to a unique alphanumeric reference code based on the ISO 17442 standardassigned"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: An 'MVTS network' is either or both of: an MVTS and its agents, or two or more MVTS bound by agreements to carry out payments or value transfers, including but not limited to net settlement of those transfers. (p. 21)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "two or more MVTS bound by one or several agreements to proceedto payments or value transfers"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: The 'Ordering financial institution' is the FI that receives the originator's instructions to transfer funds to the beneficiary, and is the start point of the payment chain. (p. 21)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The ordering financial institution is the start pointof the payment chain"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: The 'Originator' is the account holder who requests the payment or value transfer from that account, or where there is no account, the natural or legal person placing the order with the ordering FI. (p. 22)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "where there is no account, the natural or legal person thatplaces the order"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The revised R.16 glossary deletes the former definitions of 'Qualifying wire transfers' and 'Required originator and/or beneficiary information'. (p. 22)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "means a cross-border wire transfer above any applicable threshold as described in"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: 'Straight-through processing' means payment transactions conducted electronically without manual intervention. (p. 22)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "refers to payment transactions that are conducted electronically without the need formanual intervention."</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: A 'Unique official identifier' is a public-sector-issued identification scheme in the relevant jurisdiction ensuring each identifier refers to a unique person, entity or legal arrangement and each has only one identifier in the scheme. (p. 22)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "refers to an identification scheme that is issued by the public sector in the relevantjurisdiction"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: A 'Unique transaction reference number' is a combination of letters, numbers or symbols set by the payment service provider according to the protocols of the payment and settlement or messaging system used. (p. 22)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "refers to a combination of letters, numbers or symbols, determined by the paymentservice provider"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: A 'Payment or value transfer' is any transaction carried out electronically on behalf of an originator through an ordering FI to make funds available to a beneficiary at a beneficiary FI, whether or not originator and beneficiary are the same; it includes cash withdrawals. (p. 22)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "irrespective ofwhether the originator and the beneficiary are the sam"</sub>

(p. 22)[^original]

## B. Obligations and decisions for financial institutions and DNFBPs

- **expected / requirement**: INR.1: financial institutions and DNFBPs should be required to identify and assess their PF risks, possibly within existing TFS and/or compliance programmes. (p. 49)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Financial institutions and DNFBPs should be required to take appropriate steps, to identify and assess their proliferation financing risks."</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.1: FIs and DNFBPs should document PF risk assessments to demonstrate their basis, keep them up to date, and have mechanisms to give risk assessment information to competent authorities and SRBs; the assessment's extent should fit the nature and size of the business. (p. 49)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "any assessment of proliferation financing risks should be appropriate to the nature and size of the business"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.1: FIs and DNFBPs should have policies, controls and procedures to manage and mitigate identified PF risks, and should be required to monitor implementation of those controls and enhance them if necessary. (p. 49)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "They should be required to monitor the implementation of those controls and to enhance them, if necessary."</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: INR.1 states that financial institutions and DNFBPs should always understand their proliferation financing risks. (p. 49)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Financial institutions and DNFBPs should always understand their proliferation financing risks"</sub>
- **permissive / requirement**: INR.1 allows competent authorities or SRBs to determine that individual documented PF risk assessments are not required, if the specific risks inherent to the sector are clearly identified and understood. (p. 49)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "competent authorities or SRBs may determine that individual documented risk assessments are not required"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.1: PF policies, controls and procedures should be approved by senior management, and mitigation measures should be consistent with national requirements and guidance from competent authorities and SRBs. (p. 50)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "controls and procedures should be approved by senior management, and the measures taken"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.1: where PF risks are higher, countries should require FIs and DNFBPs to introduce enhanced controls aimed at detecting possible breaches, non-implementation or evasion of R.7 TFS; where lower, measures should be commensurate while R.7 is still fully implemented. (p. 50)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "introducing enhanced controls aimed at detecting possible breaches, non-implementation or evasion"</sub>

## Footnotes of INR.1

- **descriptive / context**: INR.1 footnote 2: paragraphs 1 and 2 of INR.7 set the scope of R.7, which is limited to targeted financial sanctions and does not cover other UNSCR requirements; FATF PF requirements are limited to Recommendations 1, 2, 7 and 15, and R.1 PF obligations do not expand other Recommendations. (p. 50)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "it is limited to targeted financial sanctions and does not cover other requirements of the UNSCRs"</sub>
- **permissive / requirement**: INR.1 footnote 3: countries may exempt a type of FI or DNFBP from PF risk assessment and mitigation requirements given a proven low PF risk, but full implementation of R.7 TFS remains mandatory in all cases. (p. 50)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "provided there is a proven low risk of proliferation financing relating to such financial institutions or DNFBPs"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 7: countries should implement TFS complying with UNSCRs on preventing, suppressing and disrupting WMD proliferation and its financing, freezing without delay the funds or assets of UNSC Chapter VII designees and ensuring no funds are made available to them directly or indirectly. (p. 50)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "relating to the prevention, suppression and disruption of proliferation of weapons of mass destruction and its financing"</sub>

## A. OBJECTIVE

- **descriptive / context**: INR.7 paragraph 2: R.7 does not replace other measures or obligations for dealing with funds in criminal, civil or administrative investigations or proceedings under WMD non-proliferation treaties or UNSCRs. (p. 50)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "none of the requirements in Recommendation 7 is intended to replace other measures or obligations"</sub>

- **expected / context**: INR.1 footnote 5 says that, where appropriate, PF risk assessments at a supra-national level should be taken into account when considering whether "this obligation" is satisfied. (p. 50)[^original]

## A. OBJECTIVE

- **descriptive / context**: INR.7: R.7 focuses on preventive measures, necessary and unique, to stop the flow of funds or assets to proliferators or proliferation and their use by proliferators, as required by the Security Council. (p. 51)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The focus of Recommendation 7 is on preventive measures that are necessary and unique"</sub>

## B. DESIGNATIONS

- **descriptive / context**: INR.7: designations are made by the Security Council in resolution annexes or by its Committees; Member States have no specific obligation to propose designations, although in practice the Council relies mainly on Member State requests. (p. 51)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "There is no specific obligation upon United Nations Member States to submit proposals for designations"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: UNSCR 1718 (2006) provides that its Committee shall issue guidelines to facilitate implementation; Resolution 2231 (2015) provides that the Security Council itself makes practical arrangements to undertake implementation tasks. (p. 51)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the relevant Committee shall promulgate guidelines as may be necessary to facilitate the implementation"</sub>
- **permissive / recommendation**: INR.7: countries could establish authority and procedures to propose designations, including identifying a competent executive or judicial authority to propose persons meeting UNSCR 1718 (2006) criteria to the 1718 Sanctions Committee, and UNSCR 2231 (2015) criteria to the Security Council, where it has sufficient evidence. (p. 51)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "proposing to the 1718 Sanctions Committee, for designation as appropriate, persons or entities that meet the specific criteria"</sub>
- **permissive / recommendation**: INR.7: countries could have mechanisms to identify designation targets based on criteria in resolutions 1718 (2006), 2231 (2015) and successors, determining under applicable (supra-)national principles whether reasonable grounds or a reasonable basis exist. (p. 51)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "whether reasonable grounds or a reasonable basis exists to propose a designation"</sub>
- **permissive / recommendation**: INR.7: countries could have legal authority and procedures to collect or solicit as much information as possible from all relevant sources to identify persons meeting designation criteria. (p. 51)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "to collect or solicit as much information as possible from all relevant sources"</sub>
- **descriptive / recommendation**: INR.7: in proposing designations, each country's authority applies its own legal standard, considering human rights, the rule of law and the rights of innocent third parties, and takes into account Section E criteria. (p. 52)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "taking into consideration human rights, respect for the rule of law"</sub>
- **permissive / recommendation**: INR.7: countries could have procedures to operate ex parte against a person or entity whose designation proposal is under consideration. (p. 52)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "having procedures to be able, where necessary, to operate ex parte"</sub>

## C. FREEZING AND PROHIBITING DEALING IN FUNDS OR OTHER ASSETS OF DESIGNATED PERSONS AND ENTITIES

- **mandatory / requirement**: INR.7: countries are obliged to implement TFS without delay against persons designated under resolution 1718 (2006) and successors (by the Security Council or 1718 Sanctions Committee) and under resolution 2231 (2015) and successors (by the Security Council), acting under Chapter VII. (p. 52)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "There is an obligation for countries to implement targeted financial sanctions without delay"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.7: countries should establish legal authority and identify competent domestic authorities to implement and enforce TFS. (p. 52)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Countries should establish the necessary legal authority and identify competent domestic authorities"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.7: countries should require all natural and legal persons in the country to freeze designated persons' funds or assets without delay and without prior notice. (p. 52)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "to freeze, without delay and without prior notice, the funds or other assets of designated persons and entities"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.7: the freeze extends to all funds owned or controlled by the designee, not only those tied to a particular proliferation act, plot or threat; to funds wholly or jointly owned or controlled directly or indirectly; to derived funds; and to funds of those acting on their behalf or direction. (p. 52)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "not just those that can be tied to a particular act, plot or threat of proliferation"</sub>

## B. DESIGNATIONS

- **permissive / recommendation**: INR.7 lists, among elements countries could consider when proposing names to the 1718 Sanctions Committee or the Security Council, providing as much detail as possible, including sufficient identifying information to allow accurate and positive identification of persons and entities. (p. 52)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "sufficient identifying information to allow for the accurate and positive identification of persons and entities"</sub>

## C. FREEZING AND PROHIBITING DEALING IN FUNDS OR OTHER ASSETS OF DESIGNATED PERSONS AND ENTITIES

- **expected / requirement**: INR.7: countries should prevent nationals and persons in their territory from making funds available to or for designated persons unless licensed, authorised or notified under the relevant resolutions. (p. 53)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "unless licensed, authorised or otherwise notified in accordance with the relevant Security Council resolutions"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.7: countries should communicate designations to FIs and DNFBPs immediately and give clear guidance to those possibly holding targeted funds on their freezing obligations. (p. 53)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "communicating designations to financial institutions and DNFBPs immediately upon taking such action"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.7: countries should require FIs and DNFBPs to report frozen assets and actions taken under the prohibitions, including attempted transactions, and ensure authorities use this information effectively. (p. 53)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "report to competent authorities any assets frozen or actions taken"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.7: countries should adopt effective measures protecting the rights of bona fide third parties acting in good faith. (p. 53)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "protect the rights of bona fide third parties acting in good faith"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.7: countries should monitor and ensure FI and DNFBP compliance with R.7 laws or enforceable means, and non-compliance should be subject to civil, administrative or criminal sanctions. (p. 53)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Failure to comply with such laws, or enforceable means should be subject to civil, administrative or criminal sanctions."</sub>

## D. DE-LISTING, UNFREEZING AND PROVIDING ACCESS TO FROZEN FUNDS OR OTHER ASSETS

- **expected / requirement**: INR.7: countries should have publicly known procedures to submit de-listing requests to the Security Council for designees that no longer meet the criteria; once de-listed, the freezing obligation ends. (p. 53)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the obligation to freeze no longer exists"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.7: for resolution 1718 (2006), de-listing procedures should follow guidelines under resolution 1730 (2006) including the Focal Point mechanism; countries should enable listed persons to petition the Focal Point or tell them to petition it directly. (p. 54)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "petition a request for delisting at the Focal Point for de-listing established pursuant to resolution 1730 (2006)"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.7: for false positives (same or similar names inadvertently frozen), countries should have publicly known procedures to unfreeze funds promptly once verified that the person is not a designee. (p. 54)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "who are inadvertently affected by a freezing mechanism (i.e. a false positive)"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.7: where exemption conditions in resolutions 1718 (2006) and 2231 (2015) are met, countries should authorise access to funds under their procedures. (p. 54)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "countries should authorise access to funds or other assets in accordance with the procedures set out therein"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.7: countries should permit interest, other earnings, or payments due under pre-existing contracts to be added to accounts frozen under resolution 1718 (2006) or 2231 (2015), provided these additions remain frozen. (p. 54)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "provided that any such interest, other earnings and payments continue to be subject to these provisions and are frozen"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: INR.7: freezes under resolution 1737 (2006) continued by 2231 (2015), or under 2231 (2015), do not prevent a designee paying under a contract predating listing, if countries determine the contract is unrelated to prohibited items/services and the payment is not received by a person under paragraph 6 of Annex B to resolution 2231 (2015). (p. 54)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "shall not prevent a designated person or entity from making any payment due under a contract"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: INR.7: such pre-listing contract payments also require prior notification to the Security Council of the intent to make/receive payments or authorise unfreezing, ten working days before authorisation. (p. 54)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "ten working days prior to such authorisation"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.7: countries should communicate de-listings and unfreezings to the financial sector and DNFBPs immediately, with adequate guidance on obligations to respect them. (p. 54)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "communicating de-listings and unfreezings to the financial sector and the DNFBPs immediately"</sub>

## E. UNITED NATIONS DESIGNATION CRITERIA

- **descriptive / definition**: DPRK designation criteria (Resolutions 1718 (2006), 2087 (2013), 2094 (2013), 2270 (2016)) cover persons engaged in or supporting, including through illicit means, DPRK nuclear, other WMD and ballistic missile programmes. (p. 55)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "any person or entity providing support for DPRK’s nuclearrelated, other WMD related and ballistic missile-related programmes"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: DPRK criteria also cover persons acting on behalf or at the direction of, or legal persons owned or controlled directly or indirectly by, such designees. (p. 55)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "any legal person or entity owned or controlled, directly or indirectly, by any person or entity designated"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: DPRK criteria also cover persons who assisted sanctions evasion or violated resolutions 1718 (2006) and 1874 (2009), or contributed to prohibited DPRK programmes or activities or evasion. (p. 55)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "has assisted in the evasion of sanctions or in violating the provisions of resolutions 1718 (2006)"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: DPRK criteria also cover entities of the DPRK Government or Worker's Party of Korea, and those acting for or owned or controlled by them, that countries determine are associated with DPRK nuclear or ballistic missile programmes or other prohibited activities (per resolution 2270 (2016) OP32). (p. 55)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "any entity of the Government of the DPRK or the Worker’s Party of Korea"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: Iran criteria (Resolution 2231 (2015)) cover persons engaged in, associated with or supporting Iran's proliferation-sensitive nuclear activities contrary to JCPOA commitments or nuclear weapon delivery system development, including procurement of items in Annex B to resolution 2231 (2015). (p. 55)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "contrary to Iran’s commitments in the Joint Comprehensive Plan of Action (JCPOA)"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: Iran criteria also cover persons assisting designees to evade or act inconsistently with the JCPOA or resolution 2231 (2015), and those acting on behalf of, at the direction of, or owned or controlled by such persons. (p. 55)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "any person or entity assisting designated persons or entities in evading or acting inconsistently with the JCPOA"</sub>

## Footnotes of INR.7

- **descriptive / context**: INR.7 footnote 14: R.7 focuses on TFS, including restrictions in resolution 2231 (2015) Annex B paragraphs 6(c) and (d), whereas UNSCRs are broader, prescribing travel bans, activity-based financial prohibitions, category-based sanctions and vigilance measures; FATF has issued non-binding guidance on these.

## Glossary

- **descriptive / definition**: The FATF glossary defines a virtual asset service provider (VASP) as any natural or legal person not covered elsewhere under the Recommendations who, as a business, conducts one or more of the listed activities or operations for or on behalf of another natural or legal person. (p. 111)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "any natural or legal person who is not covered elsewhere under the Recommendations, and as a business conducts"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: VASP activity includes exchange between virtual assets and fiat currencies. (p. 111)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "exchange between virtual assets and fiat currencies;"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: VASP activity includes exchange between one or more forms of virtual assets. (p. 111)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "exchange between one or more forms of virtual assets;"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: VASP activity includes transfer of virtual assets. In this context, transfer means conducting a transaction on behalf of another natural or legal person that moves a virtual asset from one virtual asset address or account to another. (p. 111)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "transfer means to conduct a transaction on behalf of another natural or legal person that moves a virtual asset"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: VASP activity includes safekeeping and/or administration of virtual assets or instruments enabling control over virtual assets. (p. 111)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "safekeeping and/or administration of virtual assets or instruments enabling control over virtual assets;"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: VASP activity includes participation in and provision of financial services related to an issuer's offer and/or sale of a virtual asset. (p. 111)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "participation in and provision of financial services related to an issuer’s offer and/or sale of a virtual asset."</sub>

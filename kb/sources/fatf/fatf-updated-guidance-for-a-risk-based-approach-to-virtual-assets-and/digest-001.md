## Executive Summary

- **descriptive / context**: In October 2018 the FATF adopted changes to its Recommendations to clarify that they apply to financial activities involving virtual assets, and added two new Glossary definitions: 'virtual asset' (VA) and 'virtual asset service provider' (VASP). (p. 6)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "adopted changes to its Recommendations to explicitly clarify that they apply to financial activities involving virtual assets"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: The amended FATF Recommendation 15 requires that VASPs be regulated for AML/CFT purposes, be licensed or registered, and be subject to effective systems for monitoring or supervision. (p. 6)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "requires that VASPs be regulated for anti-money laundering and countering the financing of terrorism"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: In June 2019 the FATF adopted an Interpretive Note to Recommendation 15 clarifying how the requirements apply to VAs and VASPs, in particular on application of the risk-based approach to VA activities and VASPs; supervision or monitoring of VASPs; licensing or registration; preventive measures such as customer due diligence, recordkeeping and suspicious transaction reporting; sanctions and other enforcement measures; and international co-operation. (p. 6)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the FATF adopted an Interpretive Note to Recommendation 15 to further clarify how the FATF requirements should apply"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The FATF adopted a first version of this Guidance on applying the risk-based approach to VAs and VASPs in June 2019 and updated it in October 2021. It is intended to help national authorities understand and develop regulatory and supervisory responses to VA activities and VASPs, and to help private sector entities engaging in VA activities understand their AML/CFT obligations and comply effectively. It also updates the 2015 FATF Guidance for a Risk-Based Approach to Virtual Currencies. (p. 6)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "It is intended to both help national authorities in understanding and developing regulatory and supervisory responses"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The Guidance discusses the five types of activities covered by the VASP definition and gives examples of VA-related activities that would fall within it and those that would potentially be excluded from FATF scope. It highlights the key elements required to qualify as a VASP: acting as a business for or on behalf of another person and providing or actively facilitating VA-related activities. (p. 6)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "acting as a business for or on behalf of another person and providing or actively facilitating VA-related activities"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The Guidance describes application of the FATF Recommendations to countries and competent authorities and to VASPs and other obliged entities engaged in VA activities, including financial institutions such as banks and securities broker-dealers. Almost all FATF Recommendations are directly relevant to the ML/TF risks of VAs and VASPs, while others are less directly or explicitly linked but still relevant and applicable. (p. 6)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Almost all of the FATF Recommendations are directly relevant to address the ML/TF risks associated with VAs and VASPs"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: VASPs have the same full set of obligations as financial institutions and designated non-financial businesses and professions (DNFBPs). (p. 6)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "VASPs therefore have the same full set of obligations as financial institutions"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: The Guidance sets out the need for countries, VASPs and other entities involved in VA activities to understand the ML/TF risks of VA activities and take appropriate mitigating measures. It offers examples of risk indicators that should specifically be considered in a VA context, stressing factors that would further obscure transactions or limit VASPs' ability to identify customers. (p. 6)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "examples of risk indicators that should specifically be considered in a VA context"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The Guidance details the full range of obligations applicable to VASPs and VAs under the FATF Recommendations on a Recommendation-by-Recommendation basis, and clarifies that all funds or value-based terms in the Recommendations (for example property, proceeds, funds, funds or other assets, and other corresponding value) include VAs. (p. 7)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "include VAs. Consequently, countries should apply all of the relevant measures under the FATF Recommendations"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Countries should apply all relevant measures under the FATF Recommendations to VAs, VA activities and VASPs. (p. 7)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "countries should apply all of the relevant measures under the FATF Recommendations to VAs, VA activities, and VASPs"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: On registration or licensing, VASPs should be registered or licensed at a minimum in the country where they were created, or, where the VASP is a natural person, in the jurisdiction where their business is located. Jurisdictions can also choose to require VASPs to be licensed or registered before conducting business in or from their jurisdiction. (p. 7)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "in the jurisdiction where their business is located in cases where they are a natural person"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: National authorities are required to take action to identify natural or legal persons that carry out VA activities without the requisite license or registration. This applies equally to countries that have chosen to prohibit VAs and VA activities at the national level. (p. 7)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "national authorities are required to take action to identify natural or legal persons that carry out VA activities"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The Guidance places specific focus on the importance of international co-operation between VASP supervisors, given the cross-border nature of VASPs' activities and service provision. (p. 7)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "importance of international co-operation between supervisors, given the cross-border nature"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: VASPs, and other entities involved in VA activities, need to apply all the preventive measures described in FATF Recommendations 10 to 21. (p. 7)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "need to apply all the preventive measures described in FATF Recommendations 10 to 21"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: Under Recommendation 10, there is a USD/EUR 1 000 threshold for occasional transactions above which VASPs must conduct customer due diligence. (p. 7)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the USD/EUR 1 000 threshold for occasional transactions, above which VASPs must conduct customer due diligence"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: Under Recommendation 16 (the 'travel rule'), there is an obligation to obtain, hold and transmit required originator and beneficiary information, immediately and securely, when conducting VA transfers. (p. 7)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "obtain, hold, and transmit required originator and beneficiary information, immediately and securely"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The Guidance also provides examples of jurisdictional approaches to regulating, supervising and enforcing rules on VA activities, VASPs and other obliged entities for AML/CFT. (p. 7)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "examples of jurisdictional approaches to regulating, supervising, and enforcing VA activities"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Area (2) of the October 2021 update gives guidance on how the FATF Standards apply to stablecoins and clarifies that a range of entities involved in stablecoin arrangements could qualify as VASPs. (p. 7)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "a range of entities involved in stablecoin arrangements could qualify as VASPs under the FATF Standards"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Area (3) of the October 2021 update gives additional guidance on the risks and the tools available to countries to address ML/TF risks of peer-to-peer transactions, which are transactions that do not involve any obliged entities. (p. 7)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "which are transactions that do not involve any obliged entities"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: On VASP supervision, the Guidance makes clear that only competent authorities may act as VASP supervisory or monitoring bodies; self-regulatory bodies may not. (p. 7)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "only competent authorities, and not self-regulatory bodies, can act as VASP supervisory or monitoring bodies"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: VASP supervisors should conduct risk-based supervision or monitoring and have adequate powers, including to carry out inspections, compel production of information and impose sanctions. (p. 7)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "They should conduct risk-based supervision or monitoring, and have adequate powers"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: On travel rule compliance, the Guidance indicates that relevant authorities should co-ordinate so that compliance can be achieved in a way compatible with national data protection and privacy rules. (p. 7)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "relevant authorities should co-ordinate to ensure this can be done in a way that is compatible with national data protection"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The October 2021 update revised the Guidance in six key areas where more FATF guidance was sought. The first was to clarify that the VA and VASP definitions are expansive, so that no relevant financial asset should escape the FATF Standards, whether as a VA or as another type of financial asset. (p. 7)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "there should not be a case where a relevant financial asset is not covered by the FATF Standards"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The remaining areas of the October 2021 update are (4) updated guidance on licensing and registration of VASPs, (5) additional guidance for the public and private sectors on implementing the 'travel rule', and (6) Principles of Information-Sharing and Co-operation Amongst VASP Supervisors. The updated document incorporates and supersedes the 2019 Guidance. (p. 8)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "This document incorporates and supersedes the 2019 Guidance."</sub>

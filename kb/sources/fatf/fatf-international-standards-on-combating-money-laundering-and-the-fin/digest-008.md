## D. EFFECTIVE APPROACH IN IDENTIFYING, PREVENTING AND COMBATING TF ABUSE OF NPOS

- **permissive / context**: For NPOs identified as low-risk for TF abuse, countries may limit themselves to outreach on terrorist financing issues and may decide not to take additional mitigating measures. (p. 64)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "For NPOs identified to be at low-risk of TF abuse, countries may focus only on undertaking outreach"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: In situations other than low-risk NPOs, an effective approach to protecting NPOs from TF abuse should involve all four elements: sustained outreach; focused, proportionate, risk-based measures including oversight or monitoring; effective information gathering and investigation; and capacity to respond to international requests - without unduly disrupting legitimate NPO activities. (p. 64)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "an effective approach should involve all four of the following elements to protect NPOs"</sub>

  <sub>anchor: "Countries should have clear policies to promote accountability, integrity and public confidence in the administration and management of NPOs."</sub>

## B. CDD – PERSONS ACTING ON BEHALF OF A CUSTOMER

  <sub>anchor: "they should take into account the risk of tipping-off when performing the CDD process"</sub>
- **permissive / expectation**: If an institution reasonably believes performing CDD will tip off the customer or potential customer, it may choose not to pursue CDD, and should file an STR. (p. 67)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "it may choose not to pursue that process, and should file an STR"</sub>

## C. CDD FOR LEGAL PERSONS AND ARRANGEMENTS

- **expected / expectation**: For customers that are legal persons or legal arrangements, financial institutions should be required to identify and verify the customer, understand the nature of its business, and understand its ownership and control structure. (p. 67)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "identify and verify the identity of the customer, and understand the nature of its business"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: Footnote 37: a legal arrangement such as a trust being a customer or carrying out a transaction refers to the trustee (natural or legal person) establishing the relationship or transacting on behalf of the beneficiaries or under the trust terms; normal CDD for natural/legal persons, including INR.10 paragraph 4, still applies, plus the additional trust and trust beneficial owner requirements. (p. 67)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "refers to situations where a natural or legal person that is the trustee establishes the business relationship"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The purpose of identifying and verifying legal-person/arrangement customers and their beneficial owners is twofold: to prevent unlawful use of legal persons and arrangements by understanding the customer enough to assess ML/TF risk, and to take appropriate steps to mitigate those risks. (p. 67)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "first, to prevent the unlawful use of legal persons and arrangements, by gaining a sufficient understanding of the customer"</sub>
- **expected / data or feature**: To identify and verify a legal-person/arrangement customer, institutions would normally need its name, legal form and proof of existence, verifiable e.g. through a certificate of incorporation, certificate of good standing, partnership agreement, deed of trust or other reliable independent documentation.   <sub>anchor: "Name, legal form and proof of existence – verification could be obtained, for example, through a certificate of incorporation"</sub>
- **expected / data or feature**: Identification of a legal-person/arrangement customer normally also requires the powers that regulate and bind it (e.g. memorandum and articles of association) and the names of relevant senior management persons (e.g. senior managing directors, trustees). - **expected / data or feature**: Identification of a legal-person/arrangement customer normally also requires the registered office address and, if different, a principal place of business. (p. 67)[^original]

- **expected / expectation**: Financial institutions should be required to identify the beneficial owners of legal-person/arrangement customers and take reasonable measures to verify their identity. (p. 67)[^original]

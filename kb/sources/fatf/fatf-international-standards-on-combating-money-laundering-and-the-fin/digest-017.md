## INR.18

- **descriptive / context**: November 2017 revision of the Interpretive Note to Recommendation 18 clarified requirements on sharing information on unusual or suspicious transactions within financial groups, including providing this information to branches and subsidiaries when necessary for AML/CFT risk management.   <sub>anchor: "to clarify the interaction of these requirements with tipping-off provisions"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: February 2018 revision of Recommendation 2 ensured compatibility of AML/CFT requirements with data protection and privacy rules and promoted domestic inter-agency information sharing among competent authorities. (p. 146)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "compatibility of AML/CFT requirements and data protection and privacy rules"</sub>

## INR. 15

- **descriptive / context**: June 2019 added a new Interpretive Note to R.15 setting out the application of the FATF Standards to virtual asset activities and service providers. (p. 147)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "sets out the application of the FATF Standards to virtual asset activities and service providers"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: October 2020 revision of R.1 and INR.1 requires countries, financial institutions and DNFBPs to identify and assess the risks of potential breaches, non-implementation or evasion of the targeted financial sanctions related to proliferation financing contained in Recommendation 7, and to take action to mitigate these risks. (p. 147)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "to identify and assess the risks of potential breaches, non- implementation or evasion of the targeted financial sanctions"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: October 2020 revision of Recommendation 2 made a minor consequential amendment inserting a reference to counter proliferation financing in national co-operation and co-ordination, and added a new Interpretive Note setting out the inter-agency framework to promote domestic co-operation, co-ordination and information exchange. (p. 147)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "inter-agency framework to promote domestic co-operation, co-ordination and information exchange"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: June 2021 revision of INR.15 clarified the applicability of proliferation financing risk assessment and mitigation requirements to virtual asset activities and service providers. (p. 147)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "applicability of proliferation financing risk assessment and mitigation requirements to virtual asset activities"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: October 2021 revision of the Glossary definition of "designated categories of offences" clarified the types of offences falling within the "environmental crime" category. (p. 147)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "to clarify the types of offences which fall within the"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: October 2021 revision of INR.22/23 and the Glossary revised R.23 to clarify DNFBP obligations to apply group-wide programmes, and clarified that the Glossary definition of "financial group" is not limited to Core Principles institutions, with a minor consequential amendment to INR.26.   <sub>anchor: "to clarify obligations on DNFBPs to apply group-wide programmes"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: March 2022 revision of Recommendation 24, INR.24 and the Glossary added definitions of "nominator" and "nominee shareholder or director" to strengthen the standards on beneficial ownership of legal persons. (p. 147)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "to strengthen the standards on beneficial ownership of legal persons"</sub>

  <sub>anchor: "to strengthen the standards on beneficial ownership of legal arrangements"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: October 2023 revision of Recommendations 4, 30, 31 and 38, INR.4, INR.30, INR.38 and INR.40 and the Glossary revised FATF's asset recovery Standards and related Glossary definitions. (p. 148)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "asset recovery Standards and Glossary definitions related to asset recovery."</sub>

## INR.16

- **descriptive / context**: October 2025 factual update at paragraph 31 of INR.16 clarified that a beneficiary financial institution should have effective risk-based policies and procedures for determining when to execute, reject or suspend any payment or value transfer lacking required originator or beneficiary information (regardless of whether above or below the applicable threshold), or when it identifies potentially misdirected payments, in implementing paragraph 30. (p. 149)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "should have effective risk-based policies and procedures for determining when to execute, reject, or suspend"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: June 2026 update to Recommendation 6 (INR.6) incorporates the humanitarian exemption contained in UN Security Council resolutions on preventing and suppressing terrorism and terrorist financing, UNSCRs 2664 and 2761, as well as UNSCR 2615. (p. 151)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "incorporate the humanitarian exemption contained in the United Nations Security Council resolutions"</sub>

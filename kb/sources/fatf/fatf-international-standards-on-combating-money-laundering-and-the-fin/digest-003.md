## DESIGNATED NON-FINANCIAL BUSINESSES AND PROFESSIONS

- **expected / requirement**: FATF Recommendation 22 applies the CDD and record-keeping requirements of Recommendations 10, 11, 12, 15 and 17 to DNFBPs in specified situations. (p. 20)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The customer due diligence and record-keeping requirements set out in Recommendations 10, 11, 12, 15, and 17"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Under Recommendation 22, casinos must apply CDD when customers engage in financial transactions equal to or above the designated threshold. (p. 20)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Casinos – when customers engage in financial transactions equal to or above the"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Under Recommendation 22, real estate agents must apply CDD when involved in transactions for clients concerning buying and selling real estate. (p. 20)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Real estate agents – when they are involved in transactions for their client concerning"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Under Recommendation 22, dealers in precious metals and stones must apply CDD when engaging in any cash transaction with a customer at or above the designated threshold. (p. 20)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Dealers in precious metals and dealers in precious stones – when they engage in any"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Under Recommendation 22, lawyers, notaries, other independent legal professionals and accountants must apply CDD when preparing or carrying out client transactions on: buying and selling real estate; managing client money, securities or assets; managing bank, savings or securities accounts; organising contributions for companies; and creating, operating or managing legal persons or arrangements and buying and selling business entities. (p. 20)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "organisation of contributions for the creation, operation or management of companies"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Under Recommendation 22, trust and company service providers must apply CDD when acting as formation agent; acting or arranging for someone to act as director, secretary, partner or similar; providing a registered office or business, correspondence or administrative address; acting as trustee of an express trust or equivalent; or acting as nominee shareholder. (p. 20)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "acting as a formation agent of legal persons"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 23 applies Recommendations 18 to 21 to all DNFBPs, subject to qualifications. (p. 20)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The requirements set out in Recommendations 18 to 21 apply to all designated non-financial businesses and professions"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Under Recommendation 23, lawyers, notaries, other legal professionals and accountants must report suspicious transactions when engaging in a financial transaction for a client in Recommendation 22(d) activities. (p. 20)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "they engage in a financial transaction in relation to the activities described in paragraph (d) of Recommendation 22"</sub>
- **recommended / recommendation**: Countries are strongly encouraged to extend STR reporting to the rest of accountants' professional activities, including auditing. (p. 20)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Countries are strongly encouraged to extend the reporting requirement to the rest of the professional activities of accountants"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Under Recommendation 23, dealers in precious metals and stones must report suspicious transactions when engaging in cash transactions at or above the designated threshold. (p. 20)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "suspicious transactions when they engage in any cash transaction with a customer equal to or above"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Under Recommendation 23, TCSPs must report suspicious transactions when engaging in transactions for a client in Recommendation 22(e) activities. (p. 20)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the activities referred to in paragraph (e) of Recommendation 22"</sub>

## E. TRANSPARENCY AND BENEFICIAL OWNERSHIP OF LEGAL PERSONS AND ARRANGEMENTS

- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 24 requires countries to assess the ML/TF misuse risks of legal persons and take measures to prevent misuse. (p. 23)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Countries should assess the risks of misuse of legal persons for money laundering or terrorist financing"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 24 requires adequate, accurate and up-to-date beneficial ownership and control information on legal persons, rapidly and efficiently obtainable by competent authorities through a beneficial ownership register or an alternative mechanism. (p. 23)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "through either a register of beneficial ownership or an alternative mechanism"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 24 prohibits legal persons from issuing new bearer shares or bearer share warrants and requires measures against misuse of existing ones. (p. 23)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "should not permit legal persons to issue new bearer shares or bearer share warrants"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 24 requires effective measures so nominee shareholders and directors are not misused for ML or TF. (p. 23)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "nominee shareholders and directors are not misused for money laundering or terrorist financing"</sub>
- **recommended / recommendation**: Recommendations 24 and 25 say countries should consider facilitating FI and DNFBP access to beneficial ownership and control information for Recommendations 10 and 22 purposes. (p. 23)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Countries should consider facilitating access to beneficial ownership and control information by financial institutions and DNFBPs"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 25 requires countries to assess and prevent ML/TF misuse of legal arrangements, ensuring adequate, accurate and up-to-date information on express trusts and similar arrangements, including settlor(s), trustee(s) and beneficiary(ies), efficiently and timely accessible to authorities. (p. 23)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "including information on the settlor(s), trustee(s) and beneficiary(ies)"</sub>

## REGULATION AND SUPERVISION

- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 26 requires FIs to be adequately regulated and supervised and effectively implementing the FATF Recommendations. (p. 24)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "financial institutions are subject to adequate regulation and supervision"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 26 requires measures to prevent criminals or associates holding or beneficially owning significant or controlling interests or management functions in FIs. (p. 24)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "prevent criminals or their associates from holding, or being the beneficial owner of, a significant or controlling interest"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 26 prohibits approving the establishment or continued operation of shell banks. (p. 24)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Countries should not approve the establishment, or continued operation, of shell banks"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: For FIs subject to the Core Principles, prudential measures relevant to ML/TF should apply similarly for AML/CFT, including consolidated group supervision for AML/CFT. (p. 24)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "This should include applying consolidated group supervision for AML/CFT purposes"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Other FIs should be licensed or registered, adequately regulated and supervised or monitored for AML/CFT having regard to sector risk; at minimum, money or value transfer and money or currency changing services must be licensed or registered and subject to effective compliance monitoring. (p. 24)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "where financial institutions provide a service of money or value transfer, or of money or currency changing"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 27 requires supervisors to have adequate powers to supervise, monitor and ensure FI AML/CFT compliance, including inspections, compelling production of relevant information, and imposing sanctions in line with Recommendation 35. (p. 24)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "including the authority to conduct inspections"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 27 requires supervisors to have a range of disciplinary and financial sanctions, including power to withdraw, restrict or suspend an FI's licence where applicable. (p. 24)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the power to withdraw, restrict or suspend the financial institution’s license"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 28 requires casinos to be under a comprehensive regulatory and supervisory regime: at minimum licensed, with measures preventing criminals or associates from holding significant interests, management functions or being operators, and effectively supervised for AML/CFT. (p. 24)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "comprehensive regulatory and supervisory regime that ensures that they have effectively implemented"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 28 requires other DNFBPs to be subject to effective, risk-sensitive compliance monitoring by a supervisor or an appropriate SRB, provided the SRB can ensure members comply. (p. 24)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "This should be performed on a risk-sensitive basis"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Under Recommendation 28 the supervisor or SRB should prevent criminals or associates from professional accreditation or significant interests or management functions, e.g. via a "fit and proper" test, and have effective, proportionate and dissuasive sanctions per Recommendation 35. (p. 25)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "through evaluating persons on the basis of a “fit and proper” test"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 29 requires an FIU serving as national centre for receiving and analysing STRs and other information relevant to ML, predicate offences and TF, and disseminating results. (p. 25)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "serves as a national centre for the receipt and analysis of"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 29 requires the FIU to be able to obtain additional information from reporting entities and have timely access to financial, administrative and law enforcement information. (p. 25)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The FIU should be able to obtain additional information from reporting entities"</sub>

## OPERATIONAL AND LAW ENFORCEMENT

- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 30 requires designated law enforcement authorities to be responsible for ML and TF investigations within national AML/CFT policies. (p. 25)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "designated law enforcement authorities have responsibility for money laundering and terrorist financing investigations"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 30 requires, at least for major proceeds-generating offences, pro-active parallel financial investigations when pursuing ML, predicate offences and TF, including where the predicate occurs abroad. (p. 25)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "should develop a pro-active parallel financial investigation"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 30 requires competent authorities responsible for expeditiously identifying, tracing and initiating freezing and seizing of criminal property, use of multi-disciplinary financial or asset investigation groups when necessary, and cooperative investigations with foreign authorities when necessary. (p. 25)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "temporary multi-disciplinary groups specialised in financial or asset investigations"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 31 requires investigators to access all necessary documents and information, including compulsory production of records from FIs, DNFBPs and others, search of persons and premises, witness statements, and seizure of evidence. (p. 25)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "compulsory measures for the production of records held by financial institutions, DNFBPs"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 31 requires a wide range of investigative techniques including undercover operations, intercepting communications, accessing computer systems and controlled delivery. (p. 25)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "undercover operations, intercepting communications, accessing computer systems and controlled delivery"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 31 requires timely access to a wide range of information to trace criminal property, such as basic and beneficial ownership data, tax information, asset registries (land, property, vehicles, shares) and citizenship, residency or social benefit registries. (p. 25)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "information held in citizenship, residency, or social benefit registries"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 31 requires mechanisms to identify in a timely manner whether persons hold or control accounts, a process to identify assets without prior notice to the owner, and the ability to request all relevant FIU information. (p. 25)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "identify assets without prior notification to the owner"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 32 requires measures to detect physical cross-border transportation of currency and bearer negotiable instruments, including a declaration and/or disclosure system. (p. 25)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "including through a declaration system and/or disclosure system"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 32 requires authorities to have legal power to stop or restrain currency or BNIs suspected of TF, ML or predicate links, or falsely declared or disclosed. (p. 25)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "legal authority to stop or restrain currency or bearer negotiable instruments"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 32 requires effective, proportionate and dissuasive sanctions for false declarations or disclosures, and measures consistent with Recommendation 4 enabling confiscation of currency or BNIs related to TF, ML or predicates. (p. 26)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "persons who make false declaration(s) or disclosure(s)"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 33 requires comprehensive statistics on AML/CFT effectiveness, including STRs received and disseminated; ML/TF investigations, prosecutions and convictions; property frozen, seized and confiscated; and MLA and other international cooperation requests. (p. 26)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Countries should maintain comprehensive statistics on matters relevant to the effectiveness and efficiency"</sub>

## GENERAL REQUIREMENTS

- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 34 requires competent authorities, supervisors and SRBs to establish guidelines and provide feedback helping FIs and DNFBPs apply AML/CFT measures, particularly detecting and reporting suspicious transactions. (p. 27)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "should establish guidelines, and provide feedback"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 35 requires a range of effective, proportionate and dissuasive criminal, civil or administrative sanctions for persons covered by Recommendations 6 and 8 to 23 failing AML/CFT requirements, applicable also to directors and senior management. (p. 27)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Sanctions should be applicable not only to financial institutions and DNFBPs, but also to their directors and senior management"</sub>

## G. INTERNATIONAL COOPERATION

- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 36 requires countries to take immediate steps to become party to and fully implement the Vienna Convention 1988, Palermo Convention 2000, UN Convention against Corruption 2003 and Terrorist Financing Convention 1999. (p. 28)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Countries should take immediate steps to become party to and implement fully the Vienna Convention, 1988"</sub>
- **recommended / recommendation**: Recommendation 36 encourages, where applicable, ratifying the Council of Europe Convention on Cybercrime 2001, the Inter-American Convention against Terrorism 2002 and the Council of Europe 2005 Convention on Laundering and Financing of Terrorism. (p. 28)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the Council of Europe Convention on Cybercrime, 2001"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 37 requires rapid, constructive and effective provision of the widest possible mutual legal assistance for ML, predicate and TF investigations, prosecutions and proceedings, on an adequate legal basis with treaties or mechanisms where appropriate. (p. 28)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the widest possible range of mutual legal assistance"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 37 requires countries not to prohibit MLA or impose unreasonable or unduly restrictive conditions on it. (p. 28)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Not prohibit, or place unreasonable or unduly restrictive conditions on, the provision of"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 37 requires clear, efficient processes for timely prioritisation and execution of MLA requests, a central authority or other official mechanism, and a case management system to monitor progress. (p. 28)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "To monitor progress on requests, a case management system should be maintained"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 37 bars refusing MLA solely because the offence also involves fiscal matters. (p. 28)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "on the sole ground that the"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 37 bars refusing MLA on grounds of FI or DNFBP secrecy or confidentiality laws, except where information is held under legal professional privilege or secrecy. (p. 28)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "except where the relevant information that is sought is held in circumstances where legal professional privilege"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 37 requires maintaining confidentiality of MLA requests subject to domestic law fundamentals; if a requested country cannot, it should promptly inform the requesting country. (p. 28)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "If the requested country cannot comply with the requirement of confidentiality"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 37 requires MLA despite absence of dual criminality where assistance is non-coercive, and countries should consider measures to allow wide assistance absent dual criminality. (p. 28)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "notwithstanding the absence of dual criminality, if the assistance does not involve coercive actions"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Where dual criminality is required (for MLA under Recommendation 37 and extradition under Recommendation 39), it is deemed satisfied if both countries criminalise the underlying conduct, regardless of offence category or terminology. (p. 28)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "provided that both countries criminalise the conduct underlying the offence"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 37 requires Recommendation 31 powers (production, search and seizure of information and financial records, witness statements) and a broad range of other techniques to be available for MLA and, if consistent with domestic framework, for direct foreign judicial or law enforcement requests. (p. 29)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "in response to direct requests from foreign judicial or law enforcement authorities to domestic counterparts"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: When requesting MLA, countries should make best efforts to provide complete factual and legal information including urgency, use expeditious means, and ascertain the requested country's legal requirements beforehand. (p. 29)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "make best efforts to provide complete factual and legal information"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: MLA and extradition authorities should have adequate financial, human and technical resources, and staff should maintain high professional and confidentiality standards, high integrity and appropriate skills. (p. 29)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "should be provided with adequate financial, human and technical resources"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 38 requires measures to act expeditiously on foreign requests to identify, trace, evaluate, investigate, freeze, seize and confiscate criminal property and property of corresponding value, and to recognise and enforce foreign freezing, seizing or confiscation orders. (p. 29)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "recognise and enforce foreign freezing, seizing, or confiscation orders"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 38 requires ability to manage property subject to confiscation at all stages, share or return confiscated property, and the widest possible range of mechanisms for asset recovery cooperation. (p. 29)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "at all stages of the asset recovery process and share or return confiscated property"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 39 requires constructive, effective execution of ML and TF extradition requests without undue delay and measures to avoid safe havens for those charged with TF, terrorist acts or terrorist organisations. (p. 29)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "do not provide safe havens for individuals charged with the financing of terrorism"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 39 requires ML and TF to be extraditable offences, clear and efficient processes with prioritisation and case management, no unreasonable conditions, and an adequate legal framework. (p. 29)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "ensure money laundering and terrorist financing are extraditable offences"</sub>
- **recommended / recommendation**: Recommendation 37 says that, to avoid conflicts of jurisdiction, consideration should be given to devising and applying mechanisms for determining the best venue for prosecuting defendants, in the interests of justice, in cases subject to prosecution in more than one country. (p. 29)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "determining the best venue for prosecution of defendants in the interests of justice"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 39 requires a country to extradite its own nationals or, if it refuses solely on nationality, to submit the case without undue delay to its own authorities for prosecution as for any serious domestic offence, cooperating on procedure and evidence. (p. 30)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Each country should either extradite its own nationals"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 39 calls for simplified extradition mechanisms consistent with domestic law, e.g. direct transmission of provisional arrest requests, extradition on warrants or judgments only, or simplified extradition of consenting persons. (p. 30)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "simplified extradition of consenting persons who waive formal extradition proceedings"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 40 requires competent authorities to provide the widest range of international cooperation on ML, predicate offences and TF rapidly, constructively and effectively, both spontaneously and on request, on a lawful basis. (p. 30)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "both spontaneously and upon request, and there should be a lawful basis for providing cooperation"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 40 requires authorities to use the most efficient means to cooperate, negotiating and signing any needed MOUs in a timely way with the widest range of foreign counterparts. (p. 30)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "these should be negotiated and signed in a timely way with the widest range of foreign counterparts"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Recommendation 40 requires clear channels for transmitting and executing requests, efficient prioritisation and timely execution, and safeguarding of information received. (p. 30)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "and for safeguarding the information received"</sub>

## A. OBJECTIVE

- **expected / requirement**: INR.6 requires all natural and legal persons in a country to freeze designated persons' and entities' funds or other assets without delay and without prior notice. (p. 50)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "without delay and without prior notice, the funds or other assets of designated persons and entities"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: The INR.6 freezing obligation covers all funds or assets owned or controlled by the designee, not only those tied to a particular terrorist act, plot or threat; those wholly or jointly, directly or indirectly owned or controlled; assets derived from them; and assets of persons acting on behalf of or at the direction of designees. (p. 50)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "and not just those that can be tied to a particular terrorist act, plot or threat"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.6 requires prohibiting nationals and persons in the jurisdiction from making funds, assets, economic resources or financial services available, directly or indirectly, wholly or jointly, to designees, entities they own or control, or persons acting for them, unless licensed, authorised or notified per UNSC resolutions. (p. 50)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "unless licensed, authorised or otherwise notified in accordance with the relevant Security Council resolutions"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: INR.6 footnote 15: resolution 2664 humanitarian provisions apply to all existing and future UNSC asset freezes absent an explicit contrary decision; for Afghanistan, paragraph 1 of resolution 2615 still applies to asset freezes under paragraph 1(a) of resolution 2255. (p. 50)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The provisions of resolution 2664 concerning humanitarian activities apply to all existing and future asset freezes"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: INR.6 footnote 16: for the EU, a supra-national jurisdiction under Recommendation 6, designees' assets are frozen by EU regulations; member states may need additional measures and all persons in the EU must respect the freeze. (p. 50)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The assets of designated persons and entities are frozen by the EU regulations and their amendments"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.6 requires countries, without prejudice to freezing obligations, to apply the provisions of the resolutions in para 5bis to UNSC-imposed asset freezes. (p. 50)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "countries should apply the provisions of the resolutions referenced in 5bis"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.6 requires mechanisms to communicate designations to the financial sector and DNFBPs immediately and provide clear guidance to those who may hold targeted assets on their freezing obligations. (p. 50)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "mechanisms for communicating designations to the financial"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.6 requires FIs and DNFBPs to report frozen assets and actions taken under UNSC prohibitions, including attempted transactions, and authorities to use this information effectively. (p. 50)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "any assets frozen or actions taken in compliance with the prohibition requirements"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: INR.6 footnote 18: Security Council resolutions apply to all natural and legal persons within the country. (p. 50)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Security Council resolutions apply to all natural and legal persons within the country"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.6 requires effective measures protecting rights of bona fide third parties acting in good faith when implementing Recommendation 6. (p. 51)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "protect the rights of bona fide third"</sub>
- **permissive / context**: INR.6 footnote 17: providing, processing or paying funds, assets or economic resources, or goods and services needed for timely humanitarian assistance or basic human needs, is permitted and not a breach of UNSC asset freezes, for actors in UNSCR 2664 paragraph 1 and providers under UNSCR 2615 paragraph 1. (p. 51)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "necessary to ensure the timely delivery of humanitarian assistance or to support other activities that support basic human needs"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.6 requires publicly known procedures to submit de-listing requests to the UNSC for 1267-regime designees that the country considers do not or no longer meet criteria; once the 1267 or 1988 Committee de-lists, the freezing obligation ends. (p. 51)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "publicly known procedures to submit de-listing requests to the Security Council"</sub>

## D. DE-LISTING, UNFREEZING AND PROVIDING ACCESS TO FROZEN FUNDS OR OTHER ASSETS

- **expected / requirement**: Countries must apply the humanitarian provisions when implementing UNSC sanctions and may also apply them to (supra-)national sanctions such as under UNSCR 1373. (p. 51)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Countries have the obligation to apply these provisions when they implement sanctions issued by the UN Security Council"</sub>
- **permissive / context**: Recommendation 6 does not prescribe how to comply with humanitarian provisions; approaches include exemptions, general licences, legislative carve-outs or other measures depending on the legal framework. (p. 51)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "exemptions, general licences, legislative carve-outs, or other measures"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Al-Qaida de-listing procedures should follow 1267 Committee procedures under resolutions 1730 (2006), 1735 (2006), 1822 (2008), 1904 (2009), 1989 (2011) and successors; Taliban/Afghanistan de-listing should follow 1988 Committee procedures under 1730, 1735, 1822, 1904, 1988 (2011) and successors. (p. 51)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "In the case of de-listing requests related to Al-Qaida"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: For resolution 1373 designees, INR.6 requires legal authority and procedures to delist and unfreeze those no longer meeting criteria, and procedures allowing review of designation decisions by a court or other independent authority on request. (p. 51)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "review of the designation decision before a court or other independent competent authority"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.6 requires publicly known procedures to unfreeze in a timely manner the assets of persons with the same or similar name as designees who are inadvertently frozen (false positives), once verified they are not designated. (p. 51)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "who are inadvertently affected by a freezing mechanism (i.e. a false positive)"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.6 requires authorising access to frozen funds of UNSC designees needed for basic expenses, certain fees and service charges, or extraordinary expenses per resolution 1452 (2002) and successors, and similarly for resolution 1373 designees as set out in resolution 1963 (2010). (p. 52)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "necessary for basic expenses, for the payment of certain types of fees, expenses and service charges"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.6 requires a mechanism for designees to challenge designation before a competent authority or court, and for Al-Qaida Sanctions List designees to be told of the UN Office of the Ombudsperson (resolution 1904 (2009)) for de-listing petitions. (p. 52)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the availability of the United Nations Office of the Ombudsperson"</sub>

## E. UNITED NATIONS DESIGNATION CRITERIA

- **expected / requirement**: INR.6 requires mechanisms to communicate de-listings and unfreezings to the financial sector and DNFBPs immediately, with adequate guidance on obligations to respect them. (p. 52)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "mechanisms for communicating de-listings and unfreezings to the financial sector and the DNFBPs"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: Designation criteria under resolutions 1267 (1999), 1989 (2011) and successors: any person or entity participating in financing, planning, facilitating, preparing or perpetrating acts of, supplying arms to, recruiting for or otherwise supporting Al-Qaida or any cell, affiliate, splinter group or derivative; and undertakings owned or controlled by such designees or persons acting for them. (p. 52)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Qaida, or any cell, affiliate, splinter group or derivative thereof20"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: Designation criteria under resolutions 1267 (1999), 1988 (2011) and successors: persons or entities financing, supporting, arming or recruiting for those associated with the Taliban in threatening the peace, stability and security of Afghanistan, and undertakings owned or controlled by such designees or persons acting for them. (p. 52)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "associated with the Taliban in constituting a threat to the peace, stability and security of Afghanistan"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: Designation criteria under resolution 1373 (2001): any person or entity committing, attempting, participating in or facilitating terrorist acts; entities owned or controlled by them; and persons or entities acting on their behalf or at their direction. (p. 52)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "any person or entity who commits or attempts to commit terrorist acts"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: INR.6 footnote 19 lists 1267 successor resolutions as of February 2012 as 1333 (2000), 1367 (2001), 1390 (2002), 1455 (2003), 1526 (2004), 1617 (2005), 1735 (2006), 1822 (2008), 1904 (2009), 1988 (2011) and 1989 (2011); footnote 20 notes OP2 of resolution 1617 (2005) further defines being "associated with" Al-Qaida or Usama bin Laden. (p. 53)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "OP2 of resolution 1617 (2005) further defines the criteria"</sub>

## A. OBJECTIVE

- **expected / requirement**: INR.7 requires implementing targeted financial sanctions freezing without delay, and prohibiting availability of, funds or assets of persons or entities designated by the UNSC under Chapter VII under resolutions on preventing and disrupting WMD proliferation financing. (p. 54)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Recommendation 7 requires countries to implement targeted financial sanctions21"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: INR.7 states Recommendation 7 does not replace other measures under WMD non-proliferation treaties or UNSC resolutions in criminal, civil or administrative proceedings; its focus is preventive measures to stop funds flowing to or being used by proliferators. (p. 54)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "stopping the flow of funds or other assets to proliferators or proliferation"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: INR.7 footnote 21: Recommendation 7 covers only targeted financial sanctions, while relevant UNSCRs also prescribe travel bans, activity-based financial prohibitions, category-based sanctions and vigilance measures; FATF has issued non-binding guidance jurisdictions are encouraged to consider. (p. 54)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "activity-based financial prohibitions, category-based sanctions and vigilance measures"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: INR.7 footnote 23 (October 2025): PF-related TFS resolutions are 1718 (2006), 1874 (2009), 2087 (2013), 2094 (2013), 2270 (2016), 2321 (2016) and 2356 (2017); Iran-related ones are 1737 (2006), 1747 (2007), 1803 (2008) and 1929 (2010); Recommendation 7 also applies to future successor resolutions. (p. 54)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "related to Iran are: resolutions 1737 (2006), 1747 (2007), 1803 (2008) and 1929 (2010)"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: INR.7 footnote 24: separate obligations exist under the Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty, Biological and Toxin Weapons Convention, Chemical Weapons Convention, and UNSCR 1540 (2004) and 2325 (2016). (p. 54)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the Biological and Toxin Weapons Convention, the Chemical Weapons Convention"</sub>

## B. DESIGNATIONS

- **descriptive / context**: PF designations are made by the UNSC in resolution annexes or by its Committees; Member States have no specific obligation to propose designations, though in practice the Committees primarily depend on Member State requests; resolutions 1718 (2006) and 1737 (2006) provide for Committee implementation guidelines. (p. 54)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "There is no specific obligation upon United Nations Member States to submit proposals for designations"</sub>
- **permissive / recommendation**: INR.7 says countries could consider establishing authority and procedures to propose PF designations, including a competent executive or judicial authority to propose names to the 1718 Sanctions Committee and the 1737 Sanctions Committee where it has sufficient evidence. (p. 54)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "proposing to the 1718 Sanctions Committee, for designation as appropriate"</sub>
- **permissive / recommendation**: INR.7 optional elements also include a mechanism to identify targets under resolutions 1718 and 1737 using a reasonable grounds or reasonable basis determination, and legal authority to collect as much information as possible from all relevant sources. (p. 55)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "whether reasonable grounds or a reasonable basis exists to propose a designation"</sub>
- **permissive / recommendation**: Under INR.7, proposing authorities apply their own legal standard considering human rights, rule of law and innocent third parties' rights, provide detailed identifying and supporting information, and may operate ex parte where necessary. (p. 55)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "taking into consideration human rights, respect for the rule of law, and in recognition"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: INR.7 footnote 25 (June 2017): successors to resolution 1718 (2006) are 1874 (2009), 2087 (2013), 2094 (2013), 2270 (2016), 2321 (2016) and 2356 (2017). (p. 55)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the successor resolutions to resolution 1718 (2006) are: resolution 1874 (2009)"</sub>

## C. FREEZING AND PROHIBITING DEALING IN FUNDS OR OTHER ASSETS OF DESIGNATED PERSONS AND ENTITIES

- **expected / requirement**: INR.7 obliges countries to implement TFS without delay against persons designated under resolution 1718 (2006) and successors by the UNSC or 1718 Committee, and under resolution 1737 (2006) by the UNSC or 1737 Committee acting under Chapter VII. (p. 56)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Council in annexes to the relevant resolutions, or by the 1718 Sanctions Committee of the Security Council26"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: INR.7 footnote 26: per resolution 2270 (2016) OP32, this also applies to DPRK Government or Worker's Party of Korea entities that countries determine are associated with DPRK nuclear or ballistic missile programmes or other prohibited activities. (p. 56)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "entities of the Government of the Democratic People’s Republic of Korea or the Worker’s Party of Korea"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: INR.7 footnote 27: in the EU, a supra-national jurisdiction under Recommendation 7, designees' assets are frozen under CFSP Council decisions and Council regulations; member states may need additional measures. (p. 56)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "frozen under EU Common Foreign and Security Policy (CFSP) Council decisions and Council regulations"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.7 requires all persons in the country to freeze PF designees' assets without delay and without prior notice, covering all owned or controlled assets (not just those tied to a specific proliferation act, plot or threat), jointly or indirectly held assets, derived assets, and assets of persons acting on their behalf. (p. 56)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "not just those that can be tied to a particular act, plot or threat of proliferation"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.7 requires preventing nationals and persons in the territory from making funds or assets available to PF designees unless licensed, authorised or notified per UNSC resolutions. (p. 57)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "available by their nationals or by any persons or entities within their territories"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.7 requires immediate communication of designations to FIs and DNFBPs with clear guidance, and requires FIs and DNFBPs to report frozen assets and actions taken including attempted transactions. (p. 57)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "including attempted transactions, and ensure that such information is effectively utilised by competent authorities"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.7 requires effective measures protecting bona fide third parties acting in good faith. (p. 57)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "parties acting in good faith when implementing the obligations under Recommendation 7"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.7 requires monitoring and ensuring FI and DNFBP compliance with Recommendation 7 obligations, with civil, administrative or criminal sanctions for non-compliance. (p. 57)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Failure to comply with such laws, or enforceable means should be subject to civil, administrative or criminal sanctions"</sub>

## INTERPRETIVE NOTES TO THE FATF RECOMMENDATIONS

- **descriptive / context**: The Interpretive Note to Recommendation 1 states the risk-based approach (RBA) is an effective way to combat ML and TF. (p. 32)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The risk-based approach (RBA) is an effective way to combat money laundering and terrorist financing"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: INR.1 says that in implementing the RBA in a sector countries should consider the sector's AML/CFT capacity and experience, since the discretion and responsibility the RBA gives FIs and DNFBPs is more appropriate in sectors with greater capacity and experience. (p. 32)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "is more appropriate in sectors with greater AML/CFT capacity and experience"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.1 states that lower sector capacity does not exempt FIs and DNFBPs from applying enhanced measures when they identify higher risk scenarios. (p. 32)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "This should not exempt financial institutions and DNFBPs from the requirement to apply enhanced measures"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.1 requires FIs and DNFBPs implementing the RBA to have processes to identify, assess, monitor, manage and mitigate ML and TF risks. (p. 32)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "should have in place processes to identify, assess, monitor, manage and mitigate money laundering and terrorist financing risks"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: The general RBA principle in INR.1: where risks are higher countries should require enhanced measures, and where lower they should allow and encourage simplified measures. (p. 32)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "where the risks are lower, countries should allow and encourage2 financial institutions and DNFBPs to take simplified measures"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.1 says countries should identify lower risk areas, e.g. through national or sub-national risk assessments, and provide guidance or information to FIs and DNFBPs on approaches to simplified measures. (p. 32)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Countries should identify areas of lower risk, for example, through their national or sub-national risk assessments"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: INR.1 footnote 2 explains encouragement of simplified measures can take the form of guidance from government, supervisor or other authority on when and how simplified measures may apply, or outreach and engagement. (p. 32)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "encouragement can take the form of guidance issued by the government, supervisor or other competent authority"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.1 prohibits simplified measures whenever there is a suspicion of ML or TF. (p. 32)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "simplified measures should not be permitted whenever there is a suspicion of money laundering or terrorist financing"</sub>
- **permissive / requirement**: INR.1 lets countries, in limited circumstances with assessed low ML/TF risk, decide not to apply certain Recommendations to a particular type of FI, activity or DNFBP. (p. 32)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "decide not to apply certain Recommendations to a particular type of financial institution or activity, or DNFBP"</sub>
- **recommended / recommendation**: INR.1 says that if risk assessments show that institutions, activities, businesses or professions outside the definitions of financial institution and DNFBP are at risk of ML/TF abuse, countries should consider applying AML/CFT requirements to those sectors. (p. 32)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "they should consider applying AML/CFT requirements to such sectors"</sub>

## ASSESSING PROLIFERATION FINANCING RISKS AND APPLYING RISK-BASED MEASURES

- **descriptive / definition**: INR.1 para 3 restates that "proliferation financing risk" refers strictly and only to potential breach, non-implementation or evasion of Recommendation 7 targeted financial sanctions obligations. (p. 33)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "“proliferation financing risk” refers strictly and only to the potential breach"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: INR.1 notes Recommendation 7 obligations impose strict, non-risk-based requirements on all natural and legal persons; risk-based PF measures by FIs and DNFBPs reinforce and complement full implementation by detecting and preventing non-implementation, breach or evasion. (p. 33)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "place strict requirements on all natural and legal persons, which are not risk-based"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: INR.1 says countries should consider sector PF risks when determining PF mitigation measures for a sector, so measures are proportionate and resources allocated effectively. (p. 33)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "countries should consider the proliferation financing risks associated with the relevant sector"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: INR.1 footnote 3: Recommendation 7 scope is limited to targeted financial sanctions and does not cover other UNSCR requirements; FATF PF requirements are limited to Recommendations 1, 2, 7 and 15, so Recommendation 1 PF obligations do not expand other Recommendations' scope. (p. 33)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The requirements of the FATF Standards relating to proliferation financing are limited to Recommendations 1, 2, 7 and 15 only"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.1 requires FIs and DNFBPs to have processes to identify, assess, monitor, manage and mitigate PF risks, which may sit within existing targeted financial sanctions and/or compliance programmes. (p. 33)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "This may be done within the framework of their existing targeted financial sanctions and/or compliance programmes"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.1 requires full implementation of Recommendation 7 in any risk scenario; higher PF risks require proportionate mitigation measures, and lower risks proportionate measures while still fully implementing targeted financial sanctions. (p. 33)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Countries should ensure full implementation of Recommendation 7 in any risk scenario"</sub>
- **permissive / requirement**: INR.1 footnote 4: countries may exempt a particular type of FI or DNFBP from PF risk identification and mitigation requirements if PF risk is assessed low, but full implementation of Recommendation 7 targeted financial sanctions is mandatory in all cases. (p. 33)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "full implementation of the targeted financial sanctions as required by Recommendation 7 is mandatory in all cases"</sub>

## ML/TF risks

- **expected / requirement**: INR.1 para 5 requires countries to identify and assess ML/TF risks on an ongoing basis to inform AML/CFT regime changes (laws, regulations, other measures), allocate and prioritise resources proportionately, and inform FI and DNFBP risk assessments. (p. 34)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "inform potential changes to the country’s AML/CFT regime, including changes to laws, regulations and other measures"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.1 requires countries to keep ML/TF assessments up to date and have mechanisms to share results with competent authorities, SRBs, FIs and DNFBPs. (p. 34)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Countries should keep the assessments up-to-date, and should have mechanisms to provide appropriate information on the results"</sub>
- **expected / context**: INR.1 footnote 5: where appropriate, supra-national AML/CFT risk assessments should be considered when judging whether the country risk assessment obligation is met. (p. 34)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "AML/CFT risk assessments at a supra-national level should be taken into account"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.1 para 6: where countries identify higher risks they should either prescribe enhanced measures for FIs and DNFBPs or ensure the information is incorporated into their risk assessments. (p. 34)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "either prescribe that financial institutions and DNFBPs take enhanced measures to manage and mitigate the risks"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: INR.1 para 6: where the Recommendations identify higher risk activities requiring enhanced or specific measures, all such measures must be applied, though their extent may vary with risk level. (p. 34)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "all such measures must be applied, although the extent of such measures may vary"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.1 para 7: countries should allow and encourage simplified measures where lower risk has been identified with due regard to the national risk assessment. (p. 34)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "provided that a lower risk has been identified with due regard to the country’s assessment"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.1 para 7: independent of any country-specified lower risk categories, countries should allow and encourage FIs and DNFBPs to apply simplified measures subject to section B obligations and paragraph 9. (p. 34)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Independent of any decision by a country to specify certain lower risk categories"</sub>
- **permissive / requirement**: INR.1 para 8(a): countries may exempt from some Recommendations where ML/TF risk is assessed low, in limited and justified circumstances, relating to a particular type of FI, activity or DNFBP. (p. 34)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "there is an assessed low risk of money laundering and terrorist financing; the exemption"</sub>
- **permissive / requirement**: INR.1 para 8(b): exemption is also possible where a financial activity other than money or value transfer is carried out occasionally or on a very limited basis (by quantitative and absolute criteria) such that ML/TF risk is low. (p. 34)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "a financial activity (other than the transferring of money or value) is carried out by a"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.1 states that although information gathered may vary with risk, Recommendation 11 retention requirements apply to whatever information is gathered. (p. 35)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the requirements of Recommendation 11 to retain information should apply to whatever information is gathered"</sub>

## PF risk

- **expected / requirement**: INR.1 para 9: supervisors (or SRBs for DNFBPs) should ensure FIs and DNFBPs implement obligations effectively and proportionately, and review their ML/TF risk profiles, assessments and mitigation measures per INR.26 and INR.28, taking results into account. (p. 35)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "review the money laundering and terrorist financing risk profiles, risk assessments and risk mitigation measures"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.1 para 10 requires countries to identify and assess PF risks on an ongoing basis to inform CPF regime changes, allocate CPF resources and inform FI and DNFBP PF risk assessments, keeping them up to date and sharing results; supra-national PF assessments should be considered where appropriate. (p. 35)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "inform potential changes to the country’s CPF regime"</sub>

## B. Obligations and decisions for financial institutions and DNFBPs

- **expected / requirement**: INR.1 para 11 requires countries to manage and mitigate identified PF risks and develop an understanding of how targeted financial sanctions may be breached, evaded or not implemented, shareable among authorities and with the private sector. (p. 35)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "develop an understanding of the means of potential breaches, evasion and non-implementation of targeted financial sanctions"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.1 para 11 requires countries to ensure FIs and DNFBPs identify circumstances presenting higher PF risks and that the CPF regime addresses them. (p. 35)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "take steps to identify circumstances, which may present higher risks"</sub>

## ML/TF risks

- **expected / requirement**: INR.1 para 12 requires FIs and DNFBPs to identify and assess ML/TF risks for customers, countries or geographic areas, and products, services, transactions or delivery channels. (p. 35)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "for customers, countries or geographic areas; and products, services, transactions or delivery channels"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.1 para 12 requires FIs and DNFBPs to document risk assessments to demonstrate their basis, keep them up to date, and be able to provide them to competent authorities and SRBs. (p. 35)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "They should document those assessments in order to be able to demonstrate their basis"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.1 requires ML/TF risk assessment to be proportionate to business nature and size; FIs and DNFBPs must always understand their risks, but authorities or SRBs may waive individual documented assessments where sector risks are clearly identified and understood. (p. 36)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "may determine that individual documented risk assessments are not required"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.1 para 13 requires FIs and DNFBPs to have senior management-approved policies, controls and procedures to manage and mitigate identified risks, monitor their implementation, and apply measures proportionate to risk and consistent with national requirements and guidance. (p. 36)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The policies, controls and procedures should be approved by senior management"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.1 paras 14-15: where higher risks are identified FIs and DNFBPs must take enhanced measures; where lower risks are identified they should be allowed and encouraged to take simplified measures. (p. 36)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Where higher risks are identified financial institutions and DNFBPs should be required to take enhanced measures"</sub>

## PF risk

- **expected / expectation**: INR.1 para 16: FIs and DNFBPs should consider all relevant risk factors before determining overall risk and mitigation level. (p. 36)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "should consider all the relevant risk factors before determining what is the level of overall risk"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.1 para 17 requires FIs and DNFBPs to identify and assess PF risks (possibly within sanctions/compliance programmes), document and update assessments, provide them to authorities, and scale them to business size; documented assessments may be waived where sector risks are clearly understood. (p. 36)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "should be required to take appropriate steps, to identify and assess their proliferation financing risks"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.1 para 18 requires FIs and DNFBPs to have senior management-approved PF risk policies, controls and procedures, monitor and enhance them as needed, consistent with national requirements and guidance. (p. 36)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "They should be required to monitor the implementation of those controls and to enhance them, if necessary"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: INR.1 para 18: for higher PF risks, countries should require proportionate measures such as enhanced controls to detect possible breaches, non-implementation or evasion of Recommendation 7 sanctions. (p. 36)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "introducing enhanced controls aimed at detecting possible breaches, non-implementation or evasion"</sub>

## Glossary of specific terms used in the context of this Recommendation

- **descriptive / definition**: In the FATF RBA context, a proportionate or commensurate measure is one that appropriately corresponds to the identified risk level and effectively mitigates the risks. (p. 36)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "is one that appropriately corresponds to the level of identified risk and effectively mitigates the risks"</sub>

## PF risk

- **recommended / recommendation**: INR.1 paragraph 16 says financial institutions and DNFBPs should consider differentiating the extent of their measures by the type and level of risk for each risk factor; for example, in a given situation they could apply normal CDD at customer acceptance but simplified or enhanced CDD for ongoing monitoring, or the reverse. (p. 36)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "they could apply normal CDD for customer acceptance measures, but simplified or enhanced CDD for ongoing monitoring"</sub>

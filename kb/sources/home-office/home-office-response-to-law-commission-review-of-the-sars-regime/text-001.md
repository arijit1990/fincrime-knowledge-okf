## Response to Law Commission review of the SARs regime


[p. 1]

The government's response to the Law Commission's review of the suspicious activity reports (SARs) regime.

From:

Home Office

Published:

12 February 2024

HTML

In 2017, the Home Office commissioned the Law Commission to review and provide recommendations on the suspicious activity reports ( SARs ) regime, and consider whether there is scope for reform.

In 2019, the Law Commission published its report, titled ‘Anti-money laundering: the SARs regime’ .

The Home Office accepts or partially accepts 13 of the 19 recommendations in the report.

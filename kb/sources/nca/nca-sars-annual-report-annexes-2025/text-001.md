SARs ANNUAL REPORT ANNEXES

Suspicious Activity Reports April 2024 - March 2025

In 2024-25 the UKFIU received 866,616 SARs.

The tables below give a breakdown by industry sector of the SARs submitted in 2024-25 via SAR Online (including bulk CSV submissions) and the new SAR Portal (including bulk API submissions).

The updated sector categories used by the new SAR Portal differ from those used by SAR Online. It is not possible to make direct comparisons at the sector-level between submissions made via SAR Online and those made via the new SAR Portal.

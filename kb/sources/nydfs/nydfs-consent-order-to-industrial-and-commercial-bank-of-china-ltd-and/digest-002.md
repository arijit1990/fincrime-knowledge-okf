## Breach of Consent Order

- **mandatory / requirement**: If the Department believes the Bank is in material breach, the Department will give written notice, and the Bank must, within ten (10) days of receiving it (or a later date at the Department's sole discretion), appear before the Department to show that no material breach occurred, or that the breach is not material or has been cured. (p. 13)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the Bank must, within ten (10) days of receiving such notice, or on a later date if so determined"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: Failure to make the required showing within the designated period is presumptive evidence of the Bank's breach. (p. 13)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "failure to make the required showing"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: Upon a finding of breach, the Department has all remedies available under the New York Banking Law and other applicable laws, and may use any evidence available to it in ensuing hearings, notices or orders. (p. 13)[^original]

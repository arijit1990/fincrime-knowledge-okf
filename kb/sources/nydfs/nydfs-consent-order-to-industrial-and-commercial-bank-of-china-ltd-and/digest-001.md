## WHEREAS

- **descriptive / context**: ICBC is a global financial institution with more than ten million corporate customers and about 720 million individual customers, operating in 49 countries, with about $5.7 trillion in assets as of 31 December 2022. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "more than ten million corporate customers and approximately 720 million individual customers"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The ICBC New York Branch had about $22.2 billion in assets as of 31 March 2023 and cleared more than $5 trillion in U.S. dollar transactions in 2022. (p. 2)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "had approximately $22.2 billion in assets as of March 31, 2023"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: The New York Branch experienced multiple compliance deficiencies over several years and engaged in specific instances of misconduct and mishandling of confidential supervisory information. (p. 2)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "experienced multiple compliance deficiencies for several years in a row"</sub>

## Introduction

- **descriptive / context**: ICBC is a state-owned, publicly traded commercial bank incorporated in the People's Republic of China and has operated the New York Branch since 2008. (p. 2)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "ICBC has operated the New York Branch since 2008"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The New York Branch is licensed by the Department and is subject to supervisory examinations by the Department and the FRBNY. (p. 2)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "is subject to supervisory examinations by the Department and the Federal Reserve Bank of New York"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: The Department found that the Bank let deficiencies in the New York Branch's BSA/AML and OFAC sanctions screening programs persist over several examination cycles, and also identified backdating of books and records in 2015 and disclosure of confidential supervisory information. (p. 2)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "permitted deficiencies in the New York Branch's Bank Secrecy Act/Anti-Money Laundering"</sub>

## Events at Issue

- **descriptive / context**: Periodic supervisory examinations of the New York Branch, typically joint by the Department and FRBNY or alternating, review risk management, BSA/AML compliance, transaction monitoring and OFAC screening. (p. 3)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "such examinations review a bank's risk management, BSA/AML compliance program, transaction monitoring"</sub>

## Long-Standing Weaknesses in BSA/AML and OFAC Screening Compliance

- **descriptive / context**: On 12 March 2018 the Federal Reserve Board entered a Cease and Desist Order with ICBC and the New York Branch to address significant BSA/AML and OFAC deficiencies, directing remediation in corporate governance and management oversight, customer due diligence, and suspicious activity monitoring and reporting. (p. 3)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "to address significant deficiencies in the New York Branch's compliance with BSA/AML requirements and OFAC regulations"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: After the C&D Order, the cited BSA/AML and OFAC deficiencies persisted for several more years through repeated examination cycles. (p. 3)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the deficiencies cited in the New York Branch's BSA/AML and OFAC compliance programs persisted for several more years"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: The 2022 joint examination found the New York Branch's OFAC program adequate but its BSA/AML program still deficient and requiring additional enhancements. (p. 3)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "its BSA/AML compliance program continued to have deficiencies and required additional enhancements"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: The 2023 FRBNY targeted examination found both programs in compliance but said additional improvements were still necessary. (p. 3)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "found that both programs were in compliance, but additional improvements were still necessary"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: The Department's most recent 2023 examination acknowledged significant enhancement efforts and remediation of all prior findings, and rated the BSA/AML and OFAC programs adequate due to significant improvements. (p. 4)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "were adequate due to significant improvements over the past several years"</sub>

## Document Backdating and Reporting Failure

- **mandatory / requirement**: Foreign banking corporations licensed by the Department are required to maintain accurate books, accounts and records under New York Banking Law § 200-c. (p. 4)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "are required to maintain accurate books, accounts, and records"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: Under 3 NYCRR § 300.1(a) the Bank must submit a report to the superintendent immediately upon discovering embezzlement, fraud, false entries, omission of true entries or other misconduct involving directors, officers, employees (excluding tellers) or agents. (p. 4)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "submit a report to the superintendent immediately upon the discovery of"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The internal KYC policy required the New York Branch to periodically obtain banks' USA PATRIOT Act certifications, which a branch staff member, typically the relationship manager, had to acknowledge and countersign; this signature requirement was internal policy, not statutory or regulatory. (p. 4)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "was part of the New York Branch's internal policy and was not a statutory or regulatory requirement"</sub>
- **descriptive / case example**: In August 2015 a senior employee learned that certain clients' certifications lacked branch countersignatures, and the relationship manager for those 2014 certifications had left the branch. (p. 5)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the individual who had been the relationship manager for the clients at the times the signed Certifications were due"</sub>
- **descriptive / case example**: The senior employee asked a former employee to countersign as relationship manager and supplied 2014 dates; the former employee signed and backdated certifications for five banking clients and returned them. (p. 5)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "signing, backdating, and returning to the Bank signed copies of the Certifications for five different banking clients"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: The Bank asserted the backdated certifications were not included in clients' KYC files, but the Department found the conduct violated the branch's obligation to maintain appropriate books and records under New York Banking Law § 200-c. (p. 5)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the conduct by the senior employee constituted a violation of the New York Branch's obligation"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: An employee raised the issue internally by January 2017; an April 2017 internal investigation confirmed backdating, but the Department only learned of it in January 2018. (p. 5)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the Department only learned about the backdated certifications in January 2018"</sub>

## Events at Issue

- **descriptive / case example**: In October 2021, the Overseas Affiliate forwarded the questionnaire to a senior staff member at the New York Branch, who consulted the transferring employee, a senior compliance member, and Branch counsel. (p. 5)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the Overseas Affiliate forwarded the questionnaire to a senior staff member at the New York Branch"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: The Department stated that the backdated Certifications plainly constituted false entries, which should have been reported to the Superintendent immediately upon discovery under 3 NYCRR § 300.1, but the Department only learned of them in January 2018. (p. 5)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The making of the backdated Certifications plainly constituted false entries"</sub>

## Disclosure of Confidential Supervisory Information

- **mandatory / definition**: Under New York Banking Law § 36(10), reports and correspondence arising from examinations and investigations are confidential communications, termed confidential supervisory information (CSI). (p. 6)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "shall be confidential communications"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: Under 3 NYCRR § 7.2 a regulated entity shall not disclose CSI to any person without the Department's prior written approval and subject to any conditions the Department imposes. (p. 6)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "shall not disclose any [CSI] to any person without the prior written approval of the department"</sub>
- **descriptive / case example**: In September 2021 the Bank sought to transfer a New York Branch employee to an overseas ICBC affiliate, which required approval from that affiliate's regulator, whose questionnaire asked about regulatory or disciplinary investigations. (p. 6)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The transfer required approval from a regulator where the Overseas Affiliate was located"</sub>
- **descriptive / case example**: Branch counsel advised in an email that answers about the ongoing investigations would be CSI, so the branch's regulators would need to approve disclosure. (p. 6)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "responses about the ongoing investigations would constitute CSI and, therefore"</sub>
- **descriptive / case example**: In November 2021, without authorization from the Department and the FRB, the branch sent proposed language and CSI-containing documents to the overseas affiliate, which passed them to its local regulator. (p. 7)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "without proper authorization from the Department and the FRB, the Branch sent the original proposed language"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: Branch counsel learned of the CSI breach in early December 2021 and the branch reported it to the Department and FRB two weeks later. (p. 7)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "New York Branch counsel learned of this CSI breach in early December 2021"</sub>

## Violations of Law and Regulations

- **descriptive / finding or result**: The Bank is found to have failed to maintain an effective and compliant anti-money laundering program at the branch, in violation of 3 NYCRR § 116.2. (p. 7)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "failed to maintain an effective and compliant anti-money laundering program"</sub>

## Events at Issue

- **descriptive / case example**: In early November 2021, Bank counsel approached the Department, the FRB, and the FRBNY and provided background and proposed language for the Overseas Affiliate's regulator. (p. 7)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Bank counsel approached the Department, the FRB, and the FRBNY and provided the background"</sub>

## Violations of Law and Regulations

- **descriptive / finding or result**: The Bank is found to have failed to maintain appropriate books, accounts and records at the branch, in violation of New York Banking Law § 200-c. (p. 8)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "failed to maintain appropriate books, accounts, and records at the New York Branch"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: The branch failed to report immediately the discovery of false entries and other misconduct, in violation of 3 NYCRR § 300.1. (p. 8)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "failed to submit a report to the Superintendent"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: The branch shared CSI, in violation of New York Banking Law § 36(10) and 3 NYCRR § 7.2. (p. 8)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The New York Branch shared confidential supervisory information"</sub>

## Monetary Penalty

- **mandatory / requirement**: No later than ten days after the Effective Date, the Bank shall pay a civil monetary penalty of $30,000,000 under New York Banking Law §§ 39 and 44 by wire transfer per Department instructions. (p. 8)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the Bank shall pay a total civil monetary penalty pursuant to New York Banking Law §§ 39 and 44"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: The Bank shall not claim or apply for any tax deduction or credit for any portion of the civil monetary penalty. (p. 8)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "shall not claim, assert, or apply for a tax deduction or tax credit"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: The Bank shall neither seek nor accept reimbursement or indemnification for the penalty, including through any insurance policy. (p. 8)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "shall neither seek nor accept, directly or indirectly, reimbursement or indemnification"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: The Department recognises the branch's ongoing remediation, including substantial BSA/AML and OFAC improvements and a prompt response to the CSI disclosure. (p. 8)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the New York Branch has made substantial improvements to its BSA/AML and OFAC programs"</sub>

## Remediation - Status Reports

- **mandatory / requirement**: Within sixty days of execution, the Bank shall submit a BSA/AML status report acceptable to the Department on planned, underway or implemented changes since the 2021 examination. (p. 9)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Within sixty (60) days of the execution of this Consent Order, the Bank shall submit a status report"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: The BSA/AML status report must cover a system of internal controls reasonably designed to ensure BSA/AML and state law compliance. (p. 9)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "A system of internal controls reasonably designed to ensure compliance with BSA/AML requirements"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: The status report must cover controls reasonably designed to ensure compliance with all requirements for correspondent accounts for foreign financial institutions. (p. 9)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Controls reasonably designed to ensure compliance with all requirements relating to correspondent accounts"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: The status report must show BSA/AML management by a qualified compliance officer with full autonomy, independence and responsibility, commensurate with the branch's size and risk profile, supported by adequate staffing and resources. (p. 9)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "who is given full autonomy, independence, and responsibility for implementing and maintaining"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: The status report must identify management information systems used for BSA/AML compliance and a timeline to review key systems for configuration to mitigate risks. (p. 9)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Identification of management information systems used to achieve compliance with BSA/AML requirements"</sub>

## Remediation

- **descriptive / finding or result**: The Department recognises that the New York Branch responded promptly to the unauthorized CSI disclosure and took steps to prevent such disclosure from recurring. (p. 9)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "took steps to prevent such disclosure from occurring again"</sub>

  <sub>anchor: "Comprehensive and timely independent testing for the New York Branch's compliance"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: The status report must cover effective training for appropriate branch and ICBC personnel performing BSA/AML compliance functions in BSA/AML requirements, state laws and internal policies. (p. 10)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Effective training for all appropriate New York Branch personnel and appropriate ICBC personnel"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: Within sixty days, the Bank shall submit a status report on the sustainable governance framework covering board actions to maintain effective control and oversight of branch management's BSA/AML compliance. (p. 10)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Actions the board of directors has taken and will continue to take to maintain effective control"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: The governance status report must cover improved management information reporting on BSA/AML compliance to senior management of ICBC and the branch. (p. 10)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Measures to improve the management information systems reporting regarding"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: The governance status report must define clearly the roles, responsibilities and accountability of ICBC's and the branch's management, compliance and internal audit staff. (p. 10)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Clearly defined roles, responsibilities, and accountability regarding compliance"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: The governance status report must include measures to ensure BSA/AML issues are appropriately tracked, escalated and reviewed by branch senior management. (p. 10)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Measures to ensure BSA/AML issues are appropriately tracked, escalated, and reviewed"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: The governance status report must include measures ensuring overseers at ICBC and the branch have appropriate subject matter expertise and are actively involved. (p. 10)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "possess appropriate subject matter expertise and are actively involved in carrying out such responsibilities"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: The governance status report must address adequate resources to ensure compliance with the Order, BSA/AML requirements and state laws. (p. 10)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Adequate resources to ensure the New York Branch's compliance with this Order"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: The governance status report must address an appropriate and effective reporting structure allowing the BSA/AML compliance officer to report timely and completely to the Board or a committee. (p. 10)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "An appropriate and effective reporting structure that permits the New York Branch's BSA/AML compliance officer"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: Within sixty days, the Bank shall submit a customer due diligence status report covering enhancements to the branch's customer due diligence program. (p. 11)[^original]

- **mandatory / requirement**: The customer due diligence report must cover policies, procedures and controls ensuring the branch collects, analyses and retains complete and accurate information for all account holders, including affiliates. (p. 11)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "collects, analyzes, and retains complete and accurate customer information for all account holders"</sub>

## Settlement Provisions > Monetary Penalty

- **mandatory / requirement**: Block must pay a civil monetary penalty of USD 40,000,000 to the Department by wire transfer within ten days after the Effective Date, under NYBL § 44(1)(c). (p. 13)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "No later than ten (10) days after the Effective Date (as defined below) of this Consent Order, the Company shall pay a total civil monetary penalty"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: The penalty payment must be made by wire transfer following instructions provided by the Department. (p. 13)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The payment shall be in the form of a wire transfer in accordance with instructions provided by the Department."</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: Block must not claim, assert or apply for any US federal, state or local tax deduction or credit, directly or indirectly, for any part of the penalty. (p. 13)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The Company shall not claim, assert, or apply for a tax deduction or tax credit"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: Block must neither seek nor accept reimbursement or indemnification for the penalty, including payment under any insurance policy. (p. 13)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The Company shall neither seek nor accept, directly or indirectly, reimbursement or indemnification with respect to payment of the penalty amount"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: In setting the penalty, the Department considered the entity's cooperation in the investigation, the gravity of the violations, and other matters justice and the public interest may require. (p. 13)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the extent to which the entity has cooperated with the Department in the investigation of such conduct, the gravity of the violations"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: The Department acknowledges Block's cooperation throughout the investigation and credits its ongoing remediation efforts. (p. 13)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The Department acknowledges Block’s cooperation throughout this investigation."</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: Block's remediation included significant financial and other resources devoted to resolving backlogs and mitigating future backlog risk. (p. 13)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "devoting significant financial and other resources to resolving backlogs and mitigating the risk of future backlogs"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: Block's remediation included increasing compliance staffing, limiting the number of accounts a customer can create, adding controls against bad actors accessing or returning to the platform, and updating its BSA/AML and OFAC compliance practices. (p. 13)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "increasing compliance staffing resources, limiting the number of accounts a customer can create"</sub>

## Settlement Provisions > Independent Monitor

- **mandatory / requirement**: Block agrees to engage an Independent Monitor selected by the Department for twelve months from execution of the Consent Order, extendable at the Department's sole regulatory discretion. (p. 15)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The Company agrees to engage the services of an Independent Monitor selected by the Department for a period of twelve (12) months"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The primary objective of the Independent Monitor is to inform and enhance Block's efforts to remediate deficiencies in its compliance programs. (p. 15)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The primary objective of the engagement of the Independent Monitor is to inform and enhance the Company’s efforts"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: The Independent Monitor's monitoring must be based on the findings and violations in the Consent Order. (p. 15)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The Independent Monitor shall base its monitoring on the findings and violations contained in this Consent Order."</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: The Monitor must commence a comprehensive review of the effectiveness of Block's BSA/AML and Sanctions programs, focused primarily on current programs (the Compliance Review), prepare a written Compliance Report of findings, conclusions and recommendations, and oversee remediation as deemed appropriate with the Department. (p. 15)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "commence a comprehensive review of the effectiveness of Block’s BSA/AML and Sanctions programs"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: The Compliance Review and Report must at minimum include a comprehensive, risk-based assessment of compliance with the Virtual Currency Regulation, Money Transmitter Regulation and Part 504 (the Transaction Monitoring Regulation). (p. 15)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "A comprehensive, risk-based assessment of Block’s compliance with the Virtual Currency Regulation"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: The Monitor must review Block's suspicious activity identification, investigation, escalation, tracking, documentation and reporting procedures to determine whether it meets its suspicious activity reporting requirements under 23 NYCRR § 200.15, 3 NYCRR § 417.2 and 3 NYCRR § 504.3. (p. 16)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "A review of Block’s suspicious activity identification, investigation, escalation, tracking, documentation, and reporting procedures"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: The Monitor must review the adequacy of planned or implemented corrective measures to prevent the types of historical transaction monitoring alert backlogs referenced in the Consent Order. (p. 16)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "A review of the adequacy of any planned or implemented corrective measures to prevent the types of historical transaction monitoring alert backlogs"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: The Monitor must review, using its own expertise, a sample of Block's transactions to determine whether OFAC-inconsistent, suspicious high-risk or illicit-finance transactions were properly identified and reported; the Monitor may access any transactions it deems appropriate. (p. 16)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "A review of a sample, based on the Independent Monitor’s expertise, of Block’s transaction activity"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: The Monitor must review Block's Blockchain Analytics Transaction Monitoring Program for compliance with BSA/AML, OFAC, Part 504 and the Department's April 28, 2022 Guidance on Use of Blockchain Analytics. (p. 16)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the Department’s April 28, 2022 “Guidance on Use of Blockchain Analytics.”"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: The Independent Monitor must, as it deems necessary, have system access to all historical data and transactions at the Company from January 1, 2021 to the present. (p. 16)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The Independent Monitor shall, as it deems necessary, have system access to all historical data and transactions"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: The Monitor's report must summarise remediation completed and evaluate the compliance program further, including recommendations for remaining remediation, if any. (p. 17)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The Independent Monitor’s report to the Department will summarize the"</sub>

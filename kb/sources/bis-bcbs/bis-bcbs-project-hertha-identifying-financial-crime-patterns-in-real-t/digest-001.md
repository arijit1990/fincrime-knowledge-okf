## Project Hertha: identifying financial crime patterns in real-time retail payment systems

- **descriptive / context**: Criminals evade detection by operating in complex networks of many accounts across multiple financial institutions; electronic payment systems process transactions across many participants, giving a network-wide view, and Hertha tested modern AI techniques to spot complex, coordinated criminal activity in payment system data. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "criminals operate in complex networks which include many accounts across multiple financial institutions"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: Project Hertha found payment system analytics could be a valuable supplementary tool to help banks and PSPs spot suspicious activity. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "payment system analytics could be a valuable supplementary tool"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: Using findings from payment system analytics helped banks and PSPs find 12% more illicit accounts than they would otherwise have found. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "helped banks and PSPs find 12% more illicit accounts than they would otherwise have found"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: Payment system analytics proved particularly valuable for spotting novel financial crime patterns, achieving a 26% improvement when trying to spot previously unseen behaviours. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "When trying to spot previously unseen behaviours, it helped achieve a 26% improvement"</sub>
- **descriptive / data or feature**: Experiments used a simulated synthetic transaction dataset developed in the project, containing 1.8 million bank accounts and 308 million transactions, built using an advanced AI model trained to simulate realistic transaction patterns; no real customer data was used, and the dataset was designed to represent an ecosystem of retail payments in a single jurisdiction. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "It includes data for 1.8 million bank accounts and 308 million transactions"</sub>
- **descriptive / limitation**: Results show promise but also limits to the application and effectiveness of payment system analytics, which is just one piece of the puzzle; introducing a similar solution would raise complex practical, legal and regulatory issues, whose analysis was beyond the project's scope. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The introduction of a similar solution would also raise complex practical, legal and regulatory issues"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: The results highlight the importance of labelled training data, a robust model feedback loop and explainable AI algorithms to maximise effectiveness. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "importance of labelled training data, robust model feedback loop and explainable AI algorithms"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Building on the findings of Project Aurora, the BIS Innovation Hub's Project Hertha explored how transaction analytics could help identify financial crime patterns in real-time retail payment systems while using only the minimum set of data points. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "while using the minimum set of data points"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Project Hertha is a collaboration between the Innovation Hub's London Centre and the Bank of England. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The project is a collaboration between the Innovation Hub's London Centre and the Bank of England."</sub>

## A primer on the transformer architecture

- **descriptive / context**: The transformer architecture has been a breakthrough in natural language processing, laying the foundation for advanced LLMs such as BERT (Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers) and GPT (Generative Pre-trained Transformer). (p. 8)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "has been a breakthrough in natural language processing (NLP), laying the foundation for the development of advanced large language models"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: At the heart of the transformer architecture are two innovations: multi-headed attention and positional encoding. (p. 8)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "At the heart of the transformer architecture are two innovations: “multi-headed attention” and “positional encoding”."</sub>
- **descriptive / method**: Multi-headed attention allows several parallel neural networks to capture different meanings for the same word, for example discerning the different meanings of "bank" in "She sat on the bank of the river" versus "She went to the bank to deposit money". (p. 8)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "it can discern the different meanings of “bank” in “She sat on the bank of the river”"</sub>
- **descriptive / method**: The attention mechanism computes dot products of the input query vector (for example "bank") with key vectors (each word in the text), resulting in a score matrix. (p. 8)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The attention mechanism computes dot products of the input query vector (“bank”) with key vectors"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: The ability to interpret the meaning of words based on much wider contexts is a significant advance over previous NLP models. (p. 8)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "This ability to interpret the meaning of words based on much wider contexts is a significant advance over previous"</sub>
- **descriptive / method**: Positional encoding enables transformers to process data concurrently rather than sequentially, setting them apart from earlier neural network models such as Recurrent Neural Networks and Long Short-Term Memory. (p. 8)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Positional encoding enables transformers to process data concurrently rather than sequentially."</sub>
- **descriptive / limitation**: Sequential models are slow to train, memory-consuming and suffer from the vanishing gradient problem, in which the signals carrying information on how to update weights eventually become too weak to effectively train deep layers. (p. 8)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Sequential models are slow to train and memory-consuming, and they suffer from the so-called vanishing gradient problem"</sub>
- **descriptive / method**: By embedding each word with positional information, transformers preserve the sequence of words and the model can be parallelised during training, allowing training with more data and the building of bigger models. (p. 8)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "By embedding each word with positional information, transformers preserve the sequence of words"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: Transformers can accurately differentiate between sentences like "Inflation causes a rate hike" and "A rate hike causes inflation", where word order determines meaning. (p. 8)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "differentiate between sentences like “Inflation causes a rate hike” and “A rate hike causes inflation”"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: The integration of multi-headed attention and positional encoding has significantly enhanced the performance of language models, giving superior accuracy and fluency in a variety of NLP tasks. (p. 8)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The integration of multi-headed attention and positional encoding has significantly enhanced the performance of language models."</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The parallel computation capabilities of transformers, facilitated by advances in graphics processing unit technology, enable rapid processing of vast data sets and complex linguistic structure. (p. 8)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The parallel computation capabilities of transformers, facilitated by advances in graphics processing unit technology"</sub>
- **descriptive / method**: Through attention, a model interprets every word in a text in relation to all the other words, which improves how well it accounts for context and for relationships within the text. (p. 8)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Attention allows each word in a text to be understood in relation to every other word"</sub>
- **descriptive / method**: In the attention mechanism the dot products of the query vector with the key vectors yield a score matrix, from which attention weights are derived; these weights gauge how similar the query and key vectors are, and combining the information into a value vector lets the model link each word to the most relevant parts of the query vector. (p. 8)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "This matrix is then used to obtain so-called attention weights, which measure the similarity between the query and key vectors."</sub>

- **descriptive / definition**: Annex E defines an ECID (encrypted comparable identifier) as a code or other means of identification allowing individuals to be tracked across data and systems without revealing their identity. (p. 94)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "to allow individuals to be tracked across data and systems without revealing"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: Annex E defines the F1 score as a statistical measure of model accuracy and overall performance, represented by a combination of precision and recall. (p. 94)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The F1 score is used in statistical analysis to measure the accuracy and overallperformance of a model"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: Annex E defines false negatives as a metric showing the number of illicit transactions that have not been flagged as suspicious by the model, and false positives as a metric showing the number of persons or transactions falsely classified as positive. (p. 94)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "A metric used for statistical analysis that shows the number of persons ortransactions that have been falselyclassified aspositive"</sub>

  <sub>anchor: "True positive/(true positive + false positive)"</sub>

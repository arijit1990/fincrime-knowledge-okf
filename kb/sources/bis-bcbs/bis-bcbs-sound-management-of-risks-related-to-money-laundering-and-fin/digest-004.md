- **expected / requirement**: Annex 4 Table 1 (continued): the minimum also includes nationality, an official personal identification number or other unique identifier, and date and place of birth; potential additional information, on the basis of risks, comprises business address, post office box number, e-mail address and landline or mobile telephone numbers, residency status and gender. (p. 41)[^original]

- **expected / limitation**: Annex 4 Table 1 notes: (a) not all the information may be required in lower-risk situations where simplified due diligence applies, and the list excludes non-AML/CFT basics such as collecting signatures; (b) some information (residential address, identifier, date and place of birth) may be legitimately unavailable, which could prevent clients accessing formal banking, and if they are allowed access banks should apply mitigating measures per internal risk policies and national law, such as alternative information or appropriate monitoring; (c) collection of residency status and gender may be subject to national data protection and privacy regimes.

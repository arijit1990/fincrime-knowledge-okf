## Annex 1 - I. Introduction

## Annex 1 - Reliance on third parties

- **descriptive / context**: Annex 1 paragraph 2: in some countries banks may rely on CDD by other financial institutions or designated non-financial businesses and professions that are themselves supervised or monitored for AML/CFT; the third party usually has an existing relationship with the customer and the bank may be exempt from applying its own CDD at the start; FATF standards permit reliance for (a) identifying the customer and verifying identity using reliable, independent source documents, data or information; (b) identifying the beneficial owner and taking reasonable measures to verify identity, including understanding ownership and control structure for legal persons and arrangements; and (c) understanding and, as appropriate, obtaining information on the purpose and intended nature of the business relationship. ## Annex 1 - III. Outsourcing/agency

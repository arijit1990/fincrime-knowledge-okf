## E.3 Cooperation and information exchange between supervisors and third parties

- **expected / expectation**: Annex 5 paragraph 32: information relevant to prudential and AML/CFT supervisors may be held by third parties domestically or abroad (eg FIUs or law enforcement agencies); in line with FATF Recommendation 2, supervisors should ensure they put in place and maintain effective channels to cooperate and exchange information at operational and policy levels, to the extent relevant, in a risk-based manner. (p. 61)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "supervisors should ensure that they put in place and maintain effective channels to cooperate"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Annex 5 paragraph 33: such cooperation should not impinge on ongoing inquiries, investigations or proceedings under criminal or administrative law. (p. 61)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Such cooperation should not impinge on ongoing inquiries, investigations or proceedings in accordance with criminal or administrative law."</sub>
- **recommended / recommendation**: Annex 5 paragraph 34: given the importance of cooperation with law enforcement agencies, FIUs and other non-supervisory AML/CFT agencies, prudential and AML/CFT supervisors should consider taking measures to facilitate cooperation and information exchange with all relevant third parties, for example through an Agreement or MoU such as the mechanisms in paragraphs 24 to 26. (p. 62)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "should consider taking measures to facilitate cooperation and information exchange with all relevant third parties"</sub>

## E.4 Confidentiality and data protection provisions

- **expected / expectation**: Annex 5 paragraph 35: when putting in place cooperation and information exchange mechanisms in line with paragraphs 24 to 26 and 32, supervisors should include, to the extent legally possible, confidentiality and data protection provisions as follows. (p. 62)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "supervisors should include, to the extent legally possible, confidentiality and data protection provisions as follows"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Paragraph 35(1), confidentiality clause: all information exchanged under the arrangement can only be used for supervisory purposes or purposes linked to the receiving party's AML/CFT responsibilities; authorities should consider specifying that exchanged information must be classified as "confidential" and adopting requirements ensuring an appropriate, high level of security for transmission and storage. (p. 62)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "can only be used for supervisory purposes or purposes linked to the AML/CFT responsibilities of the party receiving the information"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Paragraph 35(3), data protection: when required by applicable laws, parties should be allowed to exchange sanitised data; for information on ongoing law enforcement investigations or suspicious transaction reporting, transmission of sanitised data should be authorised by the originating authorities. (p. 62)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the transmission of sanitised data should be authorised by the originating authorities"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Paragraph 35(4): safeguards should be included to protect the identity of whistleblowers where the exchanged information is gathered from whistleblowing. (p. 62)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Safeguards to protect the identity of whistleblowers where the information exchanged is gathered from whistleblowing"</sub>

## Principle 8 - Supervisory approach

- **expected / expectation**: Principle 8 states effective banking supervision requires the supervisor to develop and maintain a forward-looking assessment of the risk profile of individual banks proportionate to systemic importance, identify, assess and address risks from banks and the banking system as a whole, have an early intervention framework, and have plans with other authorities to resolve non-viable banks in an orderly manner. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "have a framework in place for early intervention"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 8 essential criterion 1: the supervisor uses a well defined methodology and processes to determine and assess on an ongoing basis the nature, impact and scope of the risks banks are exposed to and present to the safety and soundness of the banking system, including interlinkages with financial system stability. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The supervisor uses a well defined methodology and processes to determine and assess on an ongoing basis"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 8 essential criterion 2: the supervisor, with relevant authorities where appropriate, uses a process to identify which banks are systemically important domestically, publicly discloses an outline of that process, and conducts these assessments sufficiently regularly to reflect the current state of the domestic financial system. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "uses a process to assess and identify which banks are systemically important in a domestic context"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 8 essential criterion 3: the supervisor assesses banks' compliance with prudential regulations and other legal requirements. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The supervisor assesses banks' compliance with prudential regulations and other legal requirements."</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 8 essential criterion 4: the supervisor considers the macroeconomic environment, climate-related financial risks and emerging risks in its risk assessment of banks, and considers cross-sectoral developments, for example in non-bank financial institutions, through frequent contact with their regulators. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The supervisor considers the macroeconomic environment, climate-related financial risks and emerging risks"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 8 essential criterion 5: the supervisor, with other relevant authorities, identifies, monitors and assesses the build-up and transmission of risks, trends and concentrations within and across the banking system, emerging or system-wide risks, and common bank behaviours (for example procyclical actions) and interconnections that may harm stability; it incorporates this analysis into bank assessments, proactively addresses serious threats to stability, and communicates significant trends or emerging risks to other relevant authorities. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "common behaviours by banks (eg procyclical actions), interlinkages and interconnections"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 8 essential criterion 6: with the resolution authority, the supervisor assesses a bank's resolvability where appropriate given its risk profile and systemic importance; where bank-specific barriers to orderly resolution are identified, it requires banks to adopt appropriate measures (for example changes to business strategies, managerial, operational and ownership structures, internal procedures), considering their effect on the soundness of ongoing business. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the supervisor requires banks to adopt appropriate measures, where necessary"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 8 essential criterion 7: the supervisor has a clear framework or process (for example risk identification and early intervention) for handling banks in the build-up to and during stress, so that decisions to require or undertake recovery or resolution actions are made in a timely manner. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The supervisor has a clear framework or process (eg identification of risk and early intervention)"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 8 essential criterion 8: where the supervisor becomes aware of banks restructuring activities to avoid the regulatory perimeter, it takes appropriate steps; where bank-like activities are performed wholly or partly outside the perimeter, it takes appropriate steps to draw the matter to the responsible authority's attention to address regulatory arbitrage. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "restructuring their activities to avoid the regulatory perimeter"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 8 methodology: the methodology and processes address (among other things) banks' group structure including risks from wider group entities, business models including sustainability, forward-looking risk profile, internal control environment and resolvability; the methodology permits relevant comparisons between banks and the nature, frequency and intensity of supervision reflect the analysis. Footnote 16 says ultimate responsibility for designing and implementing sustainable business strategies lies with a bank's board. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The methodology permits relevant comparisons between banks"</sub>

## Principle 9 - Supervisory techniques and tools

- **expected / expectation**: Principle 9 states the supervisor uses an appropriate range of techniques and tools to implement the supervisory approach and deploys supervisory resources on a proportionate basis, considering banks' risk profile and systemic importance. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "deploys supervisory resources on a proportionate basis, considering the risk profile and systemic importance of banks"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 9 essential criterion 1: the supervisor employs an appropriate mix of on-site and off-site supervision to evaluate bank condition, risk profile, internal control environment and needed corrective measures; the mix may depend on country and bank circumstances, and the supervisor regularly assesses the quality, effectiveness and integration of its on-site and off-site functions and amends its approach as needed. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The supervisor employs an appropriate mix of on-site and off-site supervision"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 9 essential criterion 2: the supervisor has a coherent process for planning and executing on-site and off-site activities, with policies ensuring thorough and consistent conduct, clear responsibilities, objectives and outputs, and effective coordination and information-sharing between the two functions. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The supervisor has a coherent process for planning and executing on-site and off-site activities."</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 9 essential criterion 3: the supervisor uses a range of information (prudential reports, statistical returns, information on related entities, publicly available information) to review bank safety and soundness and form a holistic view of risk profiles, determines that information provided by banks is reliable, and obtains additional information as necessary. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The supervisor determines that information provided by banks is reliable 19 and obtains, as necessary, additional information"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 9 essential criterion 4: the supervisor uses a variety of tools to review safety and soundness, including analysis of financial statements and accounts, business model analysis, horizontal peer reviews, analysis of corporate governance (including risk management and internal control), reviews of banks' stress test outcomes, and assessments of capital and liquidity adequacy under adverse scenarios (which may include supervisory stress tests); it uses its analysis to determine follow-up work. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "business model analysis; horizontal peer reviews; analysis of corporate governance, including risk management and internal control systems"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 9 essential criterion 5: based on bank information and its own analysis, the supervisor communicates its findings to banks as appropriate and requires them to act to mitigate vulnerabilities that could affect safety and soundness or banking system stability. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "requires them to take action to mitigate any particular vulnerabilities"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 9 essential criterion 6: the supervisor evaluates the work of the bank's internal audit function, including outsourced or co-sourced, and determines whether and to what extent it may rely on internal auditors' work to identify areas of potential risk. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "determines whether, and to what extent, it may rely on the internal auditors' work"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 9 essential criterion 7: the supervisor engages sufficiently frequently with the board, non-executive board members and senior and middle management (including heads of business units and control functions) to understand matters such as strategy, group structure, governance, performance, capital adequacy, liquidity, asset quality, risk management and internal controls, and where necessary challenges the board and senior management on assumptions in strategies and business models. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "challenges the bank's board and senior management on the assumptions made in setting strategies and business models"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 9 essential criterion 8: the supervisor communicates findings of on-site and off-site analyses to the bank in a timely manner (written reports, discussions or meetings), meets senior management and the board to discuss examination and external audit results as appropriate, and meets separately with independent board members and the external auditor as necessary. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The supervisor also meets separately with the bank's independent board members and external auditor, as necessary."</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 9 essential criterion 9: the supervisor undertakes appropriate and timely follow-up to check that banks have addressed supervisory concerns or implemented requirements, including early escalation to the appropriate supervisory level and to the bank's board if action points are not addressed adequately or on time. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "This includes early escalation to the appropriate level of the supervisory authority and to the bank's board"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 9 essential criterion 10: the supervisor requires banks to notify it in advance of substantive changes in activities, structure and overall condition, or as soon as they become aware of material adverse developments, including breaches of legal or prudential requirements. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "requires banks to notify it in advance of any substantive changes in their activities, structure and overall condition"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 9 essential criterion 12: the supervisor has an adequate information system that facilitates processing, monitoring and analysis of prudential information and aids identification of areas requiring follow-up action. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The supervisor has an adequate information system which facilitates the processing, monitoring and analysis of prudential information."</sub>
- **expected / recommendation**: Principle 9 additional criterion: the supervisor has a framework for periodic independent reviews (for example by internal audit, internal risk function or third-party assessor) of the adequacy and effectiveness of its supervisory tools and their use, makes changes as appropriate, and the supervisory approach should be reviewed periodically and improved as necessary to remain effective and fit for purpose. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The supervisory approach should be reviewed at periodic intervals and improved as necessary"</sub>

## Principle 10 - Supervisory reporting

- **expected / expectation**: Principle 10 states the supervisor collects, reviews and analyses prudential reports and statistical returns from banks on a solo and consolidated basis and independently verifies them through on-site examinations or external experts; footnote 21 says these are distinct from and required in addition to mandatory accounting reports, which Principle 27 addresses. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "independently verifies these reports through either on-site examinations or use of external experts."</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 10 essential criterion 1: the supervisor has power to require banks to submit information, solo and consolidated, on financial condition, performance and risk exposures, on demand and at regular intervals, covering items such as on- and off-balance sheet assets and liabilities, profit and loss, capital adequacy, liquidity, large exposures, risk concentrations (by sector, geography and currency), asset quality, loan loss provisioning, related party transactions, interest rate risk, market risk, and information allowing assessment of the materiality of climate-related financial and emerging risks. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The supervisor has the power to require banks to submit information, on both a solo and a consolidated basis"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 10 essential criterion 2: the supervisor provides reporting instructions clearly describing the standards for supervisory reports, which are based on widely accepted international accounting principles and rules. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The supervisor provides reporting instructions that clearly describe the standards to be used"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 10 essential criterion 3: the supervisor requires banks to have sound governance and control processes for valuation methodologies; fair value measurement maximises relevant, reliable and consistently applied inputs; valuation frameworks are independently validated and verified; the supervisor assesses whether regulatory valuations are reliable and prudent and, where not sufficiently prudent, requires the bank to adjust reporting for capital adequacy or regulatory reporting purposes. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "requires the bank to adjust its reporting for capital adequacy or regulatory reporting purposes"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 10 essential criteria 4 and 5: the supervisor collects and analyses information at a frequency commensurate with the nature of the information and the bank's risk profile and systemic importance, and to make meaningful comparisons collects data from all banks and relevant consolidated entities on a comparable basis and for the same dates (stock data) and periods (flow data). (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "for the same dates (stock data) and periods (flow data)"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 10 essential criterion 6: the supervisor has power to request and receive any relevant information from banks and entities in the wider group, irrespective of their activities, where it believes the information is material to the bank's condition, material to assessing its risks, or needed to support resolution planning, including internal management information, governance information, transactions with the wider group and related party transactions. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "irrespective of their activities, where the supervisor believes that it is"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 10 essential criterion 7: the supervisor has a means of enforcing timely and accurate submission, determines the appropriate level of senior management responsible for accuracy of supervisory returns, imposes sanctions for misreporting and persistent errors, and requires inaccurate information to be amended. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "imposes sanctions for misreporting and persistent errors"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 10 essential criteria 8 and 9: the supervisor uses policies and procedures to determine the validity and integrity of supervisory information, including a programme of periodic verification of returns by its own staff or external experts, and has a process to periodically review collected information to determine it satisfies a supervisory need. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "a programme for the periodic verification of supervisory returns"</sub>

## Principle 11 - Corrective and sanctioning powers of supervisors

- **expected / expectation**: Principle 11 states the supervisor acts at an early stage to address unsafe and unsound practices or activities that could pose risks to banks or the banking system, and has an adequate range of supervisory tools that it can apply at its discretion for timely corrective action, including the ability to revoke the banking licence or recommend its revocation. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "acts at an early stage to address unsafe and unsound practices or activities"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 11 essential criterion 1: the supervisor raises concerns with management or the board at an early stage and requires them to be addressed in a timely manner; significant corrective actions are set out in a written document to the board; the supervisor requires regular written progress reports, checks corrective actions are completed satisfactorily, and follows through conclusively and in a timely manner. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "these are addressed in a written document to the bank's board"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 11 essential criterion 3: the supervisor uses its powers to act where a bank falls below regulatory thresholds (including prescribed ratios), intervenes early to prevent breaches, and laws or regulations guard against the supervisor unduly delaying corrective action without limiting its discretion to act. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Laws or regulations guard against the supervisor unduly delaying appropriate corrective actions"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 11 essential criterion 4: the supervisor uses a broad range of measures applied in accordance with the gravity of the situation, including restricting current activities, imposing more stringent prudential limits, withholding approval of new activities or acquisitions, restricting or suspending shareholder payments or share repurchases, restricting asset transfers, barring individuals from the banking sector, replacing or restricting powers of managers, board members or controlling owners, facilitating takeover or merger with a healthier institution, interim management, and revoking or recommending revocation of the licence. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "barring individuals from the banking sector, replacing or restricting the powers of managers, board members or controlling owners"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 11 essential criterion 5: the supervisor applies sanctions not only to the bank but, when and if necessary, also to management, the board or relevant individuals, and has power to apply corrective and sanctioning measures simultaneously, including financial penalties. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The supervisor has the power to apply corrective measures and sanctioning measures simultaneously"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: Principle 11 essential criterion 7: laws, regulations or the supervisor establish a clear policy on whether sanctions are made public and what to disclose and when; the decision to publish sanctions or corrective measures against banks and individuals may be subject to confidentiality considerations and must not jeopardise other supervisory objectives or prejudice another pending case; transparency of enforcement is encouraged, and disclosure can be decided case by case depending on seriousness and frequency, among other considerations. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "it must not jeopardise other supervisory objectives or prejudice another case pending before the supervisor"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 11 essential criteria 8 and 9: the supervisor cooperates and collaborates with relevant authorities in deciding when and how to effect the orderly resolution of a problem bank (closure, assisted restructuring or merger with a stronger institution), and where appropriate informs and coordinates with supervisors of related non-bank financial entities when taking formal corrective action. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "informs the supervisor of related non-bank financial entities of its actions and coordinates its actions with them"</sub>

## Principle 12 - Consolidated supervision

- **expected / requirement**: Principle 12 essential criterion 1: the supervisor understands the overall group structure and all material activities (including non-banking) of wider group entities, domestic or cross-border, understands and assesses how group-wide risks are managed, and acts when risks from the group, in particular contagion and reputational risks, may jeopardise the bank's or banking system's safety and soundness. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "in particular contagion and reputational risks"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 12 essential criterion 2: the supervisor imposes prudential standards and collects and analyses financial and other information on a consolidated basis, covering areas such as capital adequacy, liquidity, large exposures, exposures to related parties, lending limits and group structure. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "covering areas such as capital adequacy, liquidity, large exposures, exposures to related parties, lending limits and group structure"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 12 essential criterion 3: the supervisor reviews whether management oversight of foreign operations is adequate, determines that parent banks have unimpeded access to all material information from foreign branches and subsidiaries and that policies require local management of cross-border operations to have necessary expertise and ensure compliance with supervisory and regulatory requirements; the home supervisor considers the effectiveness of supervision in host countries with material operations. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "parent banks have unimpeded access to all material information from their foreign branches and subsidiaries"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 12 essential criterion 4: the home supervisor visits the bank's foreign offices periodically, with location and frequency determined by the risk profile and systemic importance of the foreign operations, meets host supervisors during visits, and has a policy for assessing whether to conduct on-site examinations of foreign operations or require additional reporting, with the power and resources to do so. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The home supervisor visits the foreign offices of the bank periodically."</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 12 essential criteria 5 and 6: the supervisor reviews the main activities of parent companies and their affiliates that materially affect the bank's safety and soundness and takes appropriate action, and limits the range and location of activities the consolidated group may conduct (including closing foreign offices) if safety and soundness is compromised by excessive or poorly managed risk, other authorities' supervision is inadequate relative to the risks, or effective consolidated supervision is hindered. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "limits the range of activities the consolidated group may conduct and the locations in which activities can be conducted"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 12 essential criterion 7: in addition to consolidated supervision, the responsible supervisor supervises each individual bank in the group on a solo basis and understands its relationship with other group members. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The responsible supervisor supervises each bank on a solo basis and understands its relationship with other members of the group."</sub>
- **expected / recommendation**: Principle 12 additional criterion: for countries allowing corporate ownership of banks, the supervisor has power to establish and enforce fit and proper standards for senior management of parent companies. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the supervisor has the power to establish and enforce fit and proper standards for senior management of parent companies."</sub>

## Principle 13 - Home-host relationships

- **expected / expectation**: Principle 13 states home and host supervisors of cross-border banking groups share information and cooperate for effective supervision of the group and entities and effective crisis handling, and supervisors require the local operations of foreign banks to be conducted to the same standards as those required of domestic banks. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Supervisors require the local operations of foreign banks to be conducted to the same standards"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 13 essential criterion 1: the home supervisor establishes bank-specific supervisory colleges for groups with material cross-border operations, considering risk profile and systemic importance; the host supervisor with a relevant subsidiary or significant branch and a shared interest is included, and the college structure reflects the group's scale, structure, complexity and host-jurisdiction significance and the opportunity to enhance mutual trust. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "establishes bank-specific supervisory colleges for banking groups with material cross-border operations"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 13 essential criterion 2: home and host supervisors share appropriate information on a timely basis, bilaterally and through colleges, including on material risks (including those from respective macroeconomic environments), risk management practices of the group and supervisors' assessments of safety and soundness of entities under their jurisdiction, with informal or formal arrangements such as memoranda of understanding and confidentiality agreements enabling timely exchange of confidential information. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Informal or formal arrangements (such as memoranda of understanding and confidentiality agreements) are in place"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 13 essential criteria 3 and 4: home and host supervisors coordinate and plan supervisory activities or undertake collaborative work where common interests are identified, and the home supervisor develops an agreed communication strategy with relevant host supervisors reflecting the group's cross-border risk profile and systemic importance, with agreement on communicating views and outcomes of joint activities and college meetings to banks to keep messages on group-wide issues consistent. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The home supervisor develops an agreed communication strategy with the relevant host supervisors."</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 13 essential criterion 5: where appropriate given risk profile and systemic importance, the home supervisor with national resolution authorities develops a framework for cross-border crisis cooperation and coordination among home and host authorities, who share information on crisis preparations from an early stage, subject to confidentiality rules, without materially compromising the prospect of successful resolution. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "develops a framework for cross-border crisis cooperation and coordination among the relevant home and host authorities"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 13 essential criterion 6: where appropriate, the home supervisor with resolution authorities and relevant host authorities develops a group resolution plan; authorities share information necessary for a credible plan, and supervisors notify and consult relevant home and host authorities promptly when taking recovery and resolution measures. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "working with its national resolution authorities and relevant host authorities, develops a group resolution plan."</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 13 essential criterion 7: the host supervisor's national laws or regulations require cross-border operations of foreign banks to be subject to prudential, inspection and regulatory reporting requirements similar to those for domestic banks. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "are subject to prudential, inspection and regulatory reporting requirements similar to those for domestic banks"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 13 essential criterion 8: the home supervisor is given on-site access to local offices and subsidiaries of a banking group to assess safety and soundness and compliance with customer due diligence requirements, and informs host supervisors of intended visits. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "and compliance with customer due diligence requirements"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 13 essential criteria 9 and 10: the host supervisor supervises booking offices consistently with internationally agreed standards and does not permit shell banks or their continued operation; a supervisor acting on information from, or consequential for, another supervisor consults that supervisor, to the extent possible, before acting. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The supervisor does not permit shell banks or the continued operation of shell banks."</sub>

## Principle 14 - Corporate governance

- **expected / requirement**: Principle 14 essential criterion 1: laws, regulations or the supervisor establish the responsibilities of a bank's board and senior management on corporate governance to ensure effective control over the entire business, and the supervisor provides guidance to banks on expectations for sound corporate governance. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The supervisor provides guidance to banks on expectations for sound corporate governance."</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 14 essential criterion 2: the supervisor regularly conducts comprehensive evaluations of a bank's corporate governance policies, practices and implementation, determines they are robust and commensurate with risk profile and systemic importance, and requires banks to correct deficiencies in a timely manner. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "regularly conducts comprehensive evaluations of a bank's corporate governance policies and practices"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 14 essential criterion 3: the supervisor determines that board membership has a balance of skills, diversity and expertise, collectively qualified commensurate with the bank's size, complexity and risk profile, includes a sufficient number of experienced independent directors, and that members are qualified, effective and exercise their 'duty of care' and 'duty of loyalty'. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Board membership includes a sufficient number of experienced independent directors."</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 14 essential criterion 4: the supervisor determines that nomination and appointment processes for board members are appropriate; boards regularly assess the performance of the board, its committees and individual members (including ongoing suitability); membership is regularly renewed to refresh skills and independence; and board structures include audit, risk, compensation and other committees with experienced independent directors, commensurate with risk profile and systemic importance. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Board membership is regularly renewed to refresh skills and independence."</sub>

## Principle 8 - Supervisory approach

- **expected / recommendation**: Footnote 17 (attached to the forward-looking view of banks' risk profile in the Principle 8 criterion on supervisory methodology) says that the time horizon for establishing a forward-looking view should appropriately reflect climate-related financial risks and emerging risks as needed. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The time horizon for establishing a forward-looking view should appropriately reflect climate-related financial risks and emerging risks as needed."</sub>

## Principle 9 - Supervisory techniques and tools

- **permissive / requirement**: A Principle 9 essential criterion: the supervisor may use independent third parties, including external experts. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The supervisor may use independent third parties, including external experts"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: The same Principle 9 criterion states that the supervisor cannot outsource its prudential responsibilities to third parties. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "it cannot outsource its prudential responsibilities to third parties"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Where third parties are used, the supervisor clearly defines and documents their roles and responsibilities (including the scope of work where they conduct supervisory tasks); assesses their suitability for the task, the quality of their work and whether their output can be relied upon to the degree intended; ensures they are subject to appropriate confidentiality restrictions; considers the biases that may influence them; and requires them to promptly bring any material shortcomings identified to its attention. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "clearly defines and documents their roles and responsibilities, including the scope of work where they are appointed"</sub>

## Principle 12 - Consolidated supervision

- **expected / expectation**: Principle 12: the supervisor supervises the banking group on a consolidated basis, adequately monitoring it and, as appropriate, applying prudential standards to all aspects of the business the group conducts worldwide. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "supervises the banking group on a consolidated basis, adequately monitoring and, as appropriate, applying prudential standards"</sub>

## Principle 14 - Corporate governance

- **expected / expectation**: Principle 14 adds that these governance policies and processes are commensurate with the risk profile and systemic importance of the bank. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "commensurate with the risk profile and systemic importance of the bank"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: A Principle 14 essential criterion: the supervisor determines that the bank's board approves and oversees implementation of the strategic direction, risk appetite and strategy and related policies; establishes and communicates corporate culture and values (eg through a code of conduct); and establishes conflicts of interest policies and a strong control environment. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "establishes conflicts of interest policies and a strong control environment."</sub>

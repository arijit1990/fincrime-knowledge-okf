## Scope and definitions

- **descriptive / context**: The Basel Framework includes standards on scope of application; criteria that bank capital instruments must meet to be eligible for Basel capital requirements (with regulatory adjustments and transitional arrangements); the framework for risk-based capital requirements; and the calculation of capital requirements for credit risk, for market risk and credit valuation adjustment risk, and for operational risk. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "This standard describes how to calculate capital requirements for operational risk."</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The leverage ratio standard describes a simple, transparent, non-risk-based leverage ratio intended to restrict the build-up of leverage in the banking sector and to reinforce the risk-based requirements as a non-risk-based 'backstop' measure. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "This standard describes the simple, transparent, non-risk-based leverage ratio."</sub>

## Liquidity Coverage Ratio

- **descriptive / context**: The Liquidity Coverage Ratio standard describes a measure that promotes the short-term resilience of a bank's liquidity risk profile. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "a measure which promotes the short-term resilience of a bank's liquidity risk profile"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Large exposures regulation limits the maximum loss a bank could face from a sudden counterparty failure to a level that does not endanger its solvency; the standard requires banks to measure their exposures to a single counterparty or group of connected counterparties and to limit large exposures in relation to their capital. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "limits the maximum loss that a bank could face in the event of a sudden counterparty failure"</sub>

## Margin requirements

- **descriptive / context**: The margin requirements standard sets minimum standards for margin on non-centrally cleared derivatives; such requirements reduce systemic risk for non-standardised derivatives by reducing contagion and spillover risks and promoting central clearing. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "minimum standards for margin requirements for non-centrally cleared derivatives"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The Pillar 2 supervisory review process ensures banks have adequate capital and liquidity to support all the risks in their business, especially risks not fully captured by Pillar 1, and encourages good risk management. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "especially with respect to risks not fully captured by the Pillar 1 process"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The Basel disclosure requirements standard aims to encourage market discipline. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "This standard sets out disclosure requirements, which aim to encourage market discipline."</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The Basel Core Principles are described as a comprehensive standard for establishing a sound foundation for the regulation, supervision, governance and risk management of the banking sector. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "sound foundation for the regulation, supervision, governance and risk management of the banking sector"</sub>

- **descriptive / context**: The Basel Framework homepage lists all 14 standards with short descriptions. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The Basel Framework homepage lists all 14 standards together with a short description of their content."</sub>

## Basel Framework

- **descriptive / context**: The Basel Framework is the full set of Basel Committee on Banking Supervision (BCBS) standards, the primary global standard setter for prudential regulation of banks; BCBS members have agreed to fully implement the standards and apply them to internationally active banks in their jurisdictions. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The membership of the BCBS has agreed to fully implement these standards and apply them to the internationally active banks"</sub>

## Core Principles for effective banking supervision

- **descriptive / context**: The chapter on BCP40 (effective 25 Apr 2024, status current) establishes 29 Core Principles needed for an effective supervisory system: Principles 1 to 13 focus on powers, responsibilities and functions of supervisors, and Principles 14 to 29 on prudential regulations and requirements for banks; each has 'essential criteria' and 'additional criteria'. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The Core Principles establish 29 principles that are needed for a supervisory system to be effective"</sub>

## Introduction

- **descriptive / context**: The assessment criteria are based on international standards and sound supervisory practices already established even if not yet fully implemented, with underlying documents cited as 'reference documents'; there is an expectation that guidelines issued by the Committee will be observed by Committee member jurisdictions. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "There is the expectation that guidelines issued by the Committee will be observed by Committee member jurisdictions."</sub>

## Principle 1 - Responsibilities, objectives and powers

- **expected / expectation**: Principle 1 states an effective banking supervision system has clear responsibilities and objectives for each authority involved, and a suitable legal framework giving each responsible authority the necessary legal powers to authorise banks, conduct ongoing supervision, address compliance with laws and take timely corrective action on safety and soundness concerns. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "A suitable legal framework for banking supervision is in place to provide each responsible authority with the necessary legal powers"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 1 essential criterion 1: the responsibilities and objectives of each authority involved in banking supervision are clearly defined in legislation and publicly disclosed; where more than one authority supervises the banking system, a credible, publicly available framework avoids regulatory and supervisory gaps. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "are clearly defined in legislation and publicly disclosed."</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 1 essential criterion 2: the primary objective of banking supervision is to promote the safety and soundness of banks and the banking system; any broader responsibilities assigned to the supervisor are subordinate to and must not conflict with that primary objective. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The primary objective of banking supervision is to promote the safety and soundness of banks and the banking system."</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 1 essential criterion 3: laws and regulations give the supervisor a framework to set and enforce minimum prudential standards, and the supervisor has power to increase prudential requirements for individual banks based on their risk profile and systemic importance. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The supervisor has the power to increase the prudential requirements for individual banks"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 1 essential criterion 4: banking laws, regulations and prudential standards are updated as necessary to remain effective and relevant to changing industry and regulatory practices, subject to public consultation as appropriate, and published in a timely manner. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "are updated as necessary to ensure that they remain effective and relevant to changing industry and regulatory practices"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 1 essential criterion 5: the supervisor has power to have full access to a bank's board, management, staff and records (including those held by relevant service providers), to review the overall activities of a bank (including by service providers), domestic or cross-border, and to supervise foreign activities of banks incorporated in its jurisdiction; footnote 3 says access includes in-person access to premises, senior executives and the board, in person or virtually as needed. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "have full access 3 to a bank's board, management, staff and records"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 1 essential criterion 7: the supervisor has power to review activities of parent companies and companies affiliated with parent companies to determine their impact on the bank's safety and soundness, with access to all necessary information directly or through the supervised bank irrespective of where it is available. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The supervisor has the power to review the activities of parent companies and of companies affiliated with parent companies"</sub>

## Principle 2 - Independence, accountability, resourcing and legal protection for supervisors

- **expected / expectation**: Principle 2 states the supervisor possesses operational independence, transparent processes, sound governance, budgetary processes that do not undermine autonomy, and adequate resources, and is accountable for discharging its duties and using its resources; the legal framework includes legal protection for the supervisor. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The legal framework for banking supervision includes legal protection for the supervisor."</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 2 essential criterion 1: the supervisor's operational independence, accountability and governance are prescribed in legislation and publicly disclosed; there is no government or industry interference compromising independence; and the supervisor has full discretion to set prudential policy and take any supervisory actions or decisions on banks. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "There is no government or industry interference that compromises the operational independence of the supervisor."</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 2 essential criterion 2: the process for appointing and removing the head(s) of the supervisory authority and governing body members is transparent; heads are appointed for a minimum term and removed during their term only for reasons specified in law, physical or mental incapacity, or misconduct, and the reasons for removal are publicly disclosed. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "is (are) removed from office during their term only for reasons specified in law"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 2 essential criterion 3: the supervisor publishes its objectives, is accountable through a transparent framework for discharging its duties in relation to them, and regularly communicates its supervisory priorities publicly. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The supervisor regularly communicates its supervisory priorities publicly."</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 2 essential criterion 4: the supervisor has effective internal governance and communication processes enabling timely decisions at a level appropriate to the issue's significance and expedited emergency procedures; responsibilities and delegation are clearly defined; supervisory processes include internal checks and balances; and the governing body is structured to avoid real or perceived conflicts of interest. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The governing body is structured to avoid any real or perceived conflicts of interest."</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 2 essential criterion 5: the supervisor and its staff have credibility based on professionalism and integrity, with rules on avoiding conflicts of interest and on appropriate use of information obtained through work, and sanctions if these are not followed. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "There are rules on how to avoid conflicts of interest and on the appropriate use of information obtained through work"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 2 essential criterion 6: the supervisor has adequate resources and is financed without undermining autonomy, including a budget for sufficient staff with skills commensurate with banks' risk profile and systemic importance, salary scales that attract and retain qualified staff, ability to commission external experts subject to confidentiality, regular staff training, a technology budget, and a travel budget for on-site work, cross-border cooperation and meetings such as supervisory colleges. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "salary scales that allow it to attract and retain qualified staff"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 2 essential criterion 7: as part of annual resource planning, supervisors regularly take stock of existing staff skills and projected short and medium-term needs, considering emerging risks and practices, and review and implement measures to bridge gaps in numbers or skillsets. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "supervisors regularly take stock of existing staff skills and projected requirements/needs over the short and medium term"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 2 essential criterion 8: in determining supervisory programmes and allocating resources, supervisors consider the risk profile and systemic importance of individual banks and the different risk mitigation approaches available. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "supervisors consider the risk profile and systemic importance of individual banks and the different risk mitigation approaches available."</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 2 essential criterion 9: laws protect the supervisor and its staff against lawsuits for actions or omissions in discharging duties in good faith and adequately protect them against the costs of defending such actions; footnote 5 says 'supervisor and its staff' covers the head, governing body, employees and professional service providers, and protection is not removed when appointment or employment ends. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Laws provide protection to the supervisor and its staff against lawsuits for actions taken and/or omissions made"</sub>

## Principle 3 - Cooperation and collaboration

- **expected / expectation**: Principle 3 states laws, regulations or other arrangements provide a framework for cooperation and collaboration with relevant domestic authorities and foreign supervisors, and these arrangements reflect the need to protect confidential information. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "These arrangements reflect the need to protect confidential information."</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 3 essential criterion 1: formal or informal arrangements are in place for cooperation, including analysis and sharing of information and collaborative work, with all domestic authorities responsible for the safety and soundness of banks, other financial institutions or financial stability, with evidence they work in practice where necessary. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "with all domestic authorities with responsibility for the safety and soundness of banks, other financial institutions"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 3 essential criterion 2: arrangements are in place for the supervisor to coordinate, within its mandate, with authorities responsible for macroprudential policy when monitoring, identifying and addressing systemic risks that could affect banking system stability. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "relevant authorities with responsibility for macroprudential policy"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 3 essential criterion 3: formal or informal arrangements are in place for cooperation, including information analysis and sharing and collaborative work, with relevant foreign supervisors of banks, with evidence they work in practice where necessary. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "with relevant foreign supervisors of banks."</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: Principle 3 essential criterion 4: the supervisor may provide confidential information to another domestic authority or foreign supervisor but must take reasonable steps to determine that it will be used only for bank-specific or system-wide supervisory purposes and treated as confidential by the recipient. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "but must take reasonable steps to determine that any confidential information so released will be used only for"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 3 essential criterion 5: a supervisor receiving confidential information from other supervisors uses it only for bank-specific or system-wide supervisory purposes, does not disclose it to third parties without the originating supervisor's permission, and can deny any demand to disclose other than a court order or legislative mandate. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "does not disclose to third parties confidential information received without the permission of the supervisor providing the information"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 3 essential criterion 5 continues: if legally compelled to disclose confidential information received from another supervisor, the supervisor promptly notifies the originating supervisor of what is compelled and the circumstances, and where consent to pass it on is not given uses all reasonable means to resist the demand or protect confidentiality. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "it promptly notifies the originating supervisor, indicating what information it is compelled to release"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 3 essential criterion 6: processes are in place for the supervisor to support resolution authorities (for example central banks and finance ministries as appropriate) undertaking recovery and resolution planning and actions. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Processes are in place for the supervisor to support resolution authorities"</sub>

## Principle 4 - Permissible activities

- **expected / expectation**: Principle 4 states the permissible activities of institutions licensed and supervised as banks are clearly defined and the use of the word 'bank' in names is controlled. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the use of the word "bank" in names is controlled."</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 4 essential criteria 1 and 2: the term 'bank' is clearly defined in laws or regulations, and the permissible activities of licensed and supervised banks are clearly defined by supervisors, laws or regulations. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The term "bank" is clearly defined in laws or regulations."</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 4 essential criterion 4: taking deposits from the public is reserved for institutions licensed and supervised as banks; footnote 7 recognises non-bank deposit-takers regulated differently, which should be subject to regulation commensurate with the type and size of their business and collectively should not hold a significant proportion of system deposits. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The taking of deposits from the public is reserved for institutions that are licensed and subject to supervision as banks."</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 4 essential criterion 5: the supervisor or licensing authority publishes or makes available an easily accessible current list of licensed banks, including branches of foreign banks, operating in its jurisdiction. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "publishes or otherwise makes available a current list of licensed banks"</sub>

## Principle 5 - Licensing criteria

- **expected / expectation**: Principle 5 states the licensing authority has power to set licensing criteria and reject applications not meeting them; at a minimum the licensing process assesses ownership structure and governance (including fitness and propriety of board members and senior management) of the bank and its wider group, strategic and operating plan, internal controls, risk management and projected financial condition including capital base; where the owner or parent is a foreign bank, the home supervisor's prior consent is obtained. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Where the proposed owner or parent organisation is a foreign bank, the prior consent of its home supervisor is obtained."</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 5 essential criterion 1: the law identifies the authority responsible for granting and withdrawing a banking licence; if it differs from the supervisor, the supervisor has the right to have its views on each application considered and its concerns addressed, the licensing authority provides material information, and the supervisor imposes prudential conditions or limitations on a newly licensed bank where appropriate. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The law identifies the authority responsible for granting and withdrawing a banking licence."</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 5 essential criterion 2: laws or regulations give the licensing authority power to set licensing criteria and to reject an application if criteria are not met or information is inadequate; a licence can be revoked if it was based on false information. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "If the licensing authority or supervisor determines that the licence was based on false information, the licence can be revoked."</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 5 essential criterion 4: the licensing authority identifies and determines the suitability of major shareholders (including beneficial owners, and corporate owners where permitted) and others exerting significant influence, and assesses ownership transparency, the sources of initial capital and shareholders' ability to provide additional financial support where needed. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the sources of initial capital and the ability of shareholders to provide additional financial support"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 5 essential criterion 5: a minimum initial capital amount is stipulated for all banks. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "A minimum initial capital amount is stipulated for all banks."</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 5 essential criterion 6: at authorisation the licensing authority evaluates proposed board members and senior management on expertise, integrity, availability and time commitment and potential conflicts of interest (fit and proper test), including skills and experience commensurate with intended activities and no record of criminal activities or adverse regulatory judgments making a person unfit. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "no record of criminal activities or adverse regulatory judgments that make a person unfit"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 5 essential criterion 6 continues: the licensing authority determines whether the board has collective sound knowledge of the material intended activities and associated risks, and the supervisor reassesses board suitability on significant events (for example change of control or major acquisition) or on information affecting fitness and propriety. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The supervisor reassesses the suitability of board members in case of significant events"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 5 essential criterion 7: the licensing authority reviews the proposed strategic and operating plans, including determining that appropriate corporate governance, risk management and internal controls (including those related to detecting and preventing criminal activities) and oversight of proposed outsourced functions will be in place, with an operational structure reflecting the scope and sophistication of activities. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "including those related to the detection and prevention of criminal activities"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 5 essential criterion 8: the licensing authority reviews pro forma financial statements and projections, including assessing whether financial strength is adequate to support the strategic plan and financial information on principal shareholders. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The licensing authority reviews pro forma financial statements and projections of the proposed bank."</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 5 essential criterion 9: for foreign banks establishing a branch or subsidiary, the host supervisor establishes before issuing a licence that a no objection (or statement of no objection) from the home supervisor has been received, and for cross-border operations determines whether the home supervisor practises global consolidated supervision and uses this to inform licensing and supervision. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the host supervisor establishes that no objection (or a statement of no objection) from the home supervisor has been received"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 5 essential criteria 10 and 11: the licensing authority or supervisor has policies and processes to monitor new entrants' progress against business and strategic goals and licence-approval requirements, and licensing criteria are consistent with those used in ongoing supervision, with the supervisor determining banks continue to comply once licensed. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The criteria for issuing licences are consistent with those applied in ongoing supervision."</sub>

## Principle 6 - Transfer of significant ownership

- **expected / requirement**: Principle 6 essential criterion 1: laws or regulations contain clear definitions of 'significant ownership' and 'controlling interest'. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Laws or regulations contain clear definitions of "significant ownership" and "controlling interest"."</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 6 essential criterion 2: there are requirements to obtain supervisory approval or provide immediate notification for proposed changes resulting in a change in ownership (including beneficial ownership), in exercise of voting rights over a particular threshold, or in controlling interest. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "There are requirements to obtain supervisory approval or provide immediate notification"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 6 essential criterion 3: the supervisor has power to reject any proposed change in significant ownership or controlling interest, or prevent exercise of voting rights, so that changes meet criteria comparable to licensing; if the change was based on false information, the supervisor can reject, modify or reverse it. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the supervisor has the power to reject, modify or reverse the change in significant ownership"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 6 essential criterion 4: through periodic reporting or on-site examinations the supervisor obtains the names and holdings of all significant shareholders or those exerting controlling influence, including identities of beneficial owners of shares held by nominees, custodians and vehicles that might be used to disguise ownership. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "including the identities of beneficial owners of shares being held by nominees, custodians and through vehicles"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 6 essential criteria 5 and 6: the supervisor has power to modify, reverse or otherwise address a change of control that occurred without required notification or approval, and laws, regulations or the supervisor require banks to notify the supervisor as soon as they become aware of material information that may negatively affect the suitability of a major shareholder or controlling party. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "notify the supervisor as soon as they become aware of any material information"</sub>

## Principle 7 - Major acquisitions

- **expected / expectation**: Principle 7 states the supervisor has power to approve or reject (or recommend approval or rejection of) and impose prudential conditions on major acquisitions or investments by a bank, including establishing cross-border operations, against prescribed criteria, and to determine that corporate affiliations or structures do not expose the bank to undue risks or hinder effective supervision. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "determine that corporate affiliations or structures do not expose the bank to undue risks or hinder effective supervision"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 7 essential criteria 1 and 2: laws or regulations clearly define which types and amounts of acquisitions and investments need prior supervisory approval and the cases where notification after the acquisition is sufficient (primarily activities closely related to banking where the investment is small relative to capital), and provide criteria for judging individual proposals. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "cases for which notification after the acquisition or investment is sufficient"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 7 essential criterion 3: the supervisor determines new acquisitions and investments will not expose the bank to undue risks or hinder effective supervision (and where appropriate future corrective measures), and can prohibit major acquisitions or investments, including cross-border operations, in countries whose laws prohibit information flows needed for adequate consolidated supervision, considering host-country supervisory effectiveness and its own ability to supervise on a consolidated basis; footnote 14 says it may consider obstacles to orderly resolution. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "can prohibit banks from making major acquisitions/investments"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 7 essential criterion 4: the supervisor determines the bank has, from the outset, adequate financial, managerial and organisational resources to manage the acquisition or investment. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "adequate financial, managerial and organisational resources to manage the acquisition/investment."</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 7 essential criterion 5: the supervisor is aware of risks that non-banking activities can pose to a bank, has means to mitigate them, and considers the bank's ability to manage these risks before permitting investment in non-banking activities. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The supervisor is aware of the risks that non-banking activities can pose to a bank"</sub>
- **expected / requirement**: Principle 7 essential criterion 6: the supervisor reviews major acquisitions or investments by other entities in the banking group to determine they do not expose the bank to undue risks or hinder effective supervision or future corrective measures, and can effectively address resulting risks to the bank where necessary. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "reviews major acquisitions or investments by other entities in the banking group"</sub>

## Principle 1 - Responsibilities, objectives and powers

- **expected / requirement**: The same Principle 1 criterion, in the same circumstances, also gives the supervisor the power to cooperate and collaborate with relevant authorities to achieve an orderly resolution of the bank, including triggering resolution where appropriate. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "cooperate and collaborate with relevant authorities to achieve an orderly resolution of the bank, including triggering resolution where appropriate"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Footnote 2 to Principle 1: where countries have shared or transferred prudential tasks to a supranational supervisor, the roles and responsibilities shared or transferred are clearly set out in law and publicly disclosed. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the roles and responsibilities that have been shared or transferred are clearly set out in law and publicly disclosed."</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: Footnote 2 to Principle 1 adds that any residual powers or responsibilities that are retained must be publicly disclosed, so that there is clarity on the division of responsibility. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Any residual powers or responsibilities that are retained must be publicly disclosed"</sub>

## Principle 4 - Permissible activities

- **expected / expectation**: A Principle 4 essential criterion: the word "bank" and derivations such as "banking", including in domain names, may be used in a name only by licensed and supervised institutions, in every situation where the general public might otherwise be misled. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "is limited to licensed and supervised institutions in all circumstances where the general public might otherwise be misled."</sub>

## Principle 5 - Licensing criteria

- **expected / expectation**: A Principle 5 essential criterion: the licensing authority determines that the proposed legal, managerial, operational and ownership structures of the bank and its wider group will not hinder effective supervision on both a solo and a consolidated basis, or implementation of corrective measures in the future. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "will not hinder effective:Shell banks must not be licensed."</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: The Principle 5 criterion text states that shell banks must not be licensed. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Shell banks must not be licensed."</sub>

## Principle 6 - Transfer of significant ownership

- **expected / expectation**: Principle 6: the supervisor has the power to review proposals to transfer significant ownership or controlling interests (held directly or indirectly) in existing banks to other parties, to reject them, and to impose prudential conditions. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "has the power to review, reject and impose prudential conditions on any proposals to transfer significant ownership"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Footnote 13 to Principle 6 notes that, although Principle 6 uses the term supervisor throughout, in a few countries these issues might be addressed by a separate licensing authority. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "in a few countries these issues might be addressed by a separate licensing authority"</sub>

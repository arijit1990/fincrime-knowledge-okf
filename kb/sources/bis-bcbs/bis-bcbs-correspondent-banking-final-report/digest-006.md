## Cover Payment

- **mandatory / requirement**: The glossary states that an MT202 COV shall be used for a cover payment. (p. 48)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "An MT202 COV shall be used."</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: The glossary lists the CDD measures as set out in line with FATF Recommendation 10. (p. 48)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "In line with FATF Recommendation 10, CDD measures to be taken are"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: CDD measure (a) is identifying the customer and verifying that customer's identity using reliable, independent source documents, data or information. (p. 48)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Identifying the customer and verifying that customer’s identityusing reliable, independent source documents"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: CDD measure (b) is identifying the beneficial owner and taking reasonable measures to verify the beneficial owner's identity, such that the financial institution is satisfied that it knows who the beneficial owner is. (p. 48)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Identifying the beneficial owner, and taking reasonablemeasures to verify the identity of the beneficial owner"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: For legal persons and arrangements, CDD measure (b) should include financial institutions understanding the ownership and control structure of the customer. (p. 48)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "For legal persons and arrangements thisshould include financial institutions understanding theownership and control structure"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: CDD measure (c) is understanding and, as appropriate, obtaining information on the purpose and intended nature of the business relationship. (p. 48)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Understanding and, as appropriate, obtaining information onthe purpose and intended nature of the business relationship"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: CDD measure (d) is conducting ongoing due diligence on the business relationship and scrutiny of transactions undertaken throughout the course of that relationship. (p. 48)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Conducting ongoing due diligence on the business relationshipand scrutiny of transactions undertaken throughout the courseof that relationship"</sub>

(p. 48)[^original]

  <sub>anchor: "The MT 103 can be straight through processable if the message is properly formatted"</sub>

  <sub>anchor: "The MT 202 COV must not be used for any other interbank transfer."</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: The glossary defines an ordering financial institution as the financial institution that initiates the wire transfer and transfers the funds upon receiving the request for a wire transfer on behalf of the originator. (p. 50)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "refers to the financial institution whichinitiates the wire transfer and transfers the funds upon receiving therequest"</sub>

  <sub>anchor: "Originator refers to the account holder who allows the wire transfer fromthat account"</sub>

(p. 50)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Serial payment refers to a direct sequential chain of payment where thewire transfer and accompanying payment message"</sub>

  <sub>anchor: "An upstream bank is a bank that provides correspondent bankingservices to another bank."</sub>

  <sub>anchor: "an upstream bank has to ensure thatit fulfils all requirements with respect to customer due diligence"</sub>

(p. 50)[^original]

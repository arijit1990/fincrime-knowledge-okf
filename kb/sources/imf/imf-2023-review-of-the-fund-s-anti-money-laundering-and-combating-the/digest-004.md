## F. CD Program

- **descriptive / finding or result**: Since 2018 the number of countries receiving bilateral AML/CFT technical assistance has stayed consistent across almost all regions despite a small pandemic dip; staff delivers about 40 TA projects per year. (p. 39)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "On average, staff delivers around 40 TA projects per year."</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The CD program relies heavily on the AML/CFT Topical Trust Fund and has modules on reform areas including legislation, risk assessment, national strategies, supervisors, FIUs, entity transparency, IFF, corruption proceeds and tax crimes. (p. 39)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The CD program relies heavily on the AML/CFT Topical Trust Fund (TTF)"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The AML/CFT TTF was launched in 2009 as a donor-supported trust fund and is now in its third phase. (p. 39)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "In 2009, the Fund launched the AML/CFT TTF, a donor-supported trust fund"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Current TTF donors are Canada, France, Germany, Korea, Japan, Luxembourg, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Switzerland and the Netherlands. (p. 39)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Current donors under the AML/CFT TTF (now in its third phase) are Canada, France, Germany"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Regional, multi-country and thematic projects have increased, covering more countries and enabling targeted, shorter engagements such as Fintech and digital money. (p. 39)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "In recent years, there has been an increase in regional, multi-country, and thematic projects"</sub>

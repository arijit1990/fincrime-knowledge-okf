## D. Fund-Supported Programs

- **descriptive / context**: Financial integrity issues continue to be addressed in Fund-supported programs in line with the 2018 review and the AML/CFT Guidance Note (2012), since AML/CFT deficiencies can affect macroeconomic and financial stability. (p. 29)[^original]

  <sub>anchor: "improving revenue collection by mitigating tax crimes"</sub>

## Box 5. Cross-Conditionality and FATF Listing

- **descriptive / requirement**: The Fund is fully responsible for establishing and monitoring all conditions attached to use of its resources; cross-conditionality, where use of Fund resources is subjected to other organisations' rules, is to be avoided. (p. 32)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "cross-conditionality is to be avoided, under which the use of Fund resources"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: The FATF's decision to list or exit a country cannot be made a condition in a Fund-supported program, as it is outside the member's control and the Fund's responsibility. (p. 32)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the FATF’s decision to include on or exit a member country from its list cannot be made a condition"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The Fund has played a good offices role, facilitating dialogue on underlying AML/CFT deficiencies, assessing FATF listing impacts on correspondent banking and remittances, and clarifying effectiveness criteria versus technical compliance. (p. 32)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The Fund has played a “good offices” role in facilitating dialogue among stakeholders"</sub>

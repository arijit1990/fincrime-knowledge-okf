## Press release

## Executive Board Assessment

  <sub>anchor: "They emphasized that staff should continue to rely on the multipronged approach and synergies"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Directors agreed staff should enhance understanding of financial integrity risks with a greater focus on assessing and mitigating negative macroeconomic impacts, to prioritise engagement depth and scope. (p. 4)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "with a greater focus on assessing and mitigating negative macroeconomic impacts"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Directors supported staff putting greater focus in surveillance on linkages between financial integrity issues and fiscal, financial sector and structural priorities. (p. 4)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "staff will put greater focus on the linkages between financial integrity issues and fiscal, financial sector, and structural priorities"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Directors agreed staff should cover financial integrity issues in surveillance on an evenhanded basis commensurate with risks: mandatory when macro-critical, voluntary when the member requests it. (p. 4)[^original]

## Executive Summary

  <sub>anchor: "continue to pose a threat to IMF members’ financial sectors and the broader economy"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Financial crimes can cause destabilising inflows and outflows, banking crises and pressure on correspondent banking relationships, and pose financial stability and reputational risks for financial centres. (p. 8)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Financial crimes can result in destabilizing inflows and outflows, banking crises, and pressure on correspondent banking relationships"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Financial crimes can also create asset bubbles, affect tax revenue, fuel the informal sector and weaken institutions. (p. 8)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "They can also create asset bubbles, affect tax revenue, fuel the development of the informal sector"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The Fund is described as being in a unique position to advise members on ML/TF risks that can have macro-critical impacts. (p. 8)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The Fund is in a unique position to provide policy advice to member countries to address these risks"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: AML/CFT coverage was mainstreamed into Fund surveillance and lending in 2012, after the significance of AML/CFT issues was recognised over the past two decades. (p. 8)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the recognition of the significance of AML/CFT issues led to mainstreaming the coverage in the Fund’s surveillance and lending in 2012"</sub>

## Introduction

- **descriptive / context**: Financial crimes threaten the integrity and stability of members' financial systems and the broader economy, with negative implications for inclusive and sustainable growth. (p. 14)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "As financial crimes continue to threaten the integrity3 and stability of Fund members’ financial systems"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: ML can cause destabilising inflows and outflows, banking crises, pressure on correspondent banking relationships, property asset bubbles and reputational risks for international financial centres. (p. 14)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "ML can result in destabilizing inflows and outflows of funds, as well as in banking crises"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Tax evasion reduces state revenue, corruption misallocates public resources, and disruptions can affect financial system stability and price formation, including in countries with low criminal activity. (p. 14)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "substantial disruptions may occur in the performance and stability of financial systems and in price formation"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Robust risk-based policies and effective measures to prevent and deter these crimes are described as essential to protect the integrity and stability of financial markets and the global financial system. (p. 14)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Robust risk-based policies and effective measures designed to prevent and deter these crimes are essential"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The Fund's AML/CFT program has evolved considerably since its origins in the early 2000s, and AML/CFT issues are relevant across a wide range of Fund policies such as fintech, governance and fragile and conflict-affected states. (p. 14)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The Fund’s AML/CFT program has evolved considerably since its origins in the early 2000s"</sub>

  <sub>anchor: "Financial integrity is a high-level goal of the international community. It is a broad concept"</sub>

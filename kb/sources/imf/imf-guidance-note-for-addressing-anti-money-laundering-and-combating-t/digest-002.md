## A. Definitions

- **expected / requirement**: Under domestic criminal law, the offence of money laundering should cover all serious predicate offences or financial crimes capable of generating illicit revenue, aiming at the widest range of offences. (p. 9)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Under domestic criminal law, the offense of ML should apply to all serious types of predicate offenses"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: Footnote 7: the 2012 FATF Recommendations now require countries to implement targeted financial sanctions against UNSC resolutions on proliferation of WMD and its financing. (p. 9)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The 2012 FATF Recommendations now impose a requirement on countries to implement TFS"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The footnote 7 proliferation-financing TFS requirement is narrower than the TF regime: it addresses exclusively freezing without delay of funds of persons designated by the UNSC, and domestic cooperation. (p. 9)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "designated by the UNSC as being involved in illicit proliferation of WMD and domestic cooperation"</sub>

- **descriptive / typology or red flag**: SDS with offshore or non-resident business models face elevated risk as transit or destination hubs, including non-resident misuse of opaque legal persons, economic citizenship schemes, laundering through weakly supervised banks with strict secrecy, and through real estate agents, lawyers and accountants.   <sub>anchor: "Risks include non-resident misuse of opaque legal persons and arrangements, economic citizenship/residency schemes"</sub>

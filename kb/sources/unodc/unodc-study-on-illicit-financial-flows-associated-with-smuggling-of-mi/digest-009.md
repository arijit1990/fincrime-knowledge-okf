## 2.1. Movement Costs Based on Interviews

- **descriptive / method**: The movement cost figures in section 2.1 are based on interviews conducted for the study and other available sources, as footnote 196 notes. (p. 73)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Information based on interviews conducted for this study and other available sources."</sub>
- **descriptive / data or feature**: The 2.1 table records, for each interviewee profile (nationality, sex and age), the journey route and mode where known and the cost paid. Page 73 covers Afghan male migrants aged 18 to 30 and aid workers travelling from Iraq. (p. 73)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Afghan male, 18|Afghanistan-Türkiye|$2,260"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: Reported movement costs for Afghan males on the Türkiye-Serbia route range from $1,695 (age 18) to $3,000 (age 19), and an Afghan male aged 30 paid €10,000 for the Afghanistan to Bosnia and Herzegovina route. (p. 73)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Afghan male, 30|Afghanistan- Bosnia and Herzegovina|€10,000"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: Page 74 reports costs for Iranian and Iraqi interviewees, including an Iranian male aged 28 paying €13,000-€15,000 for the Iran to Bosnia and Herzegovina route and an Iraqi female aged 41 paying $9,000 for the Türkiye to Denmark route. (p. 74)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Iranian male, 28|Islamic Republic of Iran-Bosnia and Herzegovina|€13,000-€15,000"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: Aid worker interviewees reported movement costs of $3,000 for Iraq to Europe via Türkiye, $1,000 for Iraq to Türkiye and $8,000 for Türkiye to Europe. (p. 74)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Aid workers|Türkiye-Europe|$8,000"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: Page 75 reports Iraqi male costs, including $5,000 for two people on the Türkiye to Greece route and $1,000 for two people on the Greece to Bosnia and Herzegovina route, and a Pakistani female aged 25 paying $6,780 for three people on the Serbia to Austria route. (p. 75)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Iraqi male, 44|Türkiye-Greece|$5,000 (for 2 people)"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: Page 75 also records Pakistani male costs ranging from $1,200 (Pakistan to Türkiye) to €5,000 (Pakistan to Bosnia and Herzegovina), and an Iraqi male aged 25 paying €1,000 for Istanbul to Izmir. (p. 75)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Pakistani male, 30|Pakistan-Bosnia and Herzegovina|€5,000"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: Page 76 includes costs reported by prosecutors and a senior inspector, such as €15,000 for Pakistan to EU countries, €1,500 for Serbia to Bosnia and Herzegovina, and €6,000 for Türkiye to France, Italy, Germany or the United Kingdom. (p. 76)[^original]

Examples include a visa for Türkiye at $2,800 per adult and $1,400 per child, a Bulgarian visa at $9,000, and a Serbian visa at $6,800 for an Afghan male aged 41. (p. 78)[^original]

## 2.3. Movement Costs Based on Cases

- **descriptive / finding or result**: In the Servitenkloster Smuggling Case, 278 mostly Pakistani migrants paid €6,500-€10,000 each for the Pakistan to Austria route, and the group generated a total of €3 million. (p. 79)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Group gene-rated a totalsum of €3million"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: Other case-based movement costs on page 79 include €7,000-€8,000 for one Iranian male (A.M. and Others v The Queen), €1,200 for one Iranian male (R v S.J.), €20 each for 10 migrants on foot from North Macedonia to Serbia, and €8,500-€11,000 each for 900 Bangladeshis (Federal Criminal Police, 2006). (p. 79)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Higher Court of Vranje, Km. No.25/16|10 Iraqis, Libyans andSomalis|North Macedonia-Serbia (byfoot)|€20 (each)"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: The Catania case (Proc. n. 2022/2014) concerned 323 Syrians, Asians and Africans, who paid €6,000-€6,500 each for a route from Istanbul via Beirut, Cyprus and Alexandria to Italy. (p. 79)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Court of Catania (Tribunale diCatania), Appeal against orderof precautionary detention"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: The Court of Appeals of Paris case (Dossier No. 11/05649, 2012) involved 93 mostly Iranian males. The cases table gives costs of €1,500 for Calais to the United Kingdom by lorry, €3,000 for Greece to France and €10,000 for Greece to Ireland. (p. 79)[^original]

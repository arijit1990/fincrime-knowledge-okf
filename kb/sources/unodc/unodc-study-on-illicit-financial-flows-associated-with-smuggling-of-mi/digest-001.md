## Smuggling of migrants

- **descriptive / context**: The Western Balkans route runs from Türkiye through Greece or Bulgaria, out of the EU through North Macedonia, Serbia, Bosnia and Herzegovina and/or Albania, and back into the EU via Hungary, Croatia or Romania. (p. 6)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Te Western Balkans route involves refugees and migrants leaving Türkiye, traveling through Greece or Bulgaria"</sub>
- **descriptive / case example**: The Central Mediterranean Route is dominated by Libyan smuggling networks charging an average of US$1,500 to US$1,800 per client to take migrants from Libya to Italy by boat. (p. 6)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Libyan smuggling networks, who charge an average of US$1,500 - $1,800 per client to smuggle them from Libya to Italy by boat"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: Organized groups on the Central Mediterranean route were estimated to have earned gross income of $150 million in 2017, the peak arrival year, falling to just over €24 million in 2018 and €12 million in 2019. (p. 6)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "organized criminal groups running smuggling operations along this route were estimated to have earned a gross income of $150 million"</sub>

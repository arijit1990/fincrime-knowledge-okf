## Industrialization of regional cyber-enabled fraud

- **descriptive / finding or result**: Cyber-enabled fraud in Southeast Asia has reached industrial proportions, using workforces of trafficked victims and complicit individuals. (p. 43)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Cyber-enabled fraud operations in Southeast Asia have taken on industrial proportions using workforces compromising of trafficked victims and complicit individuals."</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: In 2023 China's Supreme People's Procuratorate reported that independent fraud gangs had been replaced by larger consolidated groups often operating under the guise of industrial and science and technology parks, forming stable networks. (p. 43)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "independent and scattered fraud gangs had been replaced by larger, consolidated criminal groups often operating under the guise of industrial and science and technology parks."</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: The SPP noted that as these groups grew, their criminal activities converged to include trafficking in persons, kidnapping, illegal detention and torture, among other offences. (p. 43)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "escalating to trafficking in persons, kidnapping, illegal detention, and torture, among other offences."</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: Satellite imagery shows KK Park in Kayin State, Myanmar, first showing development signs in early 2020 and emerging within four years as one of the largest and most active criminal enclaves in the region. (p. 43)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "in the span of four years it has emerged as one of the largest and most active criminal enclaves in the region."</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Growing adoption of cryptocurrency and other rapidly integrated technologies is described as an important catalyst for regional cyber-enabled fraud operators expanding globally, due to ease of rapid cross-border transactions, limited law enforcement understanding of cryptocurrency, and breakdowns in cross-border law enforcement cooperation in some cases.

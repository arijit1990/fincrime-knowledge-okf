## CONCLUSION

- **descriptive / finding or result**: The framework has proved useful for assessing IFFs from trade in illegal commodities, as shown by tests in pilot countries; separating income generation from income management allows statistics and estimates on illegal markets (supply, demand, price, seizure data) to approximate IFFs, while a different set of information is needed to analyse how income is used, so IFFs can be assessed to varying degrees of comprehensiveness depending on data availability. (p. 25)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "has proved useful for assessing IFFs from trade in illegal commodities, as demonstrated by tests in pilot countries"</sub>
- **descriptive / limitation**: Some IFF elements are challenging to estimate, including bribery, abuse of functions, illicit enrichment and illicit tax practices, with data on these activities remaining scattered; further work is needed, starting with elements for which data exist and adjusting to avoid double-counting. (p. 25)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Some elements of IFFs are challenging to estimate, including bribery, abuse of functions, illicit enrichment and illicit tax practices"</sub>
- **permissive / limitation**: The conclusion says that in practice it may be necessary to estimate separately some of the IFFs with the highest uncertainties, such as those from tax and commercial activities. (p. 25)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "it may be necessary to estimate separately some of the IFFs with the highest uncertainties"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The next steps identified in the conclusion are to develop and test a range of statistical methodologies that are consistent with the related definitions. (p. 25)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The next steps involve the development and testing of a range of statistical methodologies"</sub>
- **expected / recommendation**: The conclusion says that, although countries already collect relevant data, it will be important to strengthen their capacity to improve data collection and the compilation of IFF statistics. (p. 25)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "it will be important to strengthen their capacity to improve data collection"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: UNCTAD and UNODC, working with partner organizations, will help countries improve their statistical capacity to understand and estimate IFFs. A series of pilot studies is under way and will help refine the conceptual framework and develop methodological guidance on measuring IFFs. (p. 25)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "UNCTAD and UNODC, with partner organizations, will support countries in improving their statistical capacity"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The statistical Task Force will keep supporting countries in national data collection and compilation, with a view to developing a practical compilation guide for measuring IFFs on the basis of the conceptual framework. The guide will include a classification of activities generating IFFs, linked to the types and channels of IFFs, with recommended measurement methods. Further work will also aim at a more nuanced measurement of IFFs to support policy action. (p. 25)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "developing a practical compilation guide for the measurement of IFFs based on this conceptual framework"</sub>
- **permissive / context**: The conclusion says that in the future it could be worth exploring the measurement of IFFs as satellite accounts within the broader framework of national accounts. (p. 25)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "it could be worth exploring the measurement of IFFs as satellite accounts"</sub>

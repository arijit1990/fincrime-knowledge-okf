## Appendix A: Summary of Recommendations

- **expected / recommendation**: Laws and regulations should make no distinction between domestic and foreign PEPs; FATF and standard setters should require similar enhanced due diligence for both. (p. 93)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Laws and regulations should make no distinction between domestic and foreign PEPs."</sub>
- **expected / recommendation**: At account opening and as needed thereafter, banks should require customers to complete a written declaration identifying the ultimate beneficial owner(s) as a first step in beneficial ownership CDD. (p. 93)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "banks should require custom- ers to complete a written declaration of the identity"</sub>
- **expected / recommendation**: PEP customers should be reviewed at least yearly by senior management or a committee including at least one senior manager using a risk-based approach, with results documented. (p. 93)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "PEP customers should be reviewed by senior management or a committee in- cluding at least one senior manager"</sub>
- **expected / recommendation**: Where a person has ceased to hold a prominent public function, countries should not introduce time limits on how long the person, family member or close associate is treated as a PEP. (p. 93)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "countries should not introduce time limits on the length of time the person"</sub>

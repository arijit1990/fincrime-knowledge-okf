## PREFACE

- **descriptive / finding or result**: The UNODC Global Report on Trafficking in Persons 2024 says the number of victims detected globally fell markedly during the COVID-19 pandemic but in 2022 rose sharply and surpassed pre-pandemic levels, up 25% compared with 2019. The preface says this may partly reflect improved detection capacity but is likely also a reflection of global fragility. (p. 5)[^original]

  <sub>anchor: "victims trafficked for forced labour now account for the biggest share of all victims around the world"</sub>

AGENCY: Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN), Treasury. ACTION: Interim final rule; request for comments.

SUMMARY: FinCEN is adopting this interim final rule to narrow the existing beneficial ownership information (BOI) reporting requirements under the Corporate Transparency Act (CTA) to require only entities previously defined

as ‘‘foreign reporting companies’’ to report BOI. Under this interim final rule, entities previously defined as ‘‘domestic reporting companies’’ are exempted from the reporting requirements and do not have to report BOI to FinCEN, or update or correct BOI previously reported to FinCEN. With limited exceptions, the interim final rule does not change the existing requirement for foreign reporting companies to file BOI reports, but it extends the deadline to file initial BOI reports, and to update or correct previously filed BOI reports, to 30 days from the date of this publication to give foreign reporting companies additional time to comply. However, the interim final rule exempts foreign reporting companies from having to report the BOI of any U.S. persons who are

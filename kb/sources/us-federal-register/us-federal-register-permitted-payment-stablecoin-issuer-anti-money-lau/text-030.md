## TABLE 23—TOTAL COST IN YEAR 1—Continued


### Subpart F—Special Standards of Diligence; Prohibitions, and Special Measures for Permitted Payment Stablecoin Issuers


#### § 1033.610 Due diligence programs for correspondent accounts for foreign financial institutions.


[p. 86]

For regulations regarding due diligence programs for correspondent accounts for foreign financial institutions, refer to § 1010.610 of this chapter.

For regulations regarding due diligence programs for private banking accounts, refer to § 1010.620 of this chapter.

For regulations regarding prohibition on correspondent accounts for foreign shell banks and related provisions refer to § 1010.630 of this chapter.

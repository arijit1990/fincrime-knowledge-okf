## Safety and soundness: Model risk management – PRA

- **descriptive / limitation**: Paragraph 4.37: model risk management (MRM) is becoming increasingly important as a primary framework for some firms to manage AI-related risks, but the current scope of UK MRM regulation is very limited, with principles and regulation only for models in specific areas (e.g. internal capital models, stress-testing models), it does not explicitly mention AI, and there is no explicit guidance on explainability/interpretability of AI models. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "there is currently no explicit guidance on issues like model explainability/interpretability of AI models"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The PRA published CP6/22 on 21 June 2022 proposing MRM principles to be embedded as supervisory expectations in a new supervisory statement; the proposed principles (Box 5) cover model identification and model risk classification, governance, model development, implementation and use, independent model validation, and model risk mitigants. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The PRA published CP6/22 on Tuesday 21 June 2022"</sub>

## Who should be accountable for AI under the SM&CR?

- **descriptive / context**: Paragraph 4.50: the SM&CR currently has no dedicated senior management function (SMF) for AI; technology systems are at present the responsibility of SMF24 (Chief Operations function), while SMF4 (Chief Risk function) is responsible for overall management of the firm's risk controls, including setting and managing risk exposures.

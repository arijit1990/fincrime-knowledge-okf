(1) Information which is held by or on behalf of a permitted person (whether it was

obtained before or after the coming into force of this section) may be disclosed to the Lord Advocate in connection with the exercise of any of his functions under Part 3 or to the Scottish Ministers in connection with the exercise of any of their functions under Part 5 [F1870or 8].

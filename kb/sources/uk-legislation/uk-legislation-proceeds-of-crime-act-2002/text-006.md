“A” is the total of the values of all relevant property as decided in accordance with section 80(3A) or (3B) (as appropriate), and

[F13022A Increased available amount: compensation directions

(1) This section applies where under section 22(4) a court varies a confiscation order so

as to increase the amount required to be paid under the order.

(2) The court may make a supplementary compensation direction if—

(a) a compensation order has been made against the defendant in respect of the offence (or any of the offences) concerned, and (b) at the time the compensation order was made, the amount of the compensatable loss that had been sustained by the person in whose favour it was made was greater than the amount required to be paid by the compensation order.

(3) A supplementary compensation direction is a direction that so much of the amount

recovered under the confiscation order as the court considers appropriate is to be paid to the person in whose favour the compensation order was made.

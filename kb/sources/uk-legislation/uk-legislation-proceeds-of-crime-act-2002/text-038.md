(a) a warrant committing the defendant to prison or detention is issued for a default in payment of an amount ordered to be paid under a confiscation order in respect of an offence or offences, and (b) at the time the warrant is issued the defendant is liable to serve a term of custody in respect of the offence (or any of the offences).

of payment of the amount does not begin to run until after the term mentioned in subsection (1)(b) above.

190 Restraint orders

(a restraint order) prohibiting any specified person from dealing with any realisable property held by him.

(a) to all realisable property held by the specified person whether or not the property is described in the order; (b) to realisable property transferred to the specified person after the order is made.

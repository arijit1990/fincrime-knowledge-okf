790EB Company’s duty to notify failure to comply with notices

(1) A company must notify the registrar if a person fails to comply with a notice

given by the company under section 790D, 790DA, 790E or 790EA within the period specified in it.

end of the period specified in the notice under section 790D, 790DA, 790E or 790EA.

790EC Company’s duty to notify of late compliance with notices

(1) A company must notify the registrar if a person who has failed to comply

with a notice given by the company under section 790D, 790DA, 790E or 790EA within the period specified in it subsequently complies.

(2) The notice must be given within the period of 14 days beginning with the day

on which the person complied with the notice under section 790D, 790DA, 790E or 790EA.”

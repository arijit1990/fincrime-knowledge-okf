## Article 1

- **descriptive / definition**: A State or governmental facility means any permanent or temporary facility or conveyance used or occupied by State representatives, members of government, the legislature or judiciary, officials or employees of a State or public authority, or employees of an intergovernmental organization in connection with their official duties.   <sub>anchor: "any permanent or temporary facility or conveyance that is used or occupied by representatives of a State"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: Proceeds means any funds derived from or obtained, directly or indirectly, through the commission of an offence set forth in article 2. (p. 1)[^original]

## Article 2

- **descriptive / definition**: A person commits an offence if, by any means, directly or indirectly, unlawfully and wilfully, the person provides or collects funds intending or knowing they will be used, in full or in part, to carry out an act covered by a treaty listed in the annex or any other act intended to cause death or serious bodily injury to a civilian or non-combatant to intimidate a population or compel a government or international organisation.   <sub>anchor: "the treaty shall be deemed not to be included in the annex referred to in paragraph 1"</sub>

  <sub>anchor: "When a State Party ceases to be a party to a treaty listed in the annex, it may make a declaration"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: The offence does not require that the funds were actually used to carry out an offence. (p. 1)[^original]

## Article 3

- **descriptive / definition**: The Convention does not apply where the offence is committed within a single State, the alleged offender is a national of that State and present in its territory, and no other State has jurisdiction basis under article 7(1) or 7(2); articles 12 to 18 still apply as appropriate.

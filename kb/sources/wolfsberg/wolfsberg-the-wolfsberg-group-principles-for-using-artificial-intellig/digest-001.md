## Introduction

- **descriptive / context**: The Wolfsberg Group supports the use of AI/ML by FIs in their financial crime compliance programmes and believes it is critical for FIs to consider data ethics principles when using these technologies. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "believes that it is critical for FIs to consider data ethics principles when using these technologies"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: By leveraging data science advances underpinning AI/ML, FIs can holistically analyse customer and transactional data more effectively and efficiently to detect, investigate, and manage financial crime risk and satisfy regulatory requirements; identifying potential criminal activity more effectively lets FIs focus controls with greater precision on the highest-risk customers and transactions, reducing manual reviews and customer friction such as transaction delays or redundant inquiries. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "reducing manual reviews and customer friction such as transaction delays or redundant inquiries"</sub>
- **descriptive / limitation**: AI/ML technology solutions may require FIs to consolidate and process large amounts of data from multiple sources. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "may require FIs to consolidate and process large amounts of data, from multiple sources"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: FIs should understand the potential impact of the use of AI/ML technologies before implementation to ensure that it results in fair, effective, and explainable outcomes. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "FIs should understand the potential impact of the use of these technologies before implementation"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: FIs should monitor the use of AI/ML for consistent and stable performance after implementation. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "FIs should also monitor the use of AI/ML for consistent and stable performance after implementation"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: The Principles should be operationalised by each FI according to a risk-based approach dependent on the prevailing and evolving regulatory landscape and on its use of AI/ML against financial crime, and governed accordingly. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "should be operationalised by each FI according to a risk-based approach"</sub>

## The Wolfsberg Principles for Responsible AI/ML

- **descriptive / context**: The Wolfsberg Principles for Using AI/ML in Financial Crime Compliance consist of five elements: Legitimate Purpose; Proportionate Use; Design and Technical Expertise; Accountability and Oversight; and Openness and Transparency. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The Principles consist of five elements that support an FI’s responsible use of AI/ML"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Under Legitimate Purpose, a key consideration for FIs implementing AI/ML is how to integrate an assessment of ethical and operational risks into their risk governance approach. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "how to integrate an assessment of ethical and operational risks into their risk governance approach"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Proportionate Use: FIs should ensure that, in developing and using AI/ML solutions for financial crimes compliance, they balance the benefits of use with appropriate management of the risks that may arise from these technologies. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "they are balancing the benefits of use with appropriate management of the risks"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Under Proportionate Use, the severity of potential financial crimes risk should be appropriately assessed against any AI/ML solution's margin for error. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the severity of potential financial crimes risk should be appropriately assessed against any AI/ML solutions’ margin for error"</sub>
- **descriptive / expectation**: Accountability and Oversight: FIs are responsible for their use of AI/ML, including decisions that rely on AI/ML analysis, regardless of whether the AI/ML systems are developed in-house or sourced externally. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "regardless of whether the AI/ML systems are developed in-house or sourced externally"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Under Accountability and Oversight, FIs should train staff on the appropriate use of AI/ML technologies. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "FIs should train staff on the appropriate use of these technologies"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: FIs should establish processes to challenge their technical teams whenever necessary and probe the use of data within their organisations. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "FIs should also establish processes to challenge their technical teams whenever necessary"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Openness and Transparency: FIs should be open and transparent about their use of AI/ML, consistent with legal and regulatory requirements. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "FIs should be open and transparent about their use of AI/ML, consistent with legal and regulatory requirements"</sub>
- **recommended / recommendation**: FIs should consider engaging with regulators and educating customers about the risks and benefits of using AI/ML to prevent and detect financial crime. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "FIs should consider engaging with regulators and educating customers about the risks and benefits of using AI/ML"</sub>

## Introduction

- **descriptive / context**: Building on existing regulatory, industry and academic material on data ethics, the Wolfsberg Group wrote the Principles to guide FIs, their financial crime compliance leaders and their risk management teams in identifying and managing the operational and reputational risks that using AI/ML can create. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "to guide FIs and their financial crime compliance leaders and risk management teams"</sub>

## The Wolfsberg Principles for Responsible AI/ML

- **descriptive / context**: Legitimate Purpose: FI financial crime programmes rest on regulatory requirements and on a commitment to help protect the integrity of the financial system while achieving fair and effective outcomes. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "anchored in regulatory requirements, and a commitment to help safeguard the integrity of the financial system"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Under Legitimate Purpose, data used in AI/ML solutions adopted for financial crimes compliance should not be allowed to support other activities unless it first undergoes additional review under the FI's data and risk management framework. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "should not be allowed to support other activities without additional review under the FI"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Under Proportionate Use, FIs should put in place a programme that regularly validates how AI/ML is used and configured, which helps keep the use of data proportionate to the legitimate, intended financial crimes compliance purpose. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "FIs should implement a programme that validates the use and configuration of AI/ML regularly"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Design and Technical Expertise: to avoid ineffective financial crime risk management, FIs should keep careful control of the technology they depend on and understand what its use implies, where it is limited and what follows from using it. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "FIs should carefully control the technology they rely on and understand the implications, limitations, and consequences"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Teams that create, monitor and control AI/ML should be staffed with people who have suitable skills and varied experience, so that bias in the results can be spotted. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "should be composed of staff with the appropriate skills and diverse experiences needed to identify bias in the results"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: AI/ML system design should start from a clear statement of the intended outcomes and should make sure results can be adequately explained or evidenced given the data inputs. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Design of AI/ML systems should be driven by a clear definition of the intended outcomes"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Senior stakeholders in the FI should be given enough information about, and understanding of, AI/ML tools and their risks and benefits to make informed decisions on when and how the technology is used. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Senior stakeholders within the FI should have sufficient information on, and understanding of, AI/ML tools"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: FIs should build in a well-designed programme of continuing testing, validation and re-configuration, so that AI/ML outcomes are reviewed against the intended purpose and the Principles. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "FIs should incorporate a well-designed programme of ongoing testing, validation, and re-configuration"</sub>
- **recommended / recommendation**: Under Accountability and Oversight, FIs should consider having people with specific responsibility for the ethical use of data in AI/ML oversee their design and technical teams, which may be done through existing risk or data management frameworks. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "consider oversight of their design and technical teams by persons with specific responsibility for the ethical use of data"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Under Openness and Transparency, care should be taken so that openness about AI/ML does not help people evade the industry's financial crime capabilities or inadvertently breach reporting confidentiality requirements or other data protection obligations. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "care should be taken to ensure that this transparency does not facilitate evasion"</sub>

## Conclusion

- **recommended / recommendation**: When weighing up AI/ML solutions for financial crime and risk management challenges, FIs should consider whether their use of AI/ML fits their organisation's core values as well as regulatory requirements. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "FIs should consider how their use of AI/ML aligns with their organisation"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Existing risk management and control structures should be adapted and extended so that operational and reputational risks created by AI/ML are identified and managed within the FI's overall risk management approach. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Existing risk management and controls structures should be adapted and expanded"</sub>
- **permissive / context**: AI/ML solutions can make the detection, investigation and management of financial crime more effective and efficient. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "AI/ML solutions can improve the effectiveness and efficiency of the detection, investigation, and management of financial crime"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Ethical concerns about using AI/ML to manage financial crime risk should be considered and addressed. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "ethical concerns around the use of AI/ML to manage financial crime risk should be considered and addressed"</sub>

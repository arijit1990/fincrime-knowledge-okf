  <sub>anchor: "See The Wolfsberg Group – Statement on Effectiveness (2019), Developing an Effective AML/CTF Programme (2020)"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: In the document, "PSP" captures the full spectrum of payment service institutions providing fund transfers, including credit transfers, direct debit, money remittances (domestic or cross-border), and transfers using a payment card, electronic money instrument, mobile phone or any other digital or IT prepaid or postpaid device with similar characteristics. It includes traditional banks and MSBs as well as third party payment processors and electronic money institutions, among other PSP types. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "PSP is used to capture the full spectrum of payment service institutions that provide fund transfers"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: For the paper, intermediary agent PSPs are defined as the intermediary FI(s) in a payment chain other than the debtor agent PSP and creditor agent PSP. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "we have defined intermediary agent PSPs as the intermediary FI(s) in a payment chain"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: "Competent authorities" is used as defined in the glossary of the FATF Recommendations, referring to all public authorities with designated responsibilities for combatting money laundering and/or terrorist financing. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "all public authorities with designated responsibilities for combatting money laundering and/or terrorist financing"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: "Bundled" is used generically to capture any form of aggregation of payments, sometimes called "batch" or "bulk" payments. "Cross-border" describes transactions where the debtor agent PSP, intermediary agent PSP or creditor agent PSP are in different jurisdictions. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "to capture any form of aggregation of payments, sometimes referred to as"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: A footnote notes that in certain instances the same BIC may be shared among multiple entities. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "In certain instances, the same BIC may be shared among multiple entities."</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: A footnote states that the decision to screen the underlying debtors/creditors should be determined by the PSPs, on a risk-based approach, based on the role they play in the payment chain. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The decision to screen the underlying debtors/creditors should be determined by the PSPs, on a risk-based approach"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: A footnote describes a CBDC as a digital asset or digital form of money that a central bank issues for wholesale or retail use, denominated in the national unit of account and constituting a direct liability of the central bank in one fiat sovereign currency. It says CBDCs differ from virtual currencies because they are legal tender backed by a Central Bank or Government authority, and that they may or may not sit on a distributed ledger technology. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "representing a direct liability of a central bank in a single fiat sovereign currency"</sub>
- **recommended / recommendation**: A footnote says that when FIs develop policies on using identifiers rather than names, they should consider industry publications, such as the Payment Market Practices Group's Global adoption of the LEI in ISO 20022 Payment Messages 2021. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "In developing their policies on usage of identifiers rather than names, FIs should consider industry publications"</sub>

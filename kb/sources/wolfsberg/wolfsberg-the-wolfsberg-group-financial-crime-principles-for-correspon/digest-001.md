## 2. Correspondent Banking

- **descriptive / context**: The degree of risk in a Correspondent Relationship depends on the risks posed by the nature of the relationship. It is generally higher where the bank receiving the services (Respondent) uses the relationship to provide banking services to its own customers, because the providing FI (Correspondent) then acts as an intermediary for underlying customers of another Bank. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the degree of risk is generally higher where the bank receiving the services (Respondent) uses its Correspondent Relationship"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Where the Correspondent acts as intermediary for the Respondent's underlying customers, this activity may warrant a higher level of due diligence compared with dealing with another Bank on a purely principal to principal basis. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "may warrant a higher level of due diligence compared to when a Bank deals with another Bank"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The scope of the relationship and the extent of products and services supplied vary according to the needs of the Respondent and the Correspondent's ability and willingness to supply them. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "will vary according to the needs of the Respondent, and the Correspondent"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: A Correspondent Banking Relationship is characterised by its on-going, repetitive nature and does not generally exist in the context of one-off transactions. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "characterised by its on-going, repetitive nature and does not generally exist in the context of one-off transactions"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The Principles extend to all Correspondent Banking Relationships an FI establishes or maintains for another Respondent, including where the Respondent is a branch, subsidiary, or affiliate of that Institution. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "including those where the Respondent is a branch, subsidiary, or affiliate of that Institution"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Institutions should be mindful that some jurisdictions define Correspondent Banking more broadly than what is generally considered Correspondent Banking activity. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "some jurisdictions define Correspondent Banking in terms that are broader than what is generally considered"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: The Principles shall be implemented in a manner consistent with any applicable local requirements. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "these Principles shall be implemented in a manner that is consistent with any applicable local requirements"</sub>
- **permissive / recommendation**: Although the Principles address relationships with other Banks, Institutions may decide, on a risk-based approach, to extend them to all Correspondent Relationships maintained for Non-Bank Financial Institutions (NBFIs) and Payment Service Providers (PSPs), including but not limited to MSBs/MVTS, FinTechs, VASPs and new payment method (NPM) companies. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Institutions may decide, on a risk-based approach, to extend them to all the Correspondent Relationships"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Because correspondent relationships for NBFIs and PSPs may facilitate transactions representing the same or greater risk, Institutions should determine whether to apply these principles to those relationships. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Institutions should determine whether to apply these principles to those relationships"</sub>

## 3. Responsibility and Oversight

- **mandatory / requirement**: Institutions shall define policies and procedures that require specified personnel to be responsible for ensuring compliance with their Correspondent Banking activities, including these Principles, and shall ensure such personnel have relevant experience and have undergone training on the risks involved in Correspondent Banking. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Institutions shall define policies and procedures which require specified personnel to be responsible for ensuring compliance"</sub>
- **recommended / recommendation**: A formal governance body with specific oversight of Correspondent Banking, inclusive of on-boarding, monitoring, and escalations, should be considered for this purpose. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "A formal governance body with specific oversight of Correspondent Banking, inclusive of on-boarding, monitoring, and escalations, should be considered"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: The governance body should include representation from the first and second lines of defence. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The governance body should include representation from the first and second lines of defence"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: The policies and procedures also shall provide for independent review by appropriate personnel to ensure continued compliance. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "shall provide for independent review by appropriate personnel to ensure continued compliance"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: An Institution shall define an acceptable risk appetite which has been approved by their Board or other similar senior stakeholders. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "An Institution shall define an acceptable risk appetite which has been approved by their Board or other similar senior stakeholders"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: The Correspondent Bank's risk appetite should set out the types of parties and transactional activity that the Correspondent prohibits or limits from being processed through its Respondents' account(s), and this should be communicated internally and to its Respondent customers. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "should set out the different types of parties and transactional activity the Correspondent prohibits or limits"</sub>

## 4. Risk Based Due Diligence Guidelines / Considerations

- **mandatory / requirement**: The regulatory environment must be assessed in conjunction with other information obtained related to the specific Respondent. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The regulatory environment must be assessed in conjunction with other information obtained related to the specific Respondent"</sub>
- **permissive / recommendation**: In these instances, an Institution may also rely on publicly available information obtained from the Respondent or reliable third parties (regulators, exchanges, shared utilities, the Wolfsberg Correspondent Bank Due Diligence Questionnaire (CBDDQ), etc.) to satisfy its due diligence requirements. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "an Institution may also rely on publicly available information obtained either from the Respondent or reliable third parties"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: The Institution's policies and procedures shall require that the Respondent's information be reviewed and updated on a defined risk based, periodic basis. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "shall require that the Respondent’s information be reviewed and updated on a defined risk based, periodic basis"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: In conducting due diligence on any Respondent Bank, the listed elements addressing specific risk indicators shall be considered, as appropriate. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the elements set out below to address specific risk indicators shall be considered, as appropriate"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: Institutions shall review pronouncements from regulatory agencies and applicable international bodies, such as the FATF, to evaluate the degree of risk presented by the jurisdiction in which the Respondent is based, the jurisdiction in which its ultimate parent is headquartered, and the jurisdictions of those with whom they conduct business. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Institutions shall review pronouncements from regulatory agencies and applicable international bodies"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: Where an entity affiliated with the Institution is the account holder, it is also a Respondent that is generally subject to the due diligence measures in these Principles; the level and scope of due diligence on it shall depend on the level of control exercised by the parent Institution, facts unique to that branch, subsidiary or affiliate, and regulatory standards and risks applying in that entity's jurisdiction. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The level and scope of due diligence on such a Respondent shall be dependent upon the level of control exercised"</sub>
- **recommended / expectation**: As with branches, subsidiaries and affiliates assessed as Respondents, an FI should consider factors unique to its own affiliated entities, branches or subsidiaries which may dictate the level of enhanced due diligence (EDD) to be performed. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "an FI should consider factors that may be unique to its own affiliated entities, branches or subsidiaries"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: For Respondents that are branches, subsidiaries or affiliates of other financial groups, the level and scope of due diligence shall be determined after considering the relationship between the Respondent and its ultimate parent (if any). (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "shall be made after considering the relationship between the Respondent and its ultimate parent (if any)"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: In general, where the parent is also a Respondent, the FCC programme of the Respondent Bank parent and the extent of its oversight of the branch, subsidiary and/or affiliate in determining compliance with that programme shall be considered in determining the extent of required due diligence on those entities. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the FCC programme of the Respondent Bank parent and extent of oversight of the branch, subsidiary and/or affiliate"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: Where the Respondent Bank is a branch, subsidiary or affiliate, the entity shall be reviewed to determine if it has a comparable programme. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the entity shall be reviewed to determine if it has a comparable programme"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Certain facts unique to the branch, subsidiary or affiliate may dictate the level of EDD to be performed, particularly local, product or regulatory standards within the specific jurisdiction, as well as unique factors such as customer base or a branch operating under an offshore banking license. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "certain facts unique to the branch, subsidiary or affiliate may dictate the level of EDD to be performed"</sub>
- **permissive / recommendation**: The structure and experience of Executive Management (e.g. most senior executives in charge of day-to-day business) may also be appropriate for consideration given their influence and control over the Respondent; depending on circumstances this may include members of the Board of Directors, Supervisory Board, Executive Committee or equivalent. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "may also be appropriate for consideration given their influence and control over the Respondent"</sub>
- **descriptive / typology or red flag**: The presence of any politically exposed persons (PEPs) in the Executive Management or ownership structure is an important consideration, particularly where the PEPs have day to day control over the Respondent. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The presence of any politically exposed persons (PEPs) in the Executive Management or ownership structure is also an important consideration"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: For all significant controlling interests, the ultimate beneficial owners, sources of wealth and background (including reputation in the marketplace, particularly regarding negative or adverse financial crime matters) and recent material ownership changes shall be ascertained to the extent available through inquiry or public sources. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the ultimate beneficial owners, sources of wealth and background, including their reputation in the marketplace"</sub>
- **descriptive / typology or red flag**: Products and Services Offered by the Respondent: the financial products and services the Respondent offers to its own customers, and the markets it serves, may present greater risks. Involvement in certain higher-risk business segments and products or services generally recognised as vulnerable to money laundering, corruption, terrorist financing or sanctions evasion may present additional risks. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "may present additional risks and shall be considered in conjunction with the Respondent"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: Higher-risk business segments and products or services recognised as vulnerable to financial crime shall be considered in conjunction with the Respondent's controls to address such risks. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "shall be considered in conjunction with the Respondent’s controls to address such risks"</sub>
- **descriptive / typology or red flag**: Increased risk factors include provision of cross-border payment transactions, products offered to non-customers, products involving the movement of physical currency, and products that do not provide full transparency into the payment flows. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "provision of cross-border payment transactions, products offered to non-customers, products which involve the movement of physical currency"</sub>
- **descriptive / typology or red flag**: Respondent Bank's Customer Base: a Respondent that derives a substantial part of its business income from customers posing elevated risk due to the nature of their business, or the jurisdictions in which they operate, may present greater risk itself. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "derives a substantial part of its business income from customers posing elevated risk"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: Each Institution offering Correspondent Banking activity shall assess the customer-base factors and the Respondent's associated controls and determine whether this activity is within its risk appetite. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "shall assess these factors and the Respondent"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Any potential activity outside the Institution's risk appetite should be communicated to the Respondent Bank. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Any potential activity outside the Institution"</sub>
- **recommended / expectation**: Products or Services Offered to the Respondent Bank: certain products are more vulnerable to financial crime, and in offering them the Institution should consider all relevant risk factors, including its own ability to monitor the transactions for unusual activity. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "in offering those the Institution should consider all relevant risk factors"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: The business purpose(s) for the relationship with the Respondent and expected activity for the products and services offered should be documented and evaluated for reasonability, and referred to during the life of the relationship to help identify activity inconsistent with the documented expectations. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "should be documented and evaluated for reasonability and referred to during the life of the relationship"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: The Institution shall determine whether the Respondent has been the subject of any relevant, material regulatory action and assess how far it is relevant to establishing/continuing the relationship or whether enhanced risk mitigation measures may be appropriate. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The Institution shall determine if the Respondent has been the subject of any relevant, material regulatory action"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: The extent to which an Institution enquires about the Respondent's FCC programme will depend upon the risks presented. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The extent to which an Institution will enquire about the FCC programme will depend upon the risks presented"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: If appropriate, the Institution should speak with representatives of the Respondent to obtain additional information, review FCC controls and corroborate findings. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the Institution should speak with representatives of the Respondent to obtain additional information"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: No Business Arrangements with Shell Banks: the Institution shall confirm that the Respondent is not a Shell Bank and does not provide products or services to Shell banks. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The Institution shall confirm that the Respondent is not a Shell Bank"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Customer Visit: unless other measures suffice, a representative of the Institution should visit the Respondent Bank at its premises, prior to or within a reasonable period after establishing the relationship, to support the customer due diligence process. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "a representative of the Institution should visit the Respondent Bank at their premises"</sub>
- **permissive / recommendation**: Site visits by financial crime subject-matter experts may also be conducted if deemed necessary. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Site visits by financial crime subject-matter experts may also be conducted if deemed necessary"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Institutions should make risk-based decisions on measures to take if a customer visit is not possible. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Institutions should make risk-based decisions on measures to take if a customer visit is not possible"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: The Group has developed a CBDDQ which should be used to collect the related customer due diligence information, and has published extensive guidance materials on the CBDDQ. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "CBDDQ which should be used to collect the related customer due diligence information"</sub>

## 5. Enhanced Due Diligence

- **mandatory / requirement**: In addition to due diligence, each Institution shall apply enhanced due diligence (EDD) to those Respondents which present greater risks. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "each Institution shall also apply enhanced due diligence (EDD) to those Respondents which present greater risks"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: The EDD process shall involve further consideration of the listed elements, designed to satisfy the Institution that it has secured a greater level of understanding. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "designed to satisfy the Institution that it has secured a greater level of understanding"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: PEP Involvement: if a PEP appears to have involvement in the Respondent, the Institution shall ensure it understands the person, their role and its appropriateness, their ability to influence the customer, and the risk they may present to the relationship. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "If a PEP appears to have involvement in the Respondent, the Institution shall ensure it has an understanding"</sub>

## 2. Correspondent Banking

- **descriptive / definition**: The Wolfsberg Correspondent Banking Principles describe a Correspondent Relationship as a business relationship in which a financial institution supplies commercial or business products or services to another Bank. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "A Correspondent Relationship is a business relationship provided by a financial institution (FI) for another Bank"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: For the purposes of the Principles, Correspondent Banking activity, and therefore a Correspondent Banking Relationship, is specifically one Bank providing banking-related services to another Bank so that it can carry out third-party payments, trade transactions and paper clearing in a given currency; the services can include a current or other liability account and related services. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the provision of banking-related services by one Bank to another Bank, for the execution of third-party payments, trade transactions"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Through the Correspondent, a Respondent can offer cross-border products and services to its own customers, who may be individuals, legal entities or FIs; the Correspondent in effect acts as the Respondent's agent or conduit when it executes payments or other transactions for those customers. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "A Correspondent bank enables its Respondent customers to provide their own customers with cross-border products and services"</sub>

## 4. Risk Based Due Diligence Guidelines / Considerations

- **mandatory / requirement**: Every Respondent customer shall undergo appropriate due diligence aimed at satisfying the Institution that it is comfortable doing business with that Respondent, taking account of the Respondent's risk profile and the nature of the relationship. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "All Respondent customers shall be subjected to appropriate due diligence that will seek to satisfy an Institution"</sub>
- **permissive / recommendation**: An Institution may find it appropriate to take into account, without relying on it alone, that a Respondent operates in or is subject to a regulatory environment recognised internationally as adequate against financial crime. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "It may be appropriate for an Institution to consider, but not rely on solely"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: In assessing risk and deciding the level of due diligence, the Institution must take into account the specific Respondent's risk (whether a parent, a subsidiary or branch of that parent, or an entity affiliated with the Institution) and the potential financial crime risk of the services provided to that customer. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The evaluation of risk and level of due diligence must consider the particular risk of the Respondent"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: A trigger event, such as relevant financial-crime adverse media, adverse customer behaviour or escalations about unusual activity, that materially changes a Respondent's risk profile shall lead to the relationship being re-evaluated. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "a trigger event, e.g. relevant financial crime-related adverse media, adverse customer behaviour or escalations related to unusual activity"</sub>
- **descriptive / typology or red flag**: On the Respondent Bank's geographic risk, the Principles note that some jurisdictions are internationally recognised as having inadequate financial crime standards or weak regulatory supervision and so carry greater risk of corruption, terrorist financing, fraud, tax evasion or sanctions evasion, while jurisdictions with more robust regulation present lower risk. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Certain jurisdictions are recognised internationally as having inadequate financial crime standards or insufficient regulatory supervision"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: How effective the relevant supervisory regime is must be considered, especially where that regime has been found deficient in applying global financial crime standards. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The effectiveness of the particular supervisory regime must be considered"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Where a sub-region of a country carries specific risks, those risks should be factored into the assessment of the Respondent's geographic risk. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "a sub-region of a country may be found to have specific risks that should be considered"</sub>
- **descriptive / typology or red flag**: The Principles state that a Respondent's ownership and management structure can raise risk. Relevant considerations include where the owners are domiciled and their reputation, the Respondent's corporate legal form, whether it is state-owned, publicly-held or privately-owned, how transparent the ownership is, and whether its shares trade on an exchange in a jurisdiction with an adequately recognised regulatory regime. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "The ownership and management structure of the Respondent may present increased risks"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: Where there is evidence of associated adverse reputational risk, the Institution shall consider the reputation of the Respondent's Executive Management in more detail, along with recent material changes in that management and the identity of any significant controlling individuals. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "shall be considered where there is evidence of any associated adverse reputational risk"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: On regulatory status and history, the Institution shall take reasonable measures to verify that the Respondent is regulated and duly licensed in the jurisdictions where it operates. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Reasonable measures shall be taken to verify that the Respondent is subject to regulatory oversight"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: On financial crime controls, the Institution shall, on a risk-based approach, evaluate the quality of the Respondent's FCC programme, including whether it meets internationally recognised standards and whether it adequately mitigates the risk from the Respondent's products, customer base and jurisdiction. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the Institution shall evaluate the quality of the Respondent"</sub>

## 5. Enhanced Due Diligence

- **descriptive / definition**: A downstream (or nested) FI arrangement exists when a Respondent offers correspondent banking services to other FIs, in or outside its own country, so as to support products and services for those FIs' customers; the example given is a regional savings bank serving local savings banks in its area. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "arrangement occurs when a Respondent provides Correspondent Banking services to other FIs, domiciled inside or outside"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Correspondent banking relationships let FIs reach financial services in other jurisdictions and offer their customers cross-border payment services, which supports international trade and financial inclusion. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "supporting international trade and financial inclusion"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: Where a Respondent offers downstream correspondent services to other FIs, the Institution (Correspondent) shall take reasonable steps to understand what types of FIs receive those services. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "shall take reasonable steps to understand the types of FIs to whom the Respondent offers the downstream correspondent services"</sub>
- **permissive / recommendation**: Those reasonable steps may cover the types of downstream FIs, the scale of the services, the geographic location of the downstream FIs and their customers, and any issues identified with the Respondent or with the downstream FIs' customers. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "These steps may include consideration of the types, scale of services and geographic location of downstream FI(s)"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: Approval of higher-risk Correspondent Banking relationships at on-boarding shall require a higher level of first-line-of-defence approval, and the relationship shall be reviewed periodically. (p. 1)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "shall be subject to a higher level of approvals by the first line of defence"</sub>

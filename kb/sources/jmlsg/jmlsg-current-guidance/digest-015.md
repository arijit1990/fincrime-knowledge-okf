- **mandatory / requirement**: All of these additional EDD measures must be applied to new and existing customers, but their extent may be considered and adjusted based on the level of risk of the customer (see also Annex 5-IV). (p. 134)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "All of these additional EDD measures must be applied to new and existing customers"</sub>
- **mandatory / requirement**: Regulation 33(4A); (4B) / 5.5.12: firms that provide life insurance policies must consider the nature and identity of the policy beneficiary when assessing whether there is a high risk of ML/TF, and the extent of measures to manage and mitigate that risk. (p. 134)[^original]

(p. 134)[^original]

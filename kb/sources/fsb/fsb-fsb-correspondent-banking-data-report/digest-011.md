## Annex 2: Jurisdiction by jurisdiction information

- **descriptive / context**: The Annex 2 table lists each jurisdiction with its ISO code and two data sets: SWIFT data showing the percentage change 2012-2016 in message volume, value and number of correspondent banking relationships (CBR), and results of the FSB-CBCG survey (responses by 150 correspondents). Survey columns cover exits and restrictions between January 2011 and June 2016, percentage exits, CBR in the sample as of June 2016, and the number of accounts held with surveyed banks as of June 2016 in USD, EUR, GBP and all currencies.

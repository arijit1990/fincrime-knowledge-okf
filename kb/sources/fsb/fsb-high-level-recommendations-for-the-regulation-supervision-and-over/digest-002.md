## Recommendation 9: Comprehensive regulation of crypto-asset service providers with multiple functions

- **expected / expectation**: Recommendation 9 says authorities should ensure that service providers and affiliates combining multiple functions and activities, where permissible, are covered by appropriate regulation, supervision and oversight that fully addresses both the risks of each individual function and the risks from combining them, including but not limited to conflict-of-interest requirements and, as appropriate, separation of certain functions, activities or incorporation. (p. 14)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "are subject to appropriate regulation, supervision and oversight that comprehensively address the risks associated with individual functions"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Some crypto-asset service providers, including groups of affiliated providers, may carry out many functions: facilitating transactions, settlement and clearing, non-custodial and custodial wallet provision (including selling software and hardware for non-custodial wallets), market-making, offering investment vehicles, lending and borrowing, and proprietary trading and issuance. Some jurisdictions do not permit certain combinations. (p. 14)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "non-custodial and custodial wallet provisioning (including the sale of software and hardware for non-custodial wallets), market-making"</sub>

## Recommendation 9: Comprehensive regulation of crypto-asset service providers with multiple functions

- **expected / expectation**: Where combinations of functions are permitted, relevant authorities should work to ensure these service providers, including groups of affiliated providers, are subject to robust and comprehensive regulation, supervision and oversight addressing risks from combining multiple activities and functions, in particular those under different sectoral regimes, with strong protection for investors and consumers. (p. 15)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Where combinations are permitted, relevant authorities should work to ensure that these service providers"</sub>
- **recommended / recommendation**: Authorities should consider requirements that address not only risks on a standalone basis, but also additional risks and additional conflicts of interest when functions and activities are conducted concurrently. (p. 15)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Authorities should consider requirements that address not only risks on a standalone basis"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Authorities should require that crypto-asset service providers, including groups of affiliated providers that where permissible combine multiple functions, have transparent organisational and managerial structures consistent with their overall strategy and risk profile and well understood by the board and senior management of the provider, affiliated providers, regulators and customers. (p. 15)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "have transparent organisational and managerial structures, which are consistent with their overall strategy and risk profile"</sub>
- **recommended / recommendation**: Authorities should consider whether and, if so, how combinations of multiple functions can be appropriately regulated within a single entity or group of affiliated entities. (p. 15)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Authorities should consider whether and, if so, how combinations of multiple functions can be appropriately regulated"</sub>
- **recommended / recommendation**: Authorities should consider relevant sectoral standards developed by SSBs when they require segregation of functions. (p. 15)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Authorities should consider relevant sectoral standards developed by SSBs when they require segregation of functions."</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Where combinations of multiple functions result from non-compliance with existing rules, or will create acute conflicts of interest that authorities determine cannot be managed effectively, authorities should apply robust measures as appropriate and in line with their jurisdiction's legal framework, including legal disaggregation and separation of certain functions. (p. 15)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "authorities should apply robust measures as appropriate and in line with jurisdictional legal frameworks, including legal"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Where multiple functions are permitted within one service provider or group of affiliated providers, authorities should require adequate policies and processes to identify, mitigate and manage actual or potential conflicts of interest. These should include preventing abuse from concentrated control, managing related-party transactions (especially those involving affiliated companies), and keeping related-party transactions transparent. (p. 15)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "management of related-party transactions, in particular those involving affiliated companies, and transparency of related-party transactions"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Authorities should give particular attention to multi-function service providers that facilitate custody, trading, settlement, lending and borrowing, or proprietary trading, and should use regulatory measures designed to segregate risks adequately; the report gives legal disaggregation and separation of certain functions as an example of what this may involve. (p. 15)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "should apply regulatory measures that are designed for the adequate segregation of risks"</sub>
- **expected / expectation**: Information sharing across borders and sectors about multi-function service providers that operate across borders and sectors is described as particularly important. Authorities should share information, as appropriate, so that financial distress at these intermediaries does not spill over to other jurisdictions or sectors of the financial system. (p. 15)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Authorities should share information, as appropriate, to minimize financial distress of these intermediaries from spilling over"</sub>
- **recommended / recommendation**: Authorities should consider additional prudential requirements if appropriate to address additional risks or conflicts of interest arising from the combination of functions. (p. 16)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Authorities should consider additional prudential requirements if appropriate to address additional risks or conflicts of interest from this setup."</sub>

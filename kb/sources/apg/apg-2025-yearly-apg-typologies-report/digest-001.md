## 1.1 Overview

## Sources of funds

- **descriptive / typology or red flag**: Cyber scam hubs draw illicit proceeds from four main sources: debt bondage of trafficking victims, ransom payments for release, revenue from fraud committed by victims, and recovery agents re-victimising scam victims. (p. 8)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "which draws illicit proceeds from four main sources"</sub>
- **descriptive / typology or red flag**: Perpetrators impose debt obligations on trafficked individuals for transport, accommodation and subsistence costs, which victims are coerced to repay over time. (p. 8)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "perpetrators impose debt obligations on trafficked individuals, covering costs such as transportation, accommodation and subsistence"</sub>

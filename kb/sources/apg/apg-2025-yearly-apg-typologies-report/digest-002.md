## Enabling conditions

- **descriptive / typology or red flag**: Cyber scam hub operations exploit vulnerabilities to establish, expand and diversify, including special economic zones in Southeast Asia that attract investment but are increasingly exploited, often with lower oversight and limited law enforcement presence. (p. 13)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Special economic zones (SEZs) in Southeast Asia, initially established to attract foreign investments and stimulate economic growth, are increasingly exploited"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: 71% of member responses said cyber scam hubs were typically located in urban centres and 53% said in border areas; these often coincide with special economic zones. (p. 13)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "members generally noted cyber scam hubs were typically located in urban centres (71% of member responses) or border areas (53% of member responses)"</sub>
- **descriptive / typology or red flag**: Criminal syndicates use corporate vehicles such as shell companies, trusts and complex entities for a façade of legitimacy; professional intermediaries such as lawyers, accountants, TCSPs and formation agents may knowingly or inadvertently facilitate them. ## Emerging Threats

- **descriptive / typology or red flag**: Criminal syndicates increasingly use AI to automate processes, expand reach and optimise victim targeting; members noted chatbots, deepfakes, voice-cloning and scam content generation. (p. 15)[^original]

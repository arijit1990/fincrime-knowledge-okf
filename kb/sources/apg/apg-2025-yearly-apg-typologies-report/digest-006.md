  <sub>anchor: "The case was indicted in December 2024, for offence of aggravated fraud committed jointly by three or more co-offenders and money laundering."</sub>

  <sub>anchor: "placed the rest of the money in the men’s restroom."</sub>

  <sub>anchor: "used his account and password to login to the police department online system"</sub>

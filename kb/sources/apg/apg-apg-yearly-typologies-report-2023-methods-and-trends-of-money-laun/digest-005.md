  <sub>anchor: "The FMU conducted a search of its internal database and also circulated the Computerized National Identification Numbers"</sub>

## Case study # 57 – Ponzi scheme using online platform

- **descriptive / case example**: Persons A and B led a Ponzi scheme via a web portal that promised 7% to 20% monthly returns and defrauded public members of PKR17 billion (USD94.4 million) between January 2019 and February 2021. (p. 44)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "cheating members of the public of PKR17 billion (USD94.4 million) between January 2019 and February 2021."</sub>
- **descriptive / typology or red flag**: Investment claims in property, crypto exchange, IT, transport and trading were not real; about 50% of new deposits were diverted to pay existing investors' interest, and agents were paid to recruit new investors. (p. 44)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "It is assessed that 50% of new deposits were diverted to pay current investor interest and agents who were employed to establish new investors."</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: The A&B Group comprised 26 companies registered in names of family members; SECP found they held no licence to solicit deposits from the public. (p. 45)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Investigation with SECP revealed that these companies held no legal license to solicit deposits from the public"</sub>
- **descriptive / data or feature**: Investigation linked 70 bank accounts across several commercial banks and over 50,000 transactions were analysed, with bank data obtained via the central bank. (p. 45)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "in excess of 50,000 transactions were analysed."</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: Proceeds were used to buy 31 immoveable and 30 moveable properties estimated at PKR 6.7 billion (USD 37.2 million). (p. 45)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the POC had been used to purchase 31 immoveable properties and 30 moveable properties with an estimated value of PKR 6.7 billion"</sub>
- **descriptive / method**: Investigative techniques included forensic accounting, digital forensics, social engineering of social media profiles, banking record analysis, interviews and international assistance; a cloud database of over 1,000,000 transactions was recovered by expert forensic investigators. (p. 45)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Investigation techniques utilized during the ML Investigation included forensic accounting, digital forensics"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: Assets worth PKR 3 billion in 41 accounts, PKR 2.5 billion in 17 properties (May–July 2021), PKR 700 million in 13 properties (January 2022) and PKR 500 million in 30 vehicles (March 2022) were frozen under S12 of NAO 1999. (p. 45)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "41x accounts with deposit of PKR. 3 billion (16.6 million USD) were frozen under S12 of National Accountability Ordinance"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: SECP adjudicated the Group and its sponsors, disqualified them from directorships for five years, imposed a PKR 100 million penalty on each sponsor, and barred them from incorporating new companies. (p. 46)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "has disqualified the sponsors of the Group from becoming directors of any company for a period of 5 years"</sub>

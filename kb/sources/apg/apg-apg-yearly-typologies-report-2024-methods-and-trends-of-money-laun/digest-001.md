## 1.1 Overview

## Australia

- **descriptive / finding or result**: Australia's NRA found that legal persons and arrangements and associated banking are persistently exploited to store and move large volumes of criminal proceeds, including offshore, and can be set up easily to mask beneficial ownership. (p. 9)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Legal persons (and legal arrangements) and associated banking arrangements are persistently exploited by criminals"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: Australia lacks comprehensive mechanisms for systematic collection, verification and release of beneficial ownership information, restricting agencies' ability to detect laundering through legal structures; restraining and confiscating assets held by companies or trusts is challenging and resource intensive.   <sub>anchor: "Australia does not have comprehensive mechanisms for the systematic collection, verification and release of beneficial ownership information."</sub>

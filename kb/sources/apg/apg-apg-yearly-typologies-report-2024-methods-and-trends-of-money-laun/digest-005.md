## A multi-jurisdictional defence

- **descriptive / typology or red flag**: Criminal networks use 'multi-jurisdictional splitting', dispersing company formation, asset ownership, intermediaries and bank accounts across jurisdictions, making it very hard for any single authority to see the full picture. (p. 36)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "where they strategically disperse company formation, asset ownership, intermediaries, and bank accounts"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: Insufficient enforcement against ML offences in some jurisdictions, due to capacity constraints or policy choices, hinders assessment of the problem's extent and identification of emerging trends. (p. 36)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "some jurisdictions struggle with insufficient enforcement against ML offences due to capacity constraints"</sub>

## Liability of Legal Persons: Implementation under the United Nations Convention against Corruption with a focus on Malaysia

- **mandatory / requirement**: UNCAC Article 26 requires States parties to adopt measures establishing the liability of legal persons for participation in offences criminalised under the Convention. (p. 142)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Article 26 of the UNCAC requires States parties to adopt measures to establish the liability of legal persons"</sub>
- **descriptive / context**: Legal persons can be held accountable for corrupt acts; this is important because serious crime can be committed through companies or charitable organisations, and individuals within entities may be hard to identify due to complex structures. (p. 142)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Providing for such liability is important as serious and sophisticated crime can be committed by"</sub>

## Factors Influencing the Choice of Technique to Launder Funds: The APPT Framework

- **descriptive / finding or result**: The APPT study finds that criminal and non-criminal actors both matter, that more complex predicate crimes lead to more sophisticated laundering, that the intended purpose of funds shapes the approach, and that technology requires AML frameworks to adapt continually.

- **descriptive / case example**: The Zamtrop conspiracy involved a substantial portion of US$52 million of Zambian Ministry of Finance funds placed in the Zamtrop account at Zambia National Commercial Bank in the UK, opened in December 1995 by Xavier Franklin Chungu, head of ZSIS, who was the sole signatory at various times. ## Misconduct by DNFBP Intermediaries

- **descriptive / typology or red flag**: Two English solicitor firms, Meer Care & Desai (MCD) and Cave Malik & Co. (CM), and CM's Zambian offshoot, participated in creating and operating CVs and bank accounts for the benefit of both conspiracies. (p. 203)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Figuring prominently in both the Zamtrop and BK conspiracies were two fi rms of Eng- lish solicitors"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: Partner Iqbal Meer of MCD agreed with Chungu to act for AFSL in receiving and disbursing Republic funds for official ZSIS business, effectively washing illicit government money through client accounts, though he and MCD made little or no money from it. (p. 203)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "MCD, through Meer, eff ectively washed the illicit government money through their client accounts"</sub>
- **descriptive / typology or red flag**: Chungu selected Meer for this role because of his perceived susceptibility to the benefit of being associated with politically powerful people. (p. 203)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Chungu singled out Meer for this role because of his perceived susceptibility"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: Meer claimed a higher professional ethical standard and a character reference from Nelson Mandela, another client; the court found his professional responsibilities and international savvy should have prevented the dubious transactions. (p. 204)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the court found that Meer's professional responsibilities, as well as his international savvy"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: MCD and CM were found liable for conspiracy and dishonest assistance with judgments of several million US dollars each, though the ruling against MCD was later overturned because the judge had made an inappropriate leap between negligence and dishonest assistance. (p. 204)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the ruling against MCD were later overturned on the grounds that the judge had made an inappropriate leap"</sub>

## Distance between the Primary Conspirators and the Corporate Vehicles

- **descriptive / typology or red flag**: The primary conspirators tried to keep as much distance as possible from the illegal activity by inserting friends and associates between themselves and the transactions, and used prestige and ZSIS secrecy to get others to help without asking many questions. ## Misuse of a Publicly Tradable Entity

- **descriptive / typology or red flag**: Misuse of publicly held entities is rare because they are usually subject to disclosure rules; here a bearer share holder gained control of a publicly held Belgian entity, which converted misappropriated funds into European real estate. (p. 205)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "a person engaging in grand corruption, by holding a bearer share in his hand, was able to acquire control"</sub>

## Investigation and Asset Recovery

  <sub>anchor: "Zambia initiated criminal proceedings against Chiluba, Kabwe, and former AFSL executive director"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: The UK civil trial opened 31 October 2006 with final judgment on 5 April 2007; all defendants in the Zamtrop and BK conspiracies were found liable for roughly US$25 million and US$20 million respectively, with damages for fiduciary breaches and dishonest assistance. (p. 205)[^original]

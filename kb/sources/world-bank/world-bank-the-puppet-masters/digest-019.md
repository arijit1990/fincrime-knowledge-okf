## 1.2 Methodology

- **descriptive / method**: Cases had to involve misuse of at least one corporate vehicle used at least in part for the anonymity it offered its beneficial owners, not merely for tax or asset-protection features. (p. 135)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "for which a case could be made that it was used, at least in part, for the anonymity it off ered"</sub>
- **descriptive / method**: Cases had to involve a high-level public official or politically exposed person able to wield significant influence over the scheme; the database covers 30 years back to 1980. (p. 135)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "politically exposed person or other party who was able to wield signifi cant infl uence"</sub>
- **descriptive / method**: The scheme had to involve the equivalent of more than US$1 million at the time, with a few exceptions for particularly expansive or innovative schemes or sums with disproportionately greater local purchasing power. (p. 136)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "equivalent of more than US$1 million (at the time of the scheme)"</sub>
- **descriptive / method**: Information was first gathered from public secondary sources, then court and government documents were sought to supplement and confirm it, with substantial outreach to prosecutors, attorneys, World Bank country offices and anticorruption agencies. (p. 136)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Subsequently, court documents and other government-sanctioned documents"</sub>
- **descriptive / limitation**: Access to documents was limited in many cases because some lower court decisions were unpublished, courts did not routinely publish decisions, some access was politically constrained, and ongoing or closed proceedings withheld documents. (p. 136)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "In a signifi cant number of instances, the information-gathering process revealed that access to documents"</sub>
- **descriptive / limitation**: Most cases are documented in English, with a substantial proportion of U.S. and U.K. documents, a bias partly attributed to the many legal actions against grand corruption in those jurisdictions. (p. 136)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "It is arguable that the U.S. and U.K. bias originates in part from the signifi cant number"</sub>
- **descriptive / method**: Where the most precise data showed a range of suspected corrupt proceeds, the lower end of the spectrum was used; proceeds cover misappropriated public assets and private funds received in breach of officials' fiduciary duties. (p. 136)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "where the most precise data available involved a range of suspected corrupt proceeds, we deferred to the lower end"</sub>
- **descriptive / limitation**: Because suspicion and trials take years, the documentary trail often refers to conduct predating the documents, so a database relying only on official documentation reflects an earlier reality. (p. 137)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "a database that relies solely on offi cial documentation is bound to be a refl ection of a much earlier reality"</sub>
- **descriptive / method**: Cases with judicial confirmation of corporate vehicle misuse were included even without conviction; cases where final decisions cleared officials of all wrongdoing were excluded. (p. 137)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "In cases in which fi nal court decisions (not contradicted by legal actions in other jurisdictions) cleared"</sub>
- **descriptive / limitation**: Jurisdiction of some corporate vehicles and bank accounts could not be determined, mostly due to unspecific source documents about companies and trusts; no assumptions were made. (p. 137)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "It was not possible to ascertain the jurisdiction of all of corporate vehicles and bank accounts used."</sub>
- **descriptive / limitation**: Cases lacking evidence of corporate vehicle misuse were excluded, so some well-known grand corruption cases are absent; the 150 cases were not random, a deliberate choice to seek cases from all regions. (p. 137)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Cases for which evidence of corporate vehicle misuse was not available were excluded"</sub>

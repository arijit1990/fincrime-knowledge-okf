## Case Study 6: Joseph Estrada

- **descriptive / context**: Joseph Estrada was President of the Philippines from June 1998 to January 2001, stepped down during his Senate impeachment trial, was arrested in April 2001 and charged under the Anti-Plunder Law for allegedly amassing more than US$87 million in unexplained wealth. (p. 211)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "He was arrested in April 2001 and charged with violating the Anti-Plunder Law"</sub>
- **descriptive / requirement**: Under the Anti-Plunder Law a conviction requires three elements: the offender is a public official acting alone or in conspiracy, amassed ill-gotten wealth through a series of criminal acts, and the aggregate amount is at least US$1,065,500 (PHP 50 million). (p. 211)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the aggregate amount of the ill-gotten wealth is at least US$1,065,500 (PHP 50 million)"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: On September 12, 2007 the Sandiganbayan convicted Estrada of plunder, finding he collected US$11.6 million in jueteng kickbacks, of which US$4.26 million was concealed in Erap Muslim Youth Foundation accounts, and received US$4 million commission from Belle Corporation share purchases held under the name Jose Velarde. (p. 212)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "had collected US$11.6 million in kick- backs from illegal jueteng gambling operators"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: The Sandiganbayan ordered forfeiture of Estrada's illegally acquired assets from the jueteng collections and the Belle Corporation commissions. (p. 212)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "the Sandiganbayan ordered the forfeiture of Estrada's illegally acquired assets"</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: Estrada was tried and acquitted of a perjury charge over his allegedly false asset filings by a different Sandiganbayan court, and the court did not convict him of the tobacco excise tax misappropriation charge. (p. 212)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "he was tried and acquitted of this charge by a diff erent Sandiganbayan court"</sub>
- **descriptive / typology or red flag**: Two noteworthy issues in the Estrada case were the use of a foundation to conceal illicit proceeds and the involvement of many individuals acting in various capacities to help carry out the schemes. (p. 213)[^original]

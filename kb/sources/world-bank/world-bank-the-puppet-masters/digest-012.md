## Part 3. Where Does the Beneficial Owner Hide?

- **descriptive / typology or red flag**: Unlike a hired nominee, a front man is specifically selected, is more likely to be connected to the principal through biographical data than a contractual paper trail, and usually purports to be the beneficial owner of the corporate vehicle until legal proceedings are brought. (p. 80)[^original]

  <sub>anchor: "Hired nominees acting in a professional capacity may be selected at ran- dom, based on cost and the level of secrecy off ered."</sub>
- **descriptive / finding or result**: Absent exculpatory evidence, front men face all the risks and liabilities of being the true end-user parties in relation to a corporate vehicle, even when acting for another person. (p. 80)[^original]

  <sub>anchor: "Nearly half of the grand corruption investigations reviewed for this study involved the use of these informal front men."</sub>

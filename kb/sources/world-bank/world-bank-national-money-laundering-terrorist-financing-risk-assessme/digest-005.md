## 4.1. Output 1: Risk Overview

  <sub>anchor: "This part of the Risk Overview summarizes the ML/TF threat ratings faced by the jurisdiction."</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: Inherent vulnerability is the vulnerability without accounting for any mitigation measures; national vulnerability scores are generated from the 'National Inherent Vulnerability' tab. (p. 64)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Inherent vulnerability is the vulnerability without accounting for any mitigation measures."</sub>
- **descriptive / case example**: In the hypothetical example, foreign VASPs represent a greater vulnerability (medium) than domestic VASPs (low), while the jurisdiction's threats are higher for ML than TF and mostly incoming or internal. (p. 64)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "foreign VASPs represent a greater vulnerability (with a medium vulnerability) than domestic VASPs"</sub>
- **descriptive / definition**: Inherent risk is the overall risk level before mitigation measures are considered and combines threats and inherent vulnerabilities for ML and TF. (p. 65)[^original]  
  <sub>anchor: "Inherent risk is the overall risk level before mitigation measures have been considered."</sub>

  <sub>anchor: "The output breaks down the technical compliance and the effectiveness separately."</sub>

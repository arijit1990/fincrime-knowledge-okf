# How to use this knowledge bundle

The full instructions are in the repository's [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md). This page is the short version and
travels with the bundle when it is copied on its own.

## Find

1. Exact terms first: search `sources/` for provision numbers, document names, defined terms and typologies.
   Try spelling variants (programme/program, organisation/organization).
2. Otherwise, start from the [root index](index.md), go to a [topic hub](topics/) or [jurisdiction hub](jurisdictions/),
   then a source's `overview.md`, then the part listed in its Contents table.
3. Read whole parts. Open the previous or next part when the passage continues, or when definitions,
   scope or exceptions may sit elsewhere.

## Weigh

- Jurisdiction match first. International standards bind states and supervisors, not firms directly.
- Normative force: legal requirement > regulatory expectation > supervisory expectation > international standard >
  empirical observation > industry practice > academic finding > expert interpretation > hypothesis > vendor view.
  See [normative force](taxonomy/normative-force.md).
- Currency: check `legal_status` and the [status and supersession register](registers/status-and-supersession.md).
  Never present superseded documents or consultations as current, final rules.

## Cite

- Cite the part file and page marker (`[p. N]`) you read, plus the original URL (`resource`).
- `text-NNN.md` is verbatim and may be quoted. `digest-NNN.md` is a verified paraphrase: do not quote it as the
  source's own words.
- Check [known gaps](registers/known-gaps.md) when a source has text-quality warnings.

## Combine with your own knowledge

The bundle (snapshot 2026-11-16) outranks built-in knowledge for anything it covers. If you believe something has
changed since then, say so and recommend verification. If the bundle is silent, say so, and label anything
answered from general knowledge. Internal firm policy governs firm procedure: flag differences rather than
overriding it.

# Normative force

Every source carries one `normative_force`. Listed from most to least authoritative for a regulated firm.

- **legal_requirement**: Binding law or rule
- **regulatory_expectation**: Official regulator guidance stating expectations (not itself law)
- **supervisory_expectation**: Supervisory statements, letters, findings, enforcement outcomes
- **international_standard**: Standard-setter output (e.g. FATF, BCBS); binding on states/supervisors via adoption, not directly on firms
- **empirical_observation**: Observed data, typology/case evidence, statistics
- **industry_practice**: Industry body or firm practice
- **academic_finding**: Peer-reviewed or preprint research finding
- **expert_interpretation**: Commentary or interpretation by experts
- **hypothesis**: Proposed, consultative or speculative material
- **vendor_recommendation**: Vendor/consultant view; not independently validated
